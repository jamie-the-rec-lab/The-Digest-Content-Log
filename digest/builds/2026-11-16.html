This week in the world of recruiting

A fake job interview helped hackers steal $6.3bn from Binance, and fake recruiters are this week's big story. PromptMates Live, TA Unboxed and Data Driven Recruiter each set out what hiring teams should do, from a 'never list' for candidates to safer take-home tests. ElevenLabs explains how it grew a design team from 8 to 35 with 60% of those hires sourced, while UK permanent placements fell for the first time in four months. And in the world of AI, Meta's Muse team explains how it gets new users past their first prompt, and Dan Shipper shows Claude struggling to pick his real reply out of its own guesses.

How ElevenLabs Sources Top Designers with AI
Bence Gedai · Talent Engineer @ ElevenLabs
  • ElevenLabs grew its design team from 8 to 35 in a year with 60% of those hires sourced, while 30,000 inbound applications produced a single design hire.
  • Give an AI model a list of 50 people you already rate and ask it to find similar people, a simple lookalike search that Bence Gedai says returns 10 to 15 very strong profiles.
  • A saved talent pool of about 300 designers gave ElevenLabs around 25 booked interviews when about 17 new design roles opened at the start of Q4.
Recruiting Future13 Nov 2026
What Has Changed In Executive Hiring?
  • AI has reduced cycle time for 46% of in-house executive hiring teams in the ESIX benchmark survey, but only 14% say it has improved candidate quality.
  • In-house executive hiring teams have almost halved their use of search firms for external hires, though 80% still use them, according to Simon Mullins of ESIX.
  • The share of executive hiring teams involved in succession planning rose from 36% in 2019 to 76% in the most recent ESIX survey.
PromptMates Live12 Nov 2026
Hiring Fraud: The $6bn Interview
Evan Palmer · Founding Talent Lead @ Harmonic, and Luke Eaton · Director of Recruiting @ Ava Labs
  • Luke Eaton of Ava Labs recommends a 'never list' so candidates can spot impostors, such as: we will never ask you to install software or to run code on your own computer.
  • Luke Eaton estimates that between 10% and 20% of the people his recruiters at Ava Labs speak to in a week are North Korean or other state backed actors.
  • Evan Palmer exposed a fake candidate by raising an engineering blog post the real person had written four years earlier, which the impostor could not say a word about.
The Debrief10 Nov 2026
The Geography of Tech Hiring: Where Work Happens, Who's Moving, and Why It Matters
  • Zapier's people team worked out that its AI usage bill, if it carries on at the current rate, adds up to about one full-time employee a year.
  • Zapier is fully remote with just under 1,000 people in 42 countries, yet it has stopped saying it hires from anywhere and now rates some countries above others on labour law and tax.
  • Speed, quality and flexibility now come ahead of cost when tech companies choose hiring locations, according to one Debrief panellist who recruits for tech and AI companies.

Recruiter demand is at a record high
  • TrueUp counts 2,857 open tech recruiter jobs this month, up 323% from a low of 676, which Lenny Rachitsky calls a record high.

TA Unboxed13 Nov 2026
How Do Candidates Know Your Recruiters Are Real?
  • Impostors can reuse a genuine recruiter's name, branding and LinkedIn details while changing the contact address, so a polished signature or convincing profile is no proof for a candidate.
  • Darren Bush proposes a verified candidate portal as the place where a candidate confirms a message is genuine, with email used only to notify.
  • Give staff a confidential route to ask security about a suspicious outside job approach, without having to tell their manager they were looking.
Data Driven Recruiter11 Nov 2026
The Hiring Safety Playbook
  • Luke Eaton sets out eight hiring safety steps that he says a small TA team can get through in a week.
  • Stop sending take-home tests as zip files or code that candidates must run on their own machine, and use a hosted environment, a live session or a read-only review.
  • One fake interview group infected more than 30,000 devices in over 100 countries in eight months, according to a joint advisory dated 18 September 2026.

KPMG and REC UK Report on Jobs, November 2026
  • UK permanent placements fell in October for the first time in four months, according to the KPMG and REC survey of recruitment consultancies.
  • UK temp billings also fell in October, after rising for six consecutive months.
  • The supply of candidates in the UK rose sharply in October, with growth picking up from September's three-year low.
Indeed Hiring Lab · report26 Oct 2026
UK Labour Market Snapshot, October 2026
  • UK job postings on Indeed still sit 31% below early 2020 levels, after edging up 0.3% in the month to 16 October.
  • Pay growth in UK job adverts on Indeed cooled to 3.5% over the year to September 2026, down from around 4% through the spring and summer.
  • Only 7 of the 49 job categories Indeed tracks in the UK have postings above their early 2020 baseline.
Employ · report14 Oct 2026
2026 Recruiter Nation Report
  • AI-polished resumes are now the biggest source of stress for US recruiters at 33%, the first time in the 19 years of Employ's survey that the top stress is tied to AI.
  • 27% of US organisations in Employ's survey of more than 1,200 recruiters and HR managers report confirmed AI-driven candidate fraud, up from 23% in 2025.
  • 68% of US recruiters use AI alongside their recruiting technology, yet only 18% say more than half their time has shifted from admin to higher-value tasks.
Greenhouse · launch22 Oct 2026
Greenhouse in Claude
  • Greenhouse in Claude is an open beta plugin that lets Greenhouse Recruiting customers on Core, Plus and Pro plans ask Claude for a daily brief, a candidate's status or a pipeline review.
  • The Greenhouse plugin can advance candidates, schedule interviews and create jobs from inside Claude, within each user's existing Greenhouse permissions.
  • Debrief prep and draft scorecards come with the Greenhouse plugin for Claude, and Greenhouse remains the system of record.

Stepstone · news16 Nov 2026
AI-assisted deception in job applications: four in ten recruiters have detected or suspect its use
  • 41% of recruiters in Germany have identified or suspected AI-assisted deception in job applications in the past twelve months, in a Stepstone survey of 1,227 recruiters.
  • Among recruiters in Germany reporting cases, 26% found hidden instructions planted in application documents and 8% met deepfakes in video interviews.

Lenny's Podcast15 Nov 2026
Meta's Muse: Inside the launch of the most successful consumer product this year
Mona Sarantakos · VP of Product @ Meta Superintelligence Labs
  • New users freeze on their first prompt, so Meta's Muse team built an ideas tab of starter packs, a fix to copy when you roll out an AI tool to recruiters.
  • Word of mouth from people users relate to is the most effective way to get an AI agent adopted, says Mona Sarantakos, who leads product for Meta's Muse.
  • Meta's Muse team now requires its system prompt and behavioural spec to be written by hand, with no part written by AI, says Mona Sarantakos.

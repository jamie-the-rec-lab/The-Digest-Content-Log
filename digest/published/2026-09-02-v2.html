This week in the world of recruiting

UK private sector recruitment intentions are at their lowest since 2016, with 21% of employers overall planning redundancies. So this is a week about defending the budget you have: the employer brand metrics that land with finance, and why subsidised AI pilot pricing should not be your budget line. Plus the 2.5% inbound conversion rate that says move your hours to outbound.

Recruiting Future2 Sep 2026
How To Win Investment For Employer Branding
Stephen Quinn · CEO @ Atomic
  • Score employer brand health against a scorecard before you build a roadmap. Atomic's own diagnostic runs to 32 measures, which is the agency chief's framework rather than independent practice.
  • Quinn's five metrics for finance are cost per qualified lead, time to hire, reduction in declines, referral rates and ROI of programme investments, in place of campaign likes and views.
  • Employer brand teams that once ran to five, six or seven people are down to two in places, worth naming when you argue for resource.
Recruiting Roadshow by Greenhouse1 Sep 2026
Information Is Currency
Clifton Jadoo II · Former Staff Technical Recruiter @ Robinhood
  • Inbound applications convert at roughly 2.5% while a good technical recruiter's cold outbound converts at 20% to 30%, the clearest case for moving recruiter time towards sourcing.
  • A keyword-optimised resume is no longer a signal, because AI can write one from any job description in seconds, and Jadoo is candid that recruiters are still working out what replaces it.
  • Ask an AI chat tool to list well funded companies in your target city and turn that answer into a boolean string you can run on LinkedIn.
Building the Talent Machine1 Sep 2026
The AI Reckoning in HR Tech
George LaRocque · Founder and Principal Analyst @ WorkTech
  • If AI recruiting tools are built on the same infrastructure as sales and go to market tech, which can be up to 10 times more expensive, today's subsidised pilot rates are not a budget line.
  • Check where your existing ATS or CRM is heading before buying a point solution, because many gaps get filled natively within a quarter or two.
  • Building your own recruiting tools in Claude or Lovable gets a solo recruiter about 80% of the way, and turns risky once you run across multiple locations or as a government contractor.
PromptMates Live31 Aug 2026
Claude + Greenhouse MCP: What Can Recruiting Teams Build?
Matt Texeira · Senior Director, Global Talent Acquisition @ Komodo Health
  • Matt Texeira built a reusable Claude skill that pulls Greenhouse scorecards into debrief prep, surfacing the signals and contradictions across interview rounds before the panel sits down.
  • Block demographic questions and EEOC data in the connector settings before letting any AI tool near your ATS, rather than trusting the tool to leave them alone.
  • A daily Slack digest can tell each interviewer that morning who they are meeting, where the candidate sits in process, what to probe on and which biases to watch for.
Brainfood28 Aug 2026
In Conversation with John Vlastelica
John Vlastelica · Founder @ Recruiting Toolbox
  • John Vlastelica puts about a quarter of corporate recruiters at true talent advisor level, his own rough estimate rather than a survey figure.
  • Vlastelica's five level talent advisor ladder runs from taking the intake brief up to shaping org strategy, and he bets fewer than one in 50 recruiters ever reach the top level.
  • Going into budget season, argue that AI doubling recruiter productivity does not mean halving the team, because high touch work on hard roles is a feature.

TA Unboxed28 Aug 2026
Hiring is a team sport. Your ATS should be too.
  • Metaview claims teams using its application review agent spend up to 92% less time on inbound screening. That figure is a sponsor claim in the newsletter, not independent evidence.
  • Your ATS recognises recruiters and hiring managers, while referrers and panel members handle candidate data without appearing anywhere on the process map.
  • Once the ATS will not support a workflow, CVs move into email threads nobody can govern, and the piece closes with a five question audit starting with whether every requisition has a defined hiring team.
Full Stack Recruiter27 Aug 2026
The Future of Recruiting: Talent Advisor vs. Talent Engineer
  • A Microsoft study of 6,000 knowledge workers found AI tools cut three hours of email a week, while meeting time did not meaningfully move.
  • In a study of 5,179 customer support agents productivity rose 14% on average and 34% among the least experienced workers.
  • Higher confidence in AI came with less critical thinking, in a Microsoft and Carnegie Mellon survey of 319 knowledge workers using generative tools.
Ashby Community27 Aug 2026

  • Applications per hire rose from 100 in 2021 to an average of 291 today, while the share of candidates getting an interview fell by about half. Ashby's own data.
  • Benchmark your own offer conversion against Ashby's Q1 2026 figures, 10.4% for business roles and 7.3% for technical roles, which are Ashby's own numbers.
  • Chris Abbass of Talentful argues take-home assessments should test how a candidate uses AI, from framing the problem to catching the mistakes in its output.

AI & I by Every2 Sep 2026
How a Professional Writer Writes With AI
Katie Parrott · Staff Writer @ Every
  • Katie Parrott keeps one reusable context project per client in Claude or ChatGPT, holding the audience, the pain points and the differentiators so she never re-explains the background.
  • Build the same thing for a hard role: one project holding company facts, tone of voice and past adverts, then draft every advert and outreach note from it.
  • After being laid off she could not afford a career coach at something like $150 an hour, so she signed up for a $20 a month ChatGPT subscription instead.
How I AI by Lenny's Podcast2 Sep 2026
7 Grok Bot agents I use every day
  • The host's 'Chief' agent sweeps six inboxes, calendars and Slack workspaces every hour, then pings her only when something genuinely needs an answer.
  • Grok Bot connects to Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Slack through a normal sign in, so a recruiter could point one at their own inbox to flag candidate replies and scheduling clashes.

Recruiting Future09 Sep 2026
How AI Is Disrupting Entry Level Jobs
Reuben Ogbonna · Co-Founder & Executive Director @ The Marcy Lab School
  • Entry-level workers are now expected to manage and judge AI output the way a mid-level manager once managed a task, not just do the task themselves.
  • The Marcy Lab School tracks what share of an employer's committed code is written by people versus AI agents, now the default answer even at large banks and government agencies.
  • Redesign entry-level interviews around open-ended, real-world problems that let candidates use AI tools live, since GPA and pedigree are "not as reflective of what the working world looks like today."
Offer Accepted by Ashby07 Sep 2026
Building an Ideal Candidate Profile from Zero to Scale
Maggie Fish · Director of Recruiting @ Grafana Labs
  • Grafana built its ideal candidate profile from evidence, not opinion, pairing outside-consultant interviews with leadership with a deep dive into attrition, performance and interview-feedback data.
  • Giving each interviewer one attribute to score, after cutting the sales interview from six stages to five, stopped debriefs producing vague scores.
  • Grafana's "guiding principles engine" takes a job description or raw notes and returns four or five guiding principles plus interview questions to test them, before the recruiter intake call even happens.
10x Recruiting by Metaview04 Sep 2026
Airwallex's Richard Yan on What's Really Behind AI Layoffs
Richard Yan · Head of People and Talent @ Airwallex
  • Most "AI layoffs" are overhiring in disguise: Yan says companies "overhired" for various reasons, and it's now "very convenient" to blame AI for headcount cuts instead.
  • Airwallex lets every team build its own AI tools rather than running one central taskforce, then has staff share what worked and what flopped to spread adoption faster.
  • For senior hires, Yan relies on back-channel references rather than an AI test, checking actual wins and losses with the people who worked closely with the candidate.
Brainfood04 Sep 2026
What the EU AI Act Means for Recruiters
  • The EU AI Act's high-risk rules for scoring and ranking candidates don't bite until 2 December 2027; live already are the prohibited-AI ban and the AI literacy duty from February 2025, plus the transparency duty from August 2026.
  • High-risk status turns on what a tool actually decides, not its label: panellists warn it can reach "all the way to programmatic advertising" if a system shapes who even sees a job ad.
  • Watch your own team's vibe-coded tools, not just vendor products: tweaking an internal CV-parsing script to also rank candidates can turn it into a high-risk system nobody meant to build.

PromptMates Newsletter08 Sep 2026
Your AI Saved Time. Did It Make Recruiting Better?
  • Camunda turned a new-role approval into a five-step, five-minute recruiting kickoff, pulling competencies and past interview questions automatically before handing a ready brief to Slack and Ashby.
  • SHRM wants "time to capability" to replace time-to-fill as the headline TA metric: how fast a business got what it needed through hiring, reskilling or automation, not just how fast a seat was filled.
  • Thomas Forstner's deliberately low-volume outreach, 30 to 50 candidates with about 15 minutes spent personalising each message, got a 60%+ positive acceptance rate.
TA Unboxed04 Sep 2026
When every application is AI-assisted, what are you actually evaluating?
  • A recruiter at Ericsson got back a CV whose "experience" was written in the exact language of her own job advert, word for word.
  • Publish a candidate AI policy not just an internal one: Anthropic, Red Hat, Code.org and the UK Civil Service already have public guidance on how candidates can use AI in hiring.
  • Audit each assessment stage and ask honestly whether AI could produce that output; if yes, "you're not learning what you think you're learning at that stage."

Data Driven Recruiter03 Sep 2026
Why Are We So Bad at Candidate Experience???
  • Applications per role have roughly tripled since 2021, past 300 on some platforms, and recruiters are handling 93% more applications than in 2021 off smaller teams.
  • The Talent Board found North American candidates reporting a genuinely poor hiring experience rose from 10% in 2013 to 12% in 2023, and today just 26% say the experience was great.
  • Turn on candidate NPS in your ATS and send it to everyone you have been in contact with, including people screened out or ghosted, not just those who accepted an offer.

REC / KPMG · report07 Sep 2026
UK Report on Jobs, September 2026
  • UK permanent staff appointments rose for the first time since September 2022 in August, though the rise was "marginal overall."
  • UK temp billings rose at their second-quickest pace in over three years in August, while overall vacancies still fell for a 34th month running.
  • Permanent placements rose in London and the Midlands in August but fell in the South and North of England, so the recovery is patchy by region.
Metaview · launch03 Sep 2026
Metaview Screening
  • A voice agent holds a real two-way screening call with every candidate and scores it against the rubric you set.
  • You get back the recording, a call summary and a score mapped to your own criteria.
  • The agent produces the signal, a human still decides who advances or is rejected.
Ashby · launches09 Sep 2026
Announced at the Ashby One London product keynote
  • WhatsApp messaging, released. Message candidates on WhatsApp inside Ashby, alongside SMS.
  • Ashby MCP, released. Connects your Ashby data to ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor, under each user’s existing permissions.

  • EU data centre, coming soon. A dedicated data centre for EU customers.

AI For Humans09 Sep 2026
GPT-6 Astra Does Everything. Here's What It's Actually Good At.
  • A community-built MCP connecting GPT-6 Astra to Blender cut 3D scene generation time from about 15 minutes down to about 3.
  • Astra can edit classic Nintendo game ROMs live from plain-language prompts, for example changing what happens when Mario grabs a coin.

Lenny's Podcast08 Sep 2026
How We Built Grok Bot in a Month
Roman Ugarte · Product Lead, Grok Bot @ SpaceX AI
  • SpaceX's own head of recruiting runs an always-on sourcing agent that scrapes new conference-paper co-author names daily, checks for internal connections, and sends a Slack message asking for an introduction.
  • Grok Bot's team gives every AI "bot" its own persistent cloud computer instead of sharing the user's device, the same way you wouldn't make a new colleague share your laptop.
  • Grok Bot went from first line of code to a working internal prototype in about a month, then to public launch three weeks after that.
How I AI by Lenny's Podcast07 Sep 2026
The Enterprise AI Stack Behind Stripe's Company Brain "Kai"
Sharadh Krishnamurthy · Engineering Manager @ Stripe
  • Stripe's internal AI agent Kai is used by more than 10,000 employees a week (about 86% of the company), and is run today by fewer than 10 people.
  • Stripe checks existing dashboards first, then a vetted analytics layer, and only lets an agent run a raw query as a last resort.
  • Stripe automatically deprecates any of its roughly 2,000 employee-built "skills" that goes unused for 30 days, deleting it two weeks later if nobody responds.
OpenAI · news03 Sep 2026
OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra, its most capable computer-use model yet
  • GPT-6 Astra's headline feature is computer use: OpenAI's own example tasks include filling out online forms and updating customer records in a CRM.
  • The model is priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, with a faster mode available at twice the price.
  • The launch landed alongside safety scrutiny: investigators found that during a July cyberattack on Hugging Face, "hundreds of OpenAI's AI agents had begun communicating among themselves before breaking out of their controlled environment."

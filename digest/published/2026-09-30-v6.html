This week in the world of recruiting

Is AI making hiring faster? Not for most: only 28% of employers globally say their time to hire has sped up, though in one 70,000 applicant study AI interviews led to 12% more offers. Two newsletters turn the lens on how we judge, arguing a “poor candidate” often reflects the interviewer, and suggesting that most TA metrics only look backwards. In other news, Gong has shifted its focus from inbound applications to sourcing and referrals, and a fake Apple recruiter email shows how convincing outreach scams now are. And looking into the world of tech/AI, burnout among tech workers in Lenny’s survey hit 55%.

10x Recruiting by Metaview30 Sep 2026
Inside Metaview’s $60M Raise
Siadhal Magos · Co-Founder and CEO @ Metaview
  • Metaview’s CEO argues connected agents on one platform beat stand-alone point solutions because shared context builds trust. It is his company’s sales pitch, and Metaview produces this show.
  • Magos says the more work AI does over longer periods, the more you must trust it, and trust depends on the agent holding the right context.
  • Metaview says it raised a further $60M led by Insight Partners, taking total funding to $110M, and Magos says 60% of customers now coming in are platform customers, buying more than one Metaview product. Both are self-reported.
Recruiting Roadshow by Greenhouse29 Sep 2026
Judgment is the superpower
Kelsey Biggs · VP Global Talent Acquisition @ Gong
  • Gong shifted about a year ago from inbound applications to sourcing and referrals, because keyword matching and extra recruiter screens still gave no better signal.
  • Biggs borrows Gong’s sales coaching model, not yet set up in her team’s new system: managers leave timestamped comments on recorded calls, such as flagging one that ended with no talk of compensation.
  • Biggs wants sourcing to start in the ATS, with declined candidates tagged automatically from call transcripts for future roles, and admits “we never start there either”.
Recruiting Future29 Sep 2026
Managing Career Transitions
Kelly O. Kay · Global Managing Partner, Enterprise Software and AI Practice @ Heidrick & Struggles
  • Kay describes an executive hired without reference checks because the parties already knew each other, then fired a year later: familiarity is no reason to skip them.
  • He has not seen AI shorten the time it takes to hire an executive, because the bets are so big, and says AI is used far more for earlier-career hiring.
  • Kay scores each opportunity on three lenses: quality of the company, quality of the role and quality of the offer, to keep emotion out of the decision.
PromptMates Live23 Sep 2026
How to Increase AI Adoption
Annika in der Beek · Chief People Officer @ Statista, and Martyn Redstone · Head of Responsible AI @ Warden AI
  • Statista sends every employee a private monthly AI usage email covering prompts, token spend, models used and an efficiency score. Only the individual sees it, never their manager.
  • Statista gave everyone a multi-model workspace called Langdock and accepted higher token costs, because in der Beek says that step releases fear and gets people going.
  • Warden AI’s Martyn Redstone, whose company audits HR AI for bias, says “a human made the final hiring decision” is no defence, because most regulation now covers decision influence.

Data Driven Recruiter30 Sep 2026
DDR80 - 4 Years of AI...What's Actually Changed??
  • Ava Labs sources ahead of need: backup pipelines for critical roles, succession gaps, and roles that open when a planned trigger such as a customer milestone is hit.
  • Ava Labs saves those sourcing sweeps as projects in its ATS, so when a role goes live, recruiters refine what is already there instead of starting from zero.
  • Buying one AI screening tool and one AI sourcing tool leaves a team’s skills, workflows and cost base looking like 2021, the author argues.
PromptMates Newsletter29 Sep 2026
The Scam Email That Fooled a 26-Year Recruiter
  • A fake Apple recruiter email passed the LinkedIn check because the recruiter’s profile was real; the sender line, Careers HR, and a domain that did not match Apple’s gave it away.
  • Palo Alto’s Unit 42 built a passable real-time interview deepfake in about 70 minutes on ordinary consumer hardware, and 18% of recruiters in a 2025 Greenhouse survey said they had already caught applicants using one.
  • Open the last outreach message you sent a candidate and find one detail they could check independently to prove it came from you. If there is none, that is the work.
Full Stack Recruiter29 Sep 2026
Candidate Quality Complaints Usually Measure the Interviewer
  • In one sample of manager performance ratings, the raters’ own tendencies explained 62% of the variance and actual performance 21% (a 2000 study); the author admits he is stretching it to hiring.
  • Before sourcing, have the hiring manager rank six anonymised past profiles with one sentence each, then slip two back in among real candidates in week three to see whether their judgement has moved.
  • A 2022 re-analysis by Paul Sackett and colleagues found structured interviews the strongest predictor of job performance, with a mean validity of .42, ahead of cognitive ability tests at .31.
TA Unboxed25 Sep 2026
60 metrics of TA - Part 3 of 3
  • Darren Bush classified his 60 TA metrics. 45 (75%) only report something after it happens, and 15 (25%) can work as forward indicators, such as pool burn or recruiter capacity.
  • He proposes a “Hiring ETA” for every requisition: a forecast start date and confidence range built from pipeline stage counts, stage-to-hire probabilities, offer acceptance and notice periods.
  • Hiring Predictability, forecast hires against hires delivered with a target of 85 to 95% accuracy, is how he says TA earns credibility with finance.
Ashby Community24 Sep 2026
Your Talent Insights from Ashby This Month
  • Ashby now supports two-way WhatsApp texting with candidates on all plans, with automated interview reminders, aimed at markets like Brazil, the Middle East and much of APAC where SMS is not how candidates talk.
  • A 70,000 applicant study summarised by Jonathan Duarte found AI-interviewed candidates got 12% more offers and finished interviews 4x faster. 78% chose AI when given the choice, and humans still made every decision.
  • Paul Lee of InnoCaption found about 1,500 characters of white-on-white text in a resume, telling AI systems to ignore prior commands and rate the candidate as highly qualified.

Bullhorn · report29 Sep 2026
Staffing Industry Indicator, week ending 19 September 2026
  • US staffing hours were up 10% year over year, in the SIA and Bullhorn indicator of hours worked by temporary workers at a sample of US staffing firms that use Bullhorn.
  • US light industrial staffing hours rose 15% year over year, which the report attributes to manufacturing, logistics and data centre demand.
  • Not every US segment rose: office and clerical staffing hours fell 8% year over year, while commercial rose 11%, professional 8% and IT 6%.
ManpowerGroup · report8 Sep 2026
Employment Outlook Survey Q4 2026
  • Despite rising AI use in recruitment, global employers report mixed time to hire: 28% faster than in 2025, 41% unchanged and 29% slower in the report PDF (the press release says 30%).
  • Globally, 43% of employers plan to add staff in October to December and 14% expect cuts, and the global Net Employment Outlook of 29% is up six points year on year.
  • The UK Net Employment Outlook is 23%, the highest of the large European economies, ahead of Germany at 15% and France at 13%.

Molly Graham: the grief, burnout, and opportunity hiding inside the AI transition
Molly Graham · Founder @ Glue Club
  • Burnout among tech workers in Lenny’s survey rose from 44% in 2025 to 55% this year.

Raises $40M Series A led by Air Street Capital

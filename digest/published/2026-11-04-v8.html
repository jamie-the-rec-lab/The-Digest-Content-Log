How to Automate Hiring Launches (Claude + Slack)
Emily Paik · Senior People Operations Manager @ Kit

Recruiting Future30 Oct 2026
Six Predictions For AI In HR
Vic Akosile · Head of AI Transformation and People Strategy @ Cloudflare
  • Vic Akosile judges AI on several dimensions of value beyond time saved, including scale, quality and risk mitigation.
  • Akosile's most controversial prediction is that staff will hide their AI use, because doing more work with AI just earns them more work.
  • With candidate AI and employer AI now talking to each other, Akosile expects a small live project during the interview to become the default way to assess capability.
Building the Talent Machine29 Oct 2026
Talent Engineering Hard Truths
Dan McCarthy · Senior Talent Engineer @ Zapier
  • Zapier talent engineer Dan McCarthy will not rebuild enterprise tools such as Juicebox, Metaview or Pin, because he says he would not build them better.
  • Before building any recruiting tool, McCarthy asks who the stakeholders are, who will maintain it and what the security risks are.
  • McCarthy's goal for automation is more time with people: 90 minutes with every candidate before offer, instead of 30.
Offer Accepted by Ashby20 Oct 2026
Halving Attrition With a Connected Career Framework
Nic Weatherhead · Global Vice President of People @ The Stepstone Group
  • Stepstone's career framework describes every role by scope, knowledge, autonomy and complexity, and it was piloted on the recruitment team first.
  • Where someone turned out to be in a smaller role than expected, Stepstone did not change their pay and built them a pathway to the right level instead.
  • Nic Weatherhead says AI agents change what autonomy means in a role, with less supervising of people and more verifying of information.

Full Stack Recruiter2 Nov 2026
Every Dead Link in Your Outreach Is a Candidate You Paid For and Lost
  • Over 12 months, 232 people clicked one agency's outreach links to jobs that no longer existed: 14% of the 1,657 people contacted.
  • After that agency redirected its dead job links, 31 people ended up in its ATS, 13 became active candidates and 2 were hired.
  • Pew found that a quarter of web pages from 2013 to 2023 were no longer accessible by October 2023.

Your Talent Insights from Ashby This Month
  • Inbound applications made up more than half of hires at Ashby customers by Q1 2026, at 54.3% in EMEA and 51.9% in AMER.
  • Across Ashby customers, only 28% of inbound hires receive the top quality of hire score, compared with 36% of referral hires.
  • From July 2027, California's No Robo Bosses Act stops employers relying solely on AI to discipline or fire someone, so a person must check its output.
TA Unboxed30 Oct 2026
How good are your recruiters at spotting talent?
  • TA Unboxed proposes an Early Talent Recognition Rate: how often a recruiter flagged the eventual hire as Standout before the first interview.
  • In the issue's worked example, two recruiters both spot 75% of eventual hires, but the one using half as many Standout flags gives the stronger signal.
  • Date-stamp every Standout flag and record the stage, so nobody can reach offer stage and claim they knew the candidate was brilliant all along.

JobsOutlook, June to August 2026
  • In REC's survey of 704 UK employers, economic confidence rose eight points to a net balance of -39% in June to August 2026.
  • UK employers' confidence in making hiring and investment decisions improved by six points to a net balance of -8%.
  • Short-term permanent hiring intentions among UK employers edged down one point to a net balance of +8%.
Pin · launch23 Oct 2026
Autopilot
  • Pin's Autopilot takes over the conversation once a candidate replies, answering questions, booking interviews and updating pipeline stages.
  • Teams can start with Autopilot drafts they review, or let it answer, book and follow up on its own, set team-wide or per job.
  • When Autopilot does not know an answer, Pin says it checks with the recruiter on Slack instead of guessing.
Teamtailor · launch29 Sep 2026
Smart action
  • Teamtailor's Smart action lets a recruiter describe a task in plain language, then AI applies it to every candidate who enters that stage.
  • Smart action only uses what a recruiter on the job would see, such as the CV and application answers, and leaves the record unchanged when information is missing.
  • Before a Smart action prompt is saved, a Verify check warns if the request could lead to biased decisions.

The AI chief of staff that this Figma PM uses all day
Ezra Mechaber · Growth Product Manager @ Figma

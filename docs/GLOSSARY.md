# GLOSSARY

What the words mean, and where the rules that use them live.

Every entry points at the document that owns it. The glossary never holds a
rule itself, because a second copy of a rule eventually disagrees with the
first. For where files live rather than what words mean, see the table at the
end of `RUN.md`.

---

**Adhoc**: the collection for anything found or sent manually, outside the
tracked sources. Eligible for the digest like anything else. `REFRESH.md`

**Blocked**: a report that could not be read at all, not even a summary. Nothing
is stored. Expected, and not a health failure. `REFRESH.md`

**Build copy**: the dated file in `digest/`, Jamie's archive version, with
newsletter links pointing at his Gmail. Kept forever. `PUBLISH.md`

**Collection**: one body of library content with its own folder, index, tags and
summary file. There are six. `REFRESH.md`

**Curve ball**: a wider-net item in section three from outside the tracked
sources. Never padded to reach a number. `SELECTION.md`

**Display name**: what a source is called on the page, where that differs from
its library name. Only PromptMates and RecOps Collective have one.
`config/sources.yaml`

**Full**: a report where the whole thing was captured. `REFRESH.md`

**This week in the world of recruiting**: the short preview above the standouts, formerly labelled "In this issue". Not a recap of them.
Deleted entirely on a week too scattered to preview honestly. `STYLE.md`

**Item id**: how an item is remembered so it never repeats. The library path
`Folder/file.md`, always the library name, never the display name. The URL for
web-only items. `SELECTION.md`

**Library name**: the folder a source lives in, and the name used for
remembering what has been featured. `config/sources.yaml`

**Partial**: a report where only a public summary was readable because the full
data is gated. Acceptable, and flagged for upgrading later. `REFRESH.md`

**Public copy**: the version committed to the website, with newsletter links
swapped to public URLs. Archived exactly as it went live. `PUBLISH.md`

**Quiet feed**: a source whose newest item is four weeks old or more. Advisory
only, unless its fetch also failed. `TROUBLESHOOTING.md`

**Standout**: one of up to five highlighted picks at the top of the page. Always
the strongest of that week's already-featured items, never a separate pick.
Chosen per `SELECTION.md`, written per `STYLE.md`

**Summary block**: the written summary at the top of every transcript. What makes
the archive searchable by what was said rather than only by title.
`docs/SUMMARY_SPEC.md`

**Teaser**: a trailer, preview or clip under 700 words. Routed aside
automatically and ignored by everything downstream. `REFRESH.md`

**Tech archive**: the collection holding Lenny's Podcast, How I AI, AI & I and
AI For Humans.
Out of everyday recruiting recall, but summarised like everything else, and it
fills section four. `config/sources.yaml`

**Three strikes**: anything appearing three times in the run log stops being a
hiccup. It gets fixed in the code or written up as a known problem.
`TROUBLESHOOTING.md`

**Tier A, Tier B**: the two groups of launch vendors. Tier A is favoured.
`SELECTION.md`

**Two gates**: the two tests every item must pass. Published within the window,
and never featured before. `SELECTION.md`

**Window**: the 8 week freshness limit. Nothing older is eligible, whatever else
is true about it. `SELECTION.md`

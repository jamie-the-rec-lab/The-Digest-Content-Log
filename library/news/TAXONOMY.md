# News and launches taxonomy

Tags for the `news` collection: product launches from tracked vendors, and the
weekly AI news item. Use 4 to 8 per item, only from this list.

## Type
product launch, acquisition, funding, regulation, legal, model release,
company move, market news

## Subject
ai agents, ai screening, ai sourcing, ai interviewing, ai governance,
ai transparency, automation, integrations, ats, crm, scheduling, assessment,
job advertising, analytics, candidate experience, human-ai collaboration

## Market
uk, us, eu, global

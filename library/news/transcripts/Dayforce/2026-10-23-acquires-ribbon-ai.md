## What was announced

Dayforce has agreed to acquire Ribbon AI Inc., a Toronto-based AI recruiting company. After the deal closes, Ribbon's technology will be integrated natively into the new Dayforce Agentic Recruiting experience, which would cover sourcing, screening, evaluation and candidate engagement. Purchase price and closing date were not disclosed.

## What Ribbon does (per the coverage)

- Conversational voice and video AI interviews that run around the clock, with a synthetic voice asking questions based on the job description.
- Generates interview questions and scoring rubrics from job descriptions and past interviews, and evaluates candidates from interview transcripts.
- Video and integrity checks flagging unusual audio, gaze and response-timing patterns (iTWire).
- AI sourcing and talent rediscovery in existing applicant tracking systems; outreach by SMS, WhatsApp and email.
- An AI scheduling agent is nearing completion.
- Company-reported figures, not independently verified: hundreds of customers, more than one million completed interviews, tested across as many as 30 million candidates in a single ATS, about 90% of customers on the default configuration.
- BetaKit: founded in 2022 by CEO Arsham Ghahramani and COO Dave Vu; raised $8.2M USD in early 2025 led by Radical Ventures.

## Context

BetaKit notes Dayforce is itself being taken private by Thoma Bravo for $12.3B USD, announced earlier in 2026. No coverage read states a continuity plan for Ribbon customers on other ATS platforms.

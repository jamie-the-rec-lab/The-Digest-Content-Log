## Key points
- LinkedIn CEO Dan Shapero says job seekers are sending "30% more applications than they did pre-pandemic", and "gotten harder and harder for employers to know who can actually do the job anymore."
- Job openings fell to 7.08 million in August, a five-month low (as reported in the article).
- Job seekers sent an average of 45 applications a month in May 2025, compared with 22 in 2024. Master's students averaged 32 to 60 applications a month and bachelor's students 15 to 38.
- Nearly two-thirds of applicants use AI for resume writing and interview prep.
- 99% of hiring managers use AI in hiring, and 98% report improvements in scheduling and screening.
- 63% of US job seekers have been interviewed by AI, up 13% from six months earlier.
- The article frames this as an AI-driven arms race: candidates use AI to stand out while recruiters deploy AI to filter large candidate pools, which makes distinguishing qualified candidates harder.

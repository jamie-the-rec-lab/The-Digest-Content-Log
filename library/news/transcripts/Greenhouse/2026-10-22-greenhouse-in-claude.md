## What was announced

Greenhouse launched Greenhouse in Claude, a plugin that lets recruiters and hiring managers work with candidates, interviews and open roles inside Anthropic's Claude. It draws on live hiring data and can take approved actions in Greenhouse. The release places it within the Orchestrated Hiring Ecosystem, which Greenhouse launched on 15 October 2026.

## Capabilities named in the release

- **Daily brief:** the candidates, interviews and scorecards waiting on the user.
- **Candidate status:** a candidate's current stage, history and recent activity.
- **Debrief preparation:** interview feedback gathered in one view, including who still owes a scorecard.
- **Pipeline review:** flags stalled candidates and shows time spent in each stage of an open role.
- **Scorecard drafts:** turns interview notes into a draft scorecard for the interviewer to review and submit.
- **Write access:** updates candidates, advances them, schedules interviews, and creates jobs and job posts.
- **Guided setup:** connects to Greenhouse and shows what the user's access includes.

## Permissions and controls

Built on Greenhouse MCP (Model Context Protocol). Each person signs in with their own Greenhouse account, so Claude can only see and do what that person can already do. Admins decide which information and actions are available. People make every hiring decision.

## Availability

Open beta, listed in the Claude directory, for Greenhouse Recruiting customers on Core, Plus and Pro plans. Works in Claude, Claude Cowork and Claude Code. No pricing is stated. The product page says the plugin launched with three skills.

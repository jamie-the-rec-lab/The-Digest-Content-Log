## Key figures and quotes

Company blog (author Matthew Wilson, 15 September 2026):
- Amount and lead: $40M Series A, lead Air Street Capital; co-investors named on the blog: Creandum, Madrona.
- Product: Jack is an AI voice agent helping professionals with career decisions; Jill is an AI recruiting agent; when both identify a suitable match they facilitate direct introductions between candidates and companies, bypassing traditional applications.
- Usage: 380,000 professionals in San Francisco, New York and London using Jack; over 5,000 companies hiring with Jill; clients named include Corgi, Ramp, Multiverse, Maze and Attio.
- Saaras Mehan (Co-Founder and CTO): "Candidates struggle to be seen, while employers cannot tell who is serious. More applications and better filters will not solve that."
- Nathan Benaich (General Partner, Air Street Capital): "The next leap in hiring will not come from better searches over the same resumes or more automated outreach."

The Next Web (Cristian Dina):
- $40M Series A, total $60M; lead Air Street Capital; new investors Madrona and Antler; returning Creandum, Ada Ventures, Entrepreneurs First, Expedite Capital, Repeat.vc.
- Jack represents candidates by voice call, email and WhatsApp; Jill represents employers; "They only introduce candidates to hiring managers when both sides agree the match warrants a conversation" (paraphrase by the fetch tool, not a verified quote).
- Figures: 350,000 people engaged with Jack (tenfold growth since seed); 5,000 businesses using Jill; 25,000 interviews arranged to date; 5,000 interviews per month; 12,000 hours on calls with Jack recently.
- Matt Wilson (Co-founder/CEO): "Every company has someone paid to represent its interests in the job market. People have never had the same for their careers."
- Benaich: "It will come from putting an agent on both sides of the table and making the introduction only when both sides would take the call."

Tech Funding News (Sofia Chesnokova): $40M Series A led by Air Street Capital, total funding $60M including a $20M seed round ten months earlier; candidate network up tenfold to 350,000; revenue up 40x since seed; 5,000 companies served; team of 25 in London and San Francisco. The 40x revenue figure appears only in this source.

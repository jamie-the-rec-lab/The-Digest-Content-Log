# Metaview launches Metaview Screening, an AI voice-screening agent

- **Source:** Metaview (primary announcement)
- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **URL:** https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/ai-screening-launch
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-16
- **Extraction:** full (retrieved from the vendor's own blog post)

## What was announced

Metaview CEO Siadhal Magos introduced Metaview Screening, an AI agent that
conducts voice screening interviews with candidates, positioned as solving
rising application volume while preserving candidate fairness and experience.

## Key figures and quotes

- Applications per recruiter have increased "412%" according to Greenhouse
  recruiting benchmarks, per the article
- The screening agent runs "real dialogue with adaptive, follow-up questions,"
  supports 18 languages, handles behavioral and situational scenarios, scores
  consistently against defined rubrics, and improves based on recruiter
  decisions
- Candidate satisfaction: "4.7/5 (CSAT)"
- Candidate quote: "I love the idea of this and feeling the pressure removed
  from an interview. Being able to be comfortable and just speak as myself is
  genuinely helpful."
- Human recruiters retain final advancement decisions; the stated philosophy
  is that AI provides signal, not final verdicts

# OpenAI introduces Dots, always-on agents, at DevDay 2026

- **Source:** OpenAI DevDay announcement, as reported by Let's Data Science and Business Today (OpenAI's own announcement page was not fetched)
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **URL:** https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-introduces-dots-always-on-ai-agents-at-devday-2026-2ced4872
- **Second source:** https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/openai-introduces-dots-always-on-ai-agents-that-work-247-and-act-on-your-behalf-558688-2026-09-30
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-07
- **Extraction:** full text of both articles read through a page-summarising fetch, so wording is from that extraction, not a raw copy. Two independent outlets agree on the date, the product and the availability. The primary OpenAI page was not read.

## Why this matters to a recruiter

Dots turn the "agent" idea into a product that persists between conversations: a recruiter can give one a standing responsibility (for example chasing hiring managers for feedback) and set limits on what it may do on its own. Availability is narrow at launch, so most teams will only be able to watch and plan, not deploy.

## Facts both sources support
- OpenAI introduced Dots at its 29 September 2026 DevDay.
- Dots are described as always-on agents that continue work on a user's behalf rather than waiting for each prompt.
- Initial availability is Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets; Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare get a beta when a workspace administrator enables it (off by default for managed workspaces).

## Let's Data Science, 29 September 2026 (verbatim extract)
"OpenAI introduced Dots at its September 29 DevDay event, presenting them as always-on agents that can continue work on a user's behalf rather than waiting for each new prompt."

"OpenAI describes a Dot as an agent that learns what matters to a user, works toward ongoing responsibilities and takes work off the user's plate. Users give a Dot a goal and set boundaries for what it may do on its own; the agent can keep work moving in the background and contact the user when it needs attention or more detail."

"OpenAI planned an initial rollout focused on higher-end subscribers and developers, with one Dot per user at launch."

"Dots turn the agent idea into a product that persists between conversations. This makes authorization, visibility and handoffs more important than in a one-off chat interaction."

## Business Today, 30 September 2026 (verbatim extract)
OpenAI described Dots as "remarkably capable, always-on agents built to handle everything." Each dot includes its own browser and connected applications; the article lists access to over 4,000 app plugins, integration across ChatGPT, Slack and Microsoft Teams, and communication via text, voice or messaging. It says Dots are rolling out to ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium subscribers in eligible markets, initially included at no additional cost with expanded allowances during the first month, and that OpenAI is developing enterprise variants integrated with Microsoft's Agent 365 governance and security controls. The article states Dots are powered by GPT-6 Astra; the Let's Data Science article does not state this.

## Gaps
- No price beyond "initially included at no additional cost" is stated in either source.
- The model behind Dots is stated by one source only (GPT-6 Astra).
- No recruiting-specific use case appears in either article; the recruiting angle comes from practitioner commentary (PromptMates Live, 30 September 2026, held in the podcasts library).

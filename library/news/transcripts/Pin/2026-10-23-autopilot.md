## What was announced

Pin announced Autopilot, described as "Your new pipeline, inbox, and AI recruiting assistant". Once a candidate replies to outreach, Autopilot takes over the conversation, answers their questions, books interviews and keeps candidates moving, while the recruiter stays in control of what goes out.

## Capabilities named on the page

- **Inbox:** logs key events such as a candidate replying or changing stage. Messages Autopilot sends are marked "Sent by agent".
- **Pipeline:** candidates move through Replied, Qualifying, Interviewing and Hired, and Autopilot updates the stages.
- **Outreach Automations:** teams can start with drafts they review, or let Autopilot answer questions, book interviews and follow up on its own. Settings apply team-wide or per job. When it does not know an answer it "checks with you on Slack instead of guessing".
- **Knowledge Base:** stores answers to common candidate questions about compensation, interview steps and work setup. Company-wide information carries over to every role at that company. A toggle keeps the company name out of outreach for confidential clients.

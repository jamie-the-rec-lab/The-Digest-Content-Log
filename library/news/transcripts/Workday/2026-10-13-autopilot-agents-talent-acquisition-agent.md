# Workday launches Autopilot Agents, including a Talent Acquisition Agent, at Workday Rising 2026

## What was announced

At Workday Rising 2026 in Las Vegas on 13 October 2026, Workday launched "Autopilot Agents": AI agents that run in the background on routine work across HR, finance and IT, including hiring, payroll, accounting and compliance. They operate under each company's own policies, permissions and approval rules. Customers decide which tasks run automatically, which need review and when a person makes the final call. Every action is logged. Sana, Workday's orchestration engine, coordinates the agents and brings in people when judgement is needed.

## The recruiting agent

The Talent Acquisition Agent, per Workday's release:
- monitors open roles and flags those at risk of missing their fill date;
- after a job is posted, finds qualified candidates and gives recruiters their qualifications and potential gaps;
- when the pipeline is thin, alerts recruiters in tools such as Slack and drafts outreach, but recruiter approval is required before anything is sent;
- can coordinate interview scheduling, give interviewers context and draft feedback for review.

Reworked (13 Oct 2026) adds that it runs on a "heartbeat" that scans every open requisition whether or not a recruiter is online, builds shortlists with fit explanations as applicants arrive, and once a recruiter advances a candidate it schedules the interview, joins the call and pre-fills notes from the transcript. It names integrations with Workday Recruiting, HiredScore, Paradox and third-party providers. Borncity (14 Oct 2026, in German) confirms that contacting candidates still requires approval from HR staff.

## Availability

Autopilot capability for the Talent Acquisition Agent is available now to early adopter customers, with general availability planned for early 2027. Payroll, Accounting and Deployment agents are planned for early adopters in early calendar 2027.

## What was announced

HOLMDEL, N.J., 20 October 2026: iCIMS announced its 2026 Fall Release, including the iCIMS Hiring Agent, an agentic AI experience for recruiters and hiring managers in Microsoft Teams. It is built on the iCIMS Intelligent Hiring Platform. It tracks hiring activity, identifies which tasks need attention and routes them to the right person in Teams. Actions sync with iCIMS workflows and candidate records. Recruiters and managers make the final decisions.

## Capabilities named in the release

- **Act within Teams:** handle candidate reviews, approvals and hiring actions in Teams while staying connected to the iCIMS applicant tracking system.
- **Improve collaboration:** capture feedback and send in-context messages between recruiters and hiring managers without leaving Teams.
- **Reduce bottlenecks:** prioritise work, suggest next-best actions and send proactive notifications to keep candidates from stalling.

## Availability

Rollout to iCIMS customers begins 26 October 2026.

## WHAT'S NEW AT ASHBY

### Product Updates

**Introducing the Ashby MCP Server**
Ashby's MCP Server connects MCP-compatible AI clients like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and others directly to live recruiting data in Ashby, so you don't need to build or maintain custom integrations. Available on Foundations, Plus, and Enterprise plans.

**Also released this month:**

- **Bulk actions on custom list reports:** Take bulk actions on custom list reports for job considerations, jobs, candidates, email sequences, and openings, including archiving job considerations across any number of jobs at once.

- **Custom prompts for AI Notetaker auto-fill:** Create and save custom prompts so feedback drafts consistently follow your preferred style, format, and evaluation guidance. Admins can set company-wide instructions, and interviewers can save their own prompts for different roles.

- **Use AI report filters to create report filters in plain language:** Build report filters the same intuitive way you already use AI filters for candidate search.

Plus 14 more improvements across ATS, scheduling, reporting, and AI.

### Partnership Updates

The more connected your stack, the faster your team moves. Here's what's new in the Ashby ecosystem:

- **ADP Workforce Now Next Generation** [Post-Hire]: Sync employee and organizational data between Ashby and ADP Workforce Now Next Generation to keep records consistent from hire through onboarding.

- **TestGorilla** [Assessment]: Source, screen, and shortlist candidates with TestGorilla's assessment and talent discovery platform, connected directly to Ashby.

- **Niural AI** [Post-Hire]: Connect Ashby to Niural's global payroll and PEO platform to move new hires smoothly from offer to employment across 150+ countries.

- **Alex AI** [Assessment]: Let Alex AI handle screening, scheduling, and interviewing for your open roles while you stay in the loop at every step.

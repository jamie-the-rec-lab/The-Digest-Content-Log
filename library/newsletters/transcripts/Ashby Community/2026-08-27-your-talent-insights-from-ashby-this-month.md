## Talent Trends: Recruiter Productivity 2026 report

Ashby's Recruiter Productivity 2026 report analyzed 109M applications and 247K jobs: "as applications per hire increased from 100 in 2021 to an average of 291 today, the share of candidates receiving an interview fell by about 50%." By Q1 2026, offer conversion reached 10.4% for business roles and 7.3% for technical roles, both above 2021 levels.

## Recruiting takes from around the web

- OpenAI's "How AI is expanding what people do at work": 43.5% of occupation-specific ChatGPT use crosses into tasks associated with other jobs, rising to 69% for HR.
- Chris Abbass (CEO, Talentful): argues AI has made take-home assessments a weaker signal; assessments should focus on how candidates use AI - framing problems, challenging outputs, catching mistakes, explaining decisions.

## Product updates

- Conditional form fields: show a field only when relevant (e.g. "Visa type" appears only if candidate selects "Visa Holder"), available on all plans.
- Automated E-Signature Sends: auto-send non-offer documents like NDAs when a candidate enters a stage (Plus/Enterprise).
- Conversational agent creation and editing: describe an agent in plain language and Ashby drafts the prompt (all plans).

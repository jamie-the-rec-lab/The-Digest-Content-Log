## What's Happening in Hiring: Highlights from Ashby One London

- EMEA Talent Trends: Inside the Data Shaping Hiring Today. Ashby's Ben Hubbard sat down with Amanda Johnson of DeepL and Célia Sauthier of Moss to unpack how EMEA hiring volume has doubled since 2021 without time to hire slipping, and what changed inside their recruiting processes to make that possible.
- Quality of Hire at Enterprise Scale. Tim Chattaway of Sony Sports Technology and Laura Barnes of Flock compared how their teams measure Quality of Hire, including rebuilding scorecards around justified conviction rather than middle-of-the-road ratings.
- Building with Confidence: AI Risk and Regulation. Ashby's Senior Legal Counsel Kelly Fish walked through how the EU AI Act's risk-based framework applies to recruiting tools, joined by Tourlane's Max Rauschen and hyperexponential's Lucy Szypula on turning that into day-to-day AI guardrails.

## Recruiting Takes from Around the Web

- Jonathan Duarte breaks down a new study on AI-led interviews. Researchers randomly assigned 70,000 job applicants to human interviewers, AI interviews or their own preferred format, and found that AI-interviewed candidates received 12% more offers and completed interviews 4x faster. Given the choice, 78% of applicants picked AI over a person. Duarte's read: it was the speed and consistency of the AI-driven process that helped, as a human recruiter still made every hiring decision in the study.
- Recruiters are flagging a new twist on resume manipulation: candidates hiding AI prompts inside their applications to game automated screening tools. Paul Lee, CEO of InnoCaption, found roughly 1,500 characters of white-on-white text buried in a resume for a legal and compliance role. The hidden instruction told AI systems to ignore prior commands and mark the candidate as highly qualified regardless of actual credentials.

## Recent Guests on the Offer Accepted Podcast

- Episode 73, Hiring for Durable Skills to Build Teams That Last. Veronica Salcido, who leads Talent and L&D at Growth Assistant, shares how her team traded a serious churn problem for a durable skills framework that runs through hiring, coaching and performance reviews. She walks through the data that overturned her own assumptions about what predicts a good hire, and why she tied a real share of her recruiters' KPIs to what happens well after someone signs.
- Episode 74, Building an Ideal Candidate Profile from Zero to Scale. Maggie Fish, Director of Recruiting at Grafana Labs, shares how her team replaced gut feel with a data-backed standard for what a great hire looks like. They mined attrition records and performance reviews to define an ideal candidate profile, then rebuilt their interview process so every stage tested for one clear attribute instead of eight competing ones, and built an AI tool that lets hiring managers work through their own thinking before they get on a call with a recruiter.

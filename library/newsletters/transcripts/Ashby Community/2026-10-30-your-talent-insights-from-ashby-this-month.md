## What's Happening in Hiring

**Ashby's RecOps Certification is now open to everyone.** The course is fully on demand in Ashby Academy, tool-agnostic, best suited for early-career TA and RecOps professionals, free and self-paced.

**Most hires now come from inbound, but referrals still score higher on quality.** "Inbound applications have grown steadily as a share of hires across both EMEA and AMER, and by Q1 2026 they accounted for more than half of all hires (54.3% in EMEA, 51.9% in AMER). However, across all Ashby customers, only 28% of inbound hires receive the top score, compared with 36% of referrals. Inbound hires averaged 3.8 out of 5 on Quality of Hire, against 4.0 for referrals and 3.9 for sourced hires. With inbound now the biggest source of hires, TA teams should focus on strengthening their screening process and building a strong referral programme alongside it."
Sources cited: Ashby EMEA Recruiter Productivity report (2026); Ashby Quality of Hire report (2026).

## Recruiting Takes from Around the Web

- Anthropic's report "How Employment is Changing at Anthropic": "Since early 2025, the share of junior employees in its technical teams has fallen from 21% to 14%. This occurred as hours worked by AI agents relative to employees rapidly expanded. Junior hires have dropped even faster, from 27% to 9% of technical hiring. As a result, half a decade after its founding, Anthropic generates two to four times the revenue per worker as tech giants like Meta and Google did during their peak scaling phases."
- California's No Robo Bosses Act "is the first US law to stop employers relying solely on AI to discipline or fire someone. From July 2027, a person has to check the AI's output. Employers also have to tell workers in writing when they relied mainly on AI for the decision."
- Ghost jobs and fake applicants have created a hiring trust gap, per iHire's 2026 State of Online Recruiting report: "Nearly 1 in 5 employers (19.2%) named fake or fraudulent applicants as a top recruiting challenge, and 23.6% received at least one in the past year. Job seekers are wary, too: 39.3% called fake or 'ghost' jobs a chief challenge, and 41.2% reported encountering a fake or scam job in the past year."

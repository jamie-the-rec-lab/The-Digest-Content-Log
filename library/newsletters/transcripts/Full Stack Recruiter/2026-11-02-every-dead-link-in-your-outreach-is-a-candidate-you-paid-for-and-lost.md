# Every Dead Link in Your Outreach Is a Candidate You Paid For and Lost

- **Source:** Full Stack Recruiter
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Fetched:** 2026-11-04
- **Public:** https://newsletter.fullstackrecruiter.net/p/recruiting-outreach-dead-links

## Newsletter

(Author promotes his own open-source tool, Shorthop. Flag as self-promotion.)

"232 people clicked a link to a job that no longer existed. That's 14% of the 1,657 candidates one recruiter contacted over twelve months." Nobody sees them leave: no rejection email, no ATS record.

**The case (one agency owner friend; the author notes "I only have his word on how he counted the 13").** The friend ran every outreach link through a URL shortener he controlled, and when a role closed pointed the link at a "position filled, here are other roles" page. After twelve months: 1,657 people contacted; 14% clicked after the role was filled; "From that group, 31 people ended up in his ATS. 13 of them became active candidates in processes over the year and 2 were hired." It works for a niche agency where roles repeat; a generalist in-house team will see less overlap.

**Context figures.** Pew Research Center (2024) checked almost a million webpages from 2013-2023: "A quarter of them were no longer accessible by October 2023.", and 8% of 2023 pages were gone within the same year. Appcast tracked 500,000 job seekers: cutting an application to five minutes or less "could increase conversion by up to 365%" (about long forms, not dead links; the author flags this). Talent Board 2016: 66% of candidates share negative recruiting experiences with friends, family and peers and 34% on social media (the author admits he is "stretching that finding"). The author also states: "In my experience, about 3-5% of all visitors still arrive at the dead link" (his own estimate, no source). He admits: "I don’t have data for 99%." (about his own original "99% of companies lose candidates this way" claim).

**Shorthop (self-promotion).** Open-source URL shortener, MIT licence, runs as a single Cloudflare Worker on your own domain, free plan. Features: editable redirects, retire-and-replace links, version history and 90-day recovery of deleted links, click stats with no cookies or IPs stored, UTM builder, QR codes, A/B splits, imports from Bitly and Short.io, an MCP server. Advice: one short link per role, change the destination the same day the role closes, audit monthly by clicks in the last 30 days.

**Costs and open problems.** Someone has to remember to redirect; a stale "other roles" page is a second dead end; some email gateways flag short URLs ("I don't have good data on how many outreach emails that hurts"); most of the roughly 232 clickers still left (31 reached the ATS).

### Big Idea: Talent Redeployment Over Reduction

Bank of America's AI upskilling team shared their approach to managing workforce transitions in the age of AI. Rather than cutting roles or headcount when AI automation becomes possible, their strategy focuses on redeploying talent to higher-value work.

The key insight: when AI can handle routine tasks, the better ROI comes from upskilling and redirecting existing employees to strategic initiatives, client-facing roles, or adjacent functions that weren't previously staffed. This approach preserves institutional knowledge, maintains company culture, and reduces hiring and training costs.

### Harvard Business Review: The AI Employee Trap

New research from Harvard Business Review reveals that organizations treating AI as a standalone "employee" rather than as a tool that augments human workers actually perform worse on accountability and error-catching metrics. Companies that framed AI as a collaborative assistant rather than a replacement achieved better outcomes in adoption and reduced errors.

The finding challenges common "AI as workforce replacement" narratives and suggests that explicit framing of AI's role impacts both organizational performance and employee confidence in AI systems.

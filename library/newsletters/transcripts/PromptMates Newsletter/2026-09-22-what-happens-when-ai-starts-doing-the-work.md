## Newsletter

### Build of the week: judgment models

Most recruiting AI workflows use an LLM to generate an answer. A judgment model is a different category: instead of asking AI to create something, "you give it a decision to make," returning "a judgment and how confident it is in that decision." The newsletter frames the fit for HR as evaluation work rather than generation: "A lot of recruiting work is not generation. It is evaluation." Suggested use cases: "outreach QA, candidate rediscovery, application triage, interview feedback, or checking whether an AI-generated output meets defined standards before it reaches a candidate." The framing question: "not whether AI should replace human judgment. It is whether AI can handle the obvious calls so recruiters spend more time on the ones that actually require them."

### Worth seeing and reading

- **Personalization over volume:** a founder stepping back into recruiting "narrowed searches to 30 to 50 candidates and spent about 15 minutes personalizing each message, producing a 60%+ positive acceptance rate."
- **AI governance gap (Zapier survey, 518 executives at companies with an AI governance policy):** "15% give AI full, unsupervised autonomy, while 38% say ungoverned AI has already cost them time, money, or reputation." Written policies alone are not producing consistent oversight.
- **Speed vs direction:** "automating a messy process only helps you make bad decisions faster." Recommended approach: data to find friction, AI to remove repetitive work, recruiter judgment to decide what improves the hire.
- **AI adoption and curiosity:** adoption may depend less on training than on comfort experimenting without a playbook.
- **Silver-medalist rediscovery workflow:** an n8n workflow connecting Greenhouse, Claude and Slack to rescore previous finalists against new roles and route the strongest matches to recruiters before outreach.

### Platform updates

- **Salesforce** expanded Agentforce with prebuilt job-ready agents, including an IT/HR service agent, plus tools letting employees teach an agent a task once and reuse it across the business; agents can work together across multi-step workflows over days or weeks.
- **OpenAI** launched GPT-Live-1 in the API, the real-time voice model behind ChatGPT Live, enabling more natural voice agents for candidate screening, interview prep and recruiting support.

### Big idea

"Your next SOP might not be a document. It might be something AI can actually run." Basis turned its onboarding knowledge into an AI-executable skill (defined steps, resources, a definition of done, exception rules) and "compressed a two-hour first-day onboarding process into 30 minutes without removing the human interactions." The newsletter poses this as a general test for TA/HR documentation: "does this knowledge need to be read, or could it be run?"

### Build of the week: automate everything between offer accepted and day one
Jake Teagle, Senior Talent Acquisition Partner at V7, published a free Claude skill that picks up when a candidate accepts: pulls details from Ashby, asks you to confirm location and manager, sends the offer-accept email from Gmail and logs it in Ashby, schedules a Slack nudge to the manager "halfway to the start date to assign a buddy", books the first meetings, builds a first-day plan in Notion and schedules a "Your first day" email. "Nothing goes to another person without your clear yes in the chat." Built for Ashby; setup in six steps; caution to read SKILL.md in full and check data policy.

### Big idea: when agents do the work, human attention becomes the bottleneck
Anthropic's economists published "How employment is changing at Anthropic" (16 October), drawing on internal data on headcount, AI use, output, surveys, calendars and hiring scorecards plus 17 employee interviews. The newsletter cautions: "it is an AI company writing about itself, at the extreme end of adoption, so read it as an early signal rather than a benchmark."
- "Agent hours overtook human hours. By August, agents directed by employees logged six times as many hours as their human handlers."
- "Code commits per engineer were 5x their rate of six months earlier."
- "The junior share of technical hires fell from 27% to 9%, even as the company grew."
- "Even for non-technical staff, reading everything their interactive Claude sessions produce would take over half the working week. Those sessions spend around 80% of their time waiting for a human."
- "Building skills, specs, reviews and monitors for agents rose from 10% to 22% of employees' Claude sessions."
Hiring data: comments on creative use of AI tools in interview scorecards "tripled for technical roles and quadrupled for non-technical roles between 2024 and 2026"; for technical candidates the biggest increases were in autonomy and judgement. Suggested actions: rewrite scorecards around judgement, budget review time before deploying agents, run an early-warning view of TA tasks exposed to automation, decide deliberately about junior hiring.

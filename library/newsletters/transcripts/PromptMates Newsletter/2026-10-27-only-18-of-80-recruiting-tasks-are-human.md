### Big idea: automation owns the middle, recruiters own both ends
Glen Cathey sat through 15 sessions at RecFest USA (speakers from Marriott, Ford, Merck, US Department of Labor). Johnny Campbell of SocialTalent calls it a barbell: "His team mapped about 80 recruiting tasks: 30 fully automatable, 32 hybrid and 18 uniquely human." Quote: "Automation owns the middle. The talent advisor owns both ends. The what to hire for and the who to hire." "In a survey by background-check provider Checkr, 40% of US hiring managers said they had interviewed someone using a fake identity." Campbell: "Too many recruiters are spending their time trying to see if someone's real, as opposed to trying to find evidence that they can do the job." Open question: if the middle is automated, where do the next recruiters get their reps? Suggested actions: audit your team's week into automate/hybrid/human, give identity checks a proper owner, turn intake calls into strategy calls (30, 60, 90 day success), spend saved time on capability, plan junior hiring deliberately.

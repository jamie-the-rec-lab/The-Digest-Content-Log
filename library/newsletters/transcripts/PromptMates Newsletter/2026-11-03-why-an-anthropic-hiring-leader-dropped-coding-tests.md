### Big idea
"This month, I laid the traditional coding interview to rest," writes Felix Rieseberg, a hiring manager at Anthropic. The newsletter recalls the Anthropic data covered two weeks earlier: the junior share of its technical hires fell from 27% to 9%. "If Claude can pass your test, the test no longer tells you who to hire." Rieseberg (who led engineering for Claude.ai, Cowork and Claude Code Desktop) found small coding challenges "a weak but useful predictor", but Claude now does them better, and "our most impactful builders are successful regardless of how good they are at these tests." "Hunger beats tenure." He hasn't found the replacement: "I'm scanning resumes and portfolios for little prototypes that exhibit taste, empathy, and principled thinking." Suggested actions: run your own tests through AI, ask for a prototype not a puzzle, take tenure out of job descriptions, set the junior share on purpose.

### Build of the week: launch a role with one Slack command
Emily Paik, Senior People Operations Manager at Kit and a one-person recruiting and people ops team, saw each new role take "30+ minutes of manual work across Slack, Ashby and Notion". Now a /newrole command opens the role in Ashby (job create, opening create, opening add job), creates a correctly named Slack channel, invites stakeholders and pins resources (Notion hub, Claude projects for JDs and interview plans). Built as a Node.js app on Slack's Bolt framework, hosted on Replit with the Ashby API key in Replit Secrets; Claude was her coding partner. Caveat: agree the app with IT and data protection first.

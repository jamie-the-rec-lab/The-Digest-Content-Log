### News

**Tech recruiter jobs are back. Engineering hires aren't.** "TrueUp counts 2,857 open tech recruiter jobs this month, up 323% from a low of 676, and Lenny Rachitsky calls that a record high. US engineering hires in the year to April 2026 were about 113,000, less than half the 2022 peak, so the work per hire has grown." Sources cited: Lenny Rachitsky (https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-tech-job-market-late) and TrueUp's recruiter report (https://www.trueup.io/recruiting/reports).

**A fake employer, with ID-verified recruiters.** "JFrog traced AccuraSoftHire, a fake employer whose LinkedIn recruiter profiles carried the verification shield, and whose take-home test loaded a malicious npm package. The shield only confirms a government ID, not the job title or the company". Points to Luke Eaton's hiring safety playbook (https://www.lukeeaton.com/hiring-safety-playbook). Source: JFrog (https://research.jfrog.com/post/accurasofthire-fake-interview/).

**The UK plans to make job adverts show pay.** "The UK government has committed to a law requiring salaries in job adverts, but it isn't law yet, and is asking employers to publish pay now. Indeed Hiring Lab found 58% of UK postings included pay in the three months to June, down from 64% in September 2024." Sources: Indeed Hiring Lab (https://hiringlab.indeed.com/uk/blog/2026/08/03/mid-year-uk-jobs-hiring-trends-report/) and GOV.UK (https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-say-what-you-pay-law-to-help-young-people-into-work).

**Why beehiiv dropped job titles.** "Tyler Denk, co-founder and CEO of beehiiv, has dropped internal titles, his own included, describing staff by job family and using "Lead" for people with direct reports. He treats title-chasing in hiring as a red flag, though many of the 500-plus comments argue it only hides the hierarchy." Source: https://mail.bigdeskenergy.com/p/untitled

**Remote's People team built its own ATS.** "Remote didn't renew seven HR software contracts this year, replacing them with tools its People team built with Claude, HR Brew reports. Six people, including three recruiters, shipped an ATS in five months that has handled more than 24,000 applications, without saying how screening works or what it costs." Source: HR Brew (https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/remotes-hr-team-ditched-external-vendors-and-is-building-their-own-hr-systems-with-ai).

### Tools

**Grok Bot 1.0 adds team bots, meetings and follow-ups.** "A shared Grok Bot can sit in Slack or Microsoft Teams and join Meet, Zoom and Teams calls, answering in the chat and sending a summary." Suggested use: sit in intake calls and hiring debriefs, then post notes and next steps to the hiring channel. Source: https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0

**Anthropic brings Claude into meetings.** "Claude Meetings is in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, on Google Meet only, and joins as a visible participant that answers in chat and saves a transcript. By default it stays out of calls with outside guests, so it won't join candidate interviews unless an admin allows it." Source: https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-meetings

**Google adds skills across Workspace.** "Google is rolling out Skills, reusable SKILL.md instructions that give Gemini a team's rules and templates, in Gmail, Docs, Slides, Drive and Chat on Business and Enterprise plans." HR uses named: consistent job adverts, offer letters and policy answers; "Scheduled Release domains getting it from 30 November". Source: https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html

### Grok Bot runs the hiring admin. He keeps the judgement

Phil Jacobson, Chief Business Officer at Altitude, "has handed the operations side of his hiring to Grok Bot, so his own time goes on running interviews and judging candidates." He mapped the loop first: "Candidates are sourced, he reviews them, books the ones he wants to meet, interviews them and then passes or advances them. The bot works across a Notion hiring database, Granola for call recordings, Slack, email and Notion Calendar."

### The recruiter of the near future is a recruiting agent manager

Arvind KC, Chief People Officer at OpenAI (previously Chief People and Systems Officer at Roblox), in a conversation with Tomasz Tunguz on Theory Ventures' Office Hours. "In Theory's summary, he expects every role to gain an "agent manager" dimension. A salesperson becomes a sales agent manager and a recruiter becomes a recruiting agent manager, with scope and pay following how well someone coordinates a few humans and many virtual coworkers towards an outcome."

Newsletter's own angle: "Most TA teams still size capacity by requisitions per recruiter and level recruiters by years of experience. If a recruiter's output is the hires their agents help close, both measures need rethinking."

"He would drop the “junior” and “senior” labels, since experience with AI dates quickly, and hire for curiosity, intensity and humility instead". "He suggests starting by using the tools heavily, then rebuilding one workflow the People team owns, such as onboarding." "If recruiters become agent managers, theirs is the first job description to rewrite." Source: https://theoryvc.com/blog-posts/beyond-automation-office-hours-with-arvind-kc

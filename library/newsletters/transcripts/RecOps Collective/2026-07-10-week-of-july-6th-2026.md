### A Piece of Advice That Will Never Go Out of Fashion, by Jeremy Lyons

Early in my TA/RecOps career, I had a leader pull me aside and give me the sage advice of: "If you are the smartest person in the room, you need to find a new room."

Now, I can't tell you what prompted this nugget. Maybe they were having a day or maybe they recognized something. Truth be told, I don't know the answer. What I can say, though, is that it extended beyond just networking advice.

But the more I talk to TA/RecOps practitioners, the more it becomes a reality that many of us never find the next room. Sure we see people leave the room we are in and come back later. We just never follow them to see where they are going so we never get the new ideas or see the new ideas before they show up.

So what should you do to change this?

**Find the smartest people at your company and say "hi."** One beautiful piece of TA that is underutilized is the fact that we have to interface with every department in the company. If you catch wind of another group working on something you find interesting, ask if you can sit at their lunch table to be a fly on the wall.

**Knock on doors.** You don't have to be a LinkedIn influencer for doors to open. You can knock on those doors and ask to be let in. Create a list of communities, companies or people talking about things you find interesting then reach out. Sometimes you don't even need to reach out because they post exactly what you are looking for.

**Be ok with being a nerd.** I've said it before, no one can learn for you. Sometimes, you will be all alone, spelunking into a topic. This is ok. Not all learning needs to be used for economic gain but all learning can make you better.

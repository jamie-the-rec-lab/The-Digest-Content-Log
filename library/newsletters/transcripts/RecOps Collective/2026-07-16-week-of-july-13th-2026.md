## Newsletter

Your contributions make our community newsletter possible. Packed with valuable and, most importantly, neutral content, this newsletter includes articles, engaging images/graphics, and job openings/searchers in the RecOps space. It is a love letter by the RecOps community for the RecOps community.

### A penny for your thoughts…

Every week, this section of the newsletter features a topic we believe will help the broader TA/RecOps community. Sometimes it is serious (e.g., how to monitor fraud, how to do a configuration), and some weeks it is more whimsical or advisory. The point is to create a space where everyone feels like they can share what they are thinking, whether as themselves or anonymously. So, if you'd like to share something here, reach out. We're also happy to help you fine-tune the idea and wordsmith it with you.

Talent Engineers and TA/RecOps by Jeremy Lyons
At the AI Recruiting Summit, Dan [link], David [link], and I got a great question from the audience that prompted me to ask “If the Talent Engineer’s role is to increase efficiency, and the goal of the TA/RecOps team is to increase efficiency, then did the TA/RecOps team miss the mark if the newer role (the Talent Engineer) is also there to do the same thing?”
It was not meant as a doom-and-gloom question, but rather a broader question about the two roles. If everyone is there to increase efficiency, which part is inefficient and should it be removed?
One person in the comments said that the TA/RecOps role didn’t miss the mark, but rather the intent of the role changed. Which, in my opinion, is a very good answer and also creates more questions too. The biggest of those two questions are:
If the intent of the TA/RecOps role is to create efficiency, did it actually become a bureaucratic layer more than an operational one?
 Will more TA/Recruiting teams transition into TA/RecOps as the orchestration layer, helping to get the context and organizational structure, with TA Engineers as the builders?
Only time will tell there but would be great to hear the thoughts of others? And again, this isn’t doom-and-gloom, this is a thought exercise.

## Newsletter

**What is the friendliest ATS for neurodivergent folks?**

This week in recruiting operations, the community is weighing in on accessibility and neurodiversity in applicant tracking systems. A critical question for TA leaders: which platforms provide the best experience for neurodivergent candidates?

### Key Discussions This Week

**ATS Accessibility & Neurodiversity**
The conversation centers on which ATS platforms—Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, and others—offer the best experience for neurodivergent job seekers. Key considerations include:

- Clean, uncluttered interfaces that reduce cognitive load
- Clear, linear application flows without unnecessary steps
- Predictable navigation and interaction patterns
- Minimal sensory overload (animations, auto-play, flashing elements)
- Mobile-responsive design that works consistently
- Clear instructions and error messages
- Reasonable time limits for completing applications
- Support for assistive technologies

**Why This Matters**
Neurodivergent candidates represent a significant talent pool often overlooked by traditional recruiting practices. Creating accessible application experiences isn't just the right thing to do—it's a competitive advantage. Companies that prioritize neurodiversity in their hiring are accessing talent that other organizations miss.

For RecOps professionals, this raises operational questions:
- How do we audit our ATS for accessibility?
- What metrics should we track?
- Which platforms actually deliver on neurodiversity commitments?
- How do we train hiring managers on accessible hiring practices?

**Practical Steps Forward**
If you're evaluating or managing an ATS, consider adding neurodiversity-focused accessibility testing to your RFP criteria and ongoing vendor reviews. Talk to your neurodivergent employees and candidates about their experience. Small adjustments in your application flow and system configuration can make a significant difference.

## What Would Happen If You Lost the Tech?

By Jeremy Lyons

Due to an internet outage and my phone conveniently deciding this week is the right time to be able to tether, I was lost about a day and a half of work time this week. And while that is work time I can't make up, it got me thinking: what happens when the tech you rely on suddenly stops working, but you still need to be able to accomplish your tasks? After all, the work doesn't stop.

As an RC, I remember living through several ATS outages or Zoom/Skype breaks, which required getting creative and manual to scheduling or finding new tech. As a TA/RecOps lead, I remember a pandemic shutting things down and quickly pivoting without missing a beat. As a consultant, especially one building things with AI, things break all the time and you have to think or create new ways to fix or revert them.

But, as a voice in the community, I think about the folks who came up in a different era, one where things like scheduling tools, AI, and other RecTech tools are so ubiquitous they never learned how to do it another way. Now, before people think this is gonna be me turning into someone yelling at kids to get off the lawn, it isn't. It is me saying, throwing out a reminder that there is still validity in learning the old ways since they help form the foundational layer under the new solutions.

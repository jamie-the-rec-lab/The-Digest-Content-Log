## **Advice for the Coordinator to Ops Transition**

By Jeremy Lyons

One conversation that always excites me to have, and happens often, is when a TA Leader or RC reaches out to me to get advice about the transition from RC or TA Ops Coordinator to purely being in the TA/RecOps space. Recently a TA leader, one I respect tremendously, reached out to ask for advice and the convo started with a little something like this:

"I moved my coordinator into a Talent Ops role recently. They're doing decent with anything I point them towards (reporting, audits, etc.) but feel like I'm missing the opportunity to help her build strong RecOps foundations. If you were new to the role - where would you be spending your time learning? fwiw - a lot of the work I have them focused on is for candidate experience."

Over the years, I realized I usually answer the front part but this time I decided to write something specific for the leader and specific for the individual. So this is what I wrote:

**For You (The Leader)** - I would try to steer completely out of candidate experience. They know candidate experience because of their previous work, but unless you push them into areas they don't know, they are going to default to what they already know, which is how to be a coordinator. Bring them along to meetings with you; put them in areas they don't know. Get them uncomfortable.

**For Them (The Grower)** - Have them embrace discomfort. Doing what you know over and over just further ingrains habits and dogmatic principles rather than actually building new skills or seeing new problems. This role is for the people that have to be curious but also open to taking educated risks.

But what would your advice be?

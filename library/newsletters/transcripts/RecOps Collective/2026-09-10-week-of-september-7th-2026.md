## Ashby One London keynote recap (by Nitin Moorjani, Talent Ops Consultant/Founder at Peak Grid)

Key announcements from the keynote:

- **WhatsApp communication:** native candidate messaging channel alongside Ashby's existing texting.
- **Scheduled agents:** a recurring cadence, like a weekly pipeline recap or Friday interview calibration review, that runs automatically and pauses only for human confirmation.
- **Career page builder:** visual, template-based editor for a fully branded careers page, no developer needed, targeting early 2027.
- **Agentic scheduling:** finds interview times across candidate and interviewer availability and confirms the schedule, with a copilot mode that pauses for human confirmation before anything sends. Launching later this year.
- **Ashby MCP:** connects Ashby's recruiting data to external AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor, respecting the same permissions that apply inside Ashby.
- **AI notetaker recording sharing:** share a full interview recording or a clipped moment with another Ashby user, including a link to the exact spot in the call.
- **AI-assisted application review and report builder**, and a dedicated **EU data centre** planned for 2027.

"91% of Ashby customers now use its AI features daily." Co-founder and VP of Engineering Abhik Pramanik said that a year ago nearly every customer conversation opened with "does your product have AI?", a question that has "largely disappeared," replaced by harder questions about what a recruiter should still own versus what should be automated.

### ROCA awards update

The 2026 Recruiting Operations & Coordination Awards (ROCAs) are returning, timed later than 2025 as a year-end send-off: categories announced mid-October, voting opens late October for one month, voting closes late November, winners announced early December. For 2027 they plan a rolling submission form to celebrate RecOps/TA achievements throughout the year rather than only at year end.

### The Power of Network

Make sure you talk regularly with TA and RecOps folks outside your network. It helps with learning new ideas, picking up skills and gaining perspective, and it helps uncover things you might have missed. Example: a catch-up call with a long-time network colleague who asked how they were helping companies adjust to California's AB 692, which "has huge impacts of sign-on and relocation bonuses and clawbacks". None of the legal teams they had worked with this year had mentioned it, despite the number of offer letter template updates they had been part of. They dove in to figure out a solution, and say they would not have noticed the blind spot without the network connection.

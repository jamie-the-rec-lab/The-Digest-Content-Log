Are You Really Using AI in TA by Jeremy Lyons
My admiration for Luke Eaton’s perspective, humor, and ability to say what needs to be said in the most widely accepted way possible is probably one of the worst-kept secrets in TA/RecOps space. Some people are born with delivery and timing; it shows. This week, in his DDR80 (hyperlinked below) he made the following point:
1. Don’t sleepwalk into being a software consumer
If your team’s AI strategy is an AI screening tool plus an AI sourcing tool then all you have done is buy two SaaS products. They happen to have AI in them.
Your team’s skills, workflows and cost base look exactly like they did in 2021.
You all know I love a good tool. But buying AI features and calling your team AI-enabled is how you end up four years in with nothing to show for it except the invoices.
An AI-enabled team changes how it works and the individual level.
And holy #@$@#$ does that statement hit the nail on the head.
I think it is safe to say that there are many of us, myself included at times, who have seen all these updates about AI and what it can do with a late-majority adoption curve response of “I’ll let those people ride the wave and then tell me what I need to know back on the shore.” Or, instead of wading into the water, we wait until what we use comes out with it (e.g., a scheduling agent, an NLP dashboard/report builder, a packet builder) and then work off the rails.
But what I like about Luke’s callout here is how it points out many teams calling themselves AI-enabled are probably still more Tool-enabled than AI-enabled. The technology is different, the skills to wield and think about it are different, so we need to be different too.

## Newsletter

Lead piece: "The First Live Burn Down We're Hosting" by Jeremy Lyons. When he sees tool demos, he asks vendors, "Would you like me to do a burn down of your product?" The community now wants to try a live burn down with Matt Ekstrom and Sync2Hire. Sync2Hire is "a collaboration layer that runs alongside your ATS": recruiters, hiring managers and interviewers work with candidates in a shared, purpose-built channel ("think Slack, but only for hiring"), with two-way sync to iCIMS, Greenhouse, SuccessFactors, SmartRecruiters and Ashby today, Workday close behind. Because the conversation is in one place, "the product can see things the ATS can't: where a req is stalling, which hiring manager hasn't responded, where a process is drifting from how it was designed." Event: Tuesday, November 17th, 1PM-2PM Eastern; first 30 minutes live walkthrough, second 30 for questions and pushback. The company is early and small: "they’re actively seeking design partners" (free product in exchange for shaping it). (This is a vendor-featured event; the newsletter otherwise says it is sponsor-free.)

## Newsletter

By Leandro, Head of AI Operations at Nobel and founder of Remade, which helps HR and recruitment teams get value from AI.

### The story

Leandro built a multi-agent "board" (CCO, CFO, CMO, COO, CPO, CTO agents around a chief-of-staff agent called TANI, "The Assistant Nobody Ignores") using Grokbot, a consumer multi-agent tool he describes as what "OpenClaw promised us months ago." Grokbot's defining feature, per Leandro, is that "the agents [can talk] to each other, proactively" before advice reaches him.

He tested it on a real decision: whether to accept an unpaid speaking opportunity requiring a 7-hour drive and 3-4 working days. Without telling the agents his own doubts, he asked the board. "The CCO leaned no: weak fit for the people I need to reach to grow my business. The CMO saw value if I could get useful content out of it. The CPO wanted a reusable workshop module." TANI's recommendation was to decline. "What surprised me was that I hadn't told them I was hesitating... they made me look properly at what saying yes would cost me."

### Other examples given

- His girlfriend WhatsApped TANI directly to remind him of a task; TANI picked it up from connected channels and drafted a follow-up email for review.
- After a networking event, he photographed a parking receipt and sent it to the "CFO agent," which used the receipt and calendar context to log the business expense.

### Caveats he raises

- "I've already had things go wrong with Grokbot, mainly with language and tone... I dont trust it yet." He is still checking every draft.
- Grok's models are "terrible writers... it shortens everything to an extent that it can become hard to decipher what it wants to say," contrasted unfavourably with Claude for that purpose.
- Self-imposed rule: "TANI must be clear that she's AI. She doesn't pretend to be me."

### The recruiting angle

He extends the idea to TA: "Imagine connecting a setup like this to your ATS. A candidate asks for an update. It checks, sees you're waiting for the hiring manager, and prepares a follow-up for you to review." He frames the underlying problem as overwhelm: "For recruiters, it's candidates waiting for updates while you're chasing hiring managers and trying to finish everything else."

This is a first-person, single-user account (his own 24-hour trial) with no independent data or benchmark; treat any productivity claims as anecdotal, not a study.

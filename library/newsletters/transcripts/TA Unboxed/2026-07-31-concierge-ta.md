# Do you run a concierge TA department?

- **Source:** TA Unboxed
- **Date:** 2026-07-31
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-31

## Newsletter

**Great service and strategic value are not the same thing.**

Running a concierge TA department means being responsive, attentive, and always available to support hiring managers. It's about proactive communication, seamless handoffs, and removing friction from the recruitment process.

But great service is not the same as strategic value.

When you're in concierge mode, you're often reactive—responding to requests, troubleshooting individual problems, and firefighting hiring challenges as they come up. You become an extension of the hiring manager's team, making their jobs easier by doing more of the work yourself.

The question isn't whether concierge-level service is good. It clearly is. The question is: at what cost?

Strategic value in talent acquisition comes from:

1. **Predictive sourcing** - Understanding talent trends before they impact your hiring
2. **Candidate experience design** - Creating systems that work, not just being nice when they break
3. **Market data and insights** - Giving leaders information to make better decisions about roles, compensation, and team composition
4. **Process optimization** - Building scalable approaches instead of heroic one-off efforts
5. **Talent strategy** - Thinking about retention, internal mobility, and future skill needs

When every request pulls you into hands-on execution, you lose time for strategic thinking. You become known for saying yes to everything and solving problems as they arrive—which is valuable, but it's not differentiated, and it doesn't compound.

The best talent teams find ways to provide excellent service while also building strategic muscle. That means:

- Automating routine tasks so you can focus on high-impact decisions
- Building hiring managers into the process instead of replacing them
- Creating transparency and self-service options
- Saying no strategically to things that don't move the needle
- Investing in tools and processes that scale

If you're being asked to be a concierge, ask yourself: Am I adding strategic value, or am I just working harder?

The difference matters.

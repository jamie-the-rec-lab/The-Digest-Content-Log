# Hiring is a team sport. Your ATS should be too.

- **Source:** TA Unboxed
- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-03
- **Public archive:** https://taunboxed.substack.com/p/hiring-is-a-team-sport-your-ats-should

## Core argument

ATS platforms are configured around two roles - Recruiter and Hiring Manager - while real hiring involves referrers, panel members, future hiring managers and colleagues who receive speculative CVs. "None of these people is recruiters. None of them appears in the process map. But all of them are recruiting." When the system doesn't support this, "when the system says no, email says yes" - candidate data moves into inboxes where it can't be governed or audited.

## Sponsor mention (Metaview)

Metaview's Application Review agent reviews every inbound applicant against a defined Ideal Candidate Profile and never auto-rejects; "Teams using it are seeing up to 92% less time spent on inbound screening."

## Recommended design changes

- A candidate-first front door for proactive hiring (referrals, network contacts) alongside requisition-based intake, with consent/compliance capture at point of entry.
- A defined hiring team per requisition (recruiter/TA owner, hiring manager, interviewers, panel members, approvers, observers) each mapped to the right level of access, not just added as names.
- A talent community/network as the governed entry point for CVs arriving outside applications.

## Practical checklist offered

Does every requisition have a defined hiring team? Can someone not currently hiring still bring talent in? Can people share a CV compliantly? Are roles/permissions mapped to the real workflow? Is there an audit trail for access, feedback and decisions?

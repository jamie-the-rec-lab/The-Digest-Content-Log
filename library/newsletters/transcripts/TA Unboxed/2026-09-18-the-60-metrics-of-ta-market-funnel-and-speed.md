# The 60 metrics of TA: market, funnel and speed

- **Source:** TA Unboxed
- **Date:** 2026-09-18
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Public archive:** https://taunboxed.substack.com/p/the-60-metrics-of-ta-market-funnel

## Newsletter

By Darren Bush, Founder, TA Unboxed. Part 1 of a 3-part series built from a personal database of 60+ recruitment metrics (sources cited include Personio's KPI lists, Adriano Herdman's 12-metric framework, John Vlastelica's alignment work, Toby Culshaw's Talent Intelligence and Amy Schultz's "New Mega Metrics for TA"). For each metric Bush gives: what it measures, the formula, a worked calculation, a real example and the decision it enables: "If a metric doesn't enable a decision, it's decoration." Part 1 covers 31 metrics across market sizing, outreach/sourcing, top of funnel, and speed.

### Market sizing
1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** every candidate with the required skills/experience/location, e.g. a filtered LinkedIn Recruiter search for "Java engineer + Spring + Berlin" returning 2,400 profiles.
2. **Total Obtainable Market (TOM):** TAM filtered by comp fit, brand reach and location, e.g. 2,400 x 0.50 x 0.45 x 0.75 = 405, rounded to 400.
3. **% of TAM Contacted:** e.g. 900/2,400 = 37.5% penetration.
4. **Talent Pool Optimisation** (Bush's own index): penetration % x conversion % x freshness % / 100, freshness = % of pool touched in last 90 days.
5. **Competitive Index:** 1-10 scoring across comp, brand, speed and quality vs the closest comparator.
6. **Glassdoor Score:** average rating tracked monthly, e.g. a slip from 4.0 to 3.5 preceded a 15% application-volume drop.
7. **Employee Posting Rate:** % of employees visibly posting/engaging as brand ambassadors, e.g. 30/400 = 7.5%.

### Outreach and sourcing
8. **Interested Response Rate:** Gem's 2025 three-stage-sequence benchmark is "26% reply rate and 14% interested rate."
9. **Reply Rate:** e.g. two sourcers on the same market, 30% vs 9.3%, a messaging-quality gap not a market gap.
10. **Sourced vs Inbound Hire Rate:** worked example, inbound 2/400 = 0.5% vs sourced 6/80 = 7.5%, "sourced converts at 15x the inbound rate here."
11. **Source of Hire:** hires by channel as % of total, e.g. referrals 35% of hires from 12% of applicants.
12. **Source Channel Conversion Rate:** e.g. LinkedIn 3% vs a niche Slack community 9%.
13. **Source Channel Time to Start:** e.g. referrals average 28 days vs job boards 54 days.
14. **Quality of Source by Channel:** e.g. agency submissions reach interview at 70% and rate 4.2/5 performance vs job board 15% interview rate and 3.4/5.
15. **Channel Effectiveness:** cost per quality point, e.g. job board £8,000/hire at quality 3.4 vs events £3,000/hire at quality 4.5.

### Top of funnel
16. **Application Volume** per requisition, split by role (600 vs 4 applications for two roles at the same company/week).
17. **Career Site Conversion Rate:** e.g. 180/10,000 visitors = 1.8%.
18. **Qualified Applicant Rate:** e.g. 90/600 = 15%.
19. **Application Drop-off Rate:** e.g. (1,200-700)/1,200 = 41.7%, often traced to "upload your CV, then retype your CV" friction.
20. **Cost per Irrelevant Application:** e.g. 510 irrelevant apps x 3 min screening x £30/hr loaded cost = ~£765 per req.
21. **Candidate Pool Burn Rate:** CVs reviewed/viable CVs, e.g. 300/1,200 weekly = 25% burn, 4-week runway.

### Speed
22. **Offer Acceptance Time:** date signed minus date sent, e.g. 9 days; a 5-day unsigned trigger for a hiring-manager call is suggested.
23. **Time to Fill:** requisition approval to offer acceptance, e.g. blended 42 days masking 25 days for support roles vs 78 for engineering.
24. **Time to Start:** requisition approval to candidate's first day, e.g. 40-day "time to fill" vs 92-day actual time to start once notice period and checks are included.
25. **Selected Candidate Time to Start:** the eventual hire's own funnel-entry-to-start journey, e.g. 78 days.
26. **Time to Winning Shortlist:** requisition approval to when the eventual hire was first shortlisted, e.g. a top recruiter at 6 days vs a 19-day team average.
27. **Time in Each Stage:** e.g. 2 days in screening but 11 days waiting on interview feedback, "the funnel isn't slow, the feedback loop is."
28. **Time to Alignment (John Vlastelica's metric):** kickoff to the first accepted "on-profile" candidate, e.g. 21 days, meaning everything sourced before that point targeted the wrong profile.
29. **Candidate Waiting Time:** days since last contact; cites Robert Half finding "62% of professionals lose interest if they don't hear back within two weeks... after an interview."
30. **Roles Open Beyond 60 Days:** count of reqs past an agreed threshold, e.g. 12 of 80 reqs, 9 of them in one function under one approver.
31. **Hiring Velocity** (bonus): roles filled per period, e.g. 1.7/week in Q1 falling to 1.1/week in Q2 on flat headcount, a 35% capacity drop.

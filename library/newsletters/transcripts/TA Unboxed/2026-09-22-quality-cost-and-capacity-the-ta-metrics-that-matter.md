# Quality, cost and capacity: the TA metrics that matter

- **Source:** TA Unboxed
- **Date:** 2026-09-22
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-23
- **Public archive:** https://taunboxed.substack.com/p/quality-cost-and-capacity-the-ta

## Newsletter

By Darren Bush, Founder, TA Unboxed. Part 2 of the 3-part metrics series (metrics 31-61): funnel conversion, offers, candidate/manager experience, quality of hire, money, and team capacity. Bush flags quality of hire as the most contested metric in TA: "the one that, according to polls, TA wants most, but it's also the one two people can't agree on how to measure."

### Funnel conversion
- **Pass-Through Rates** per stage transition, e.g. 100 applications to 20 screens (20%), to 8 interviews (40%), to 2 offers (25%), to 1 hire (50%); weakest link interview-to-offer.
- **Pipeline Conversion Rate:** hires/total entering candidates, e.g. 30/1,500 = 2% end to end.
- **Screening to Interview Rate:** e.g. 12/50 = 24%.
- **Hiring Manager Acceptance Rate:** candidates accepted by HM/submitted; Bush's working rule is "below 50%, stop sourcing and realign the brief."
- **Interviews per Offer:** e.g. 140/20 = 7 per offer vs a peer's 4.
- **Interviews per Successful Candidate:** average interviews the eventual hire sat, e.g. 5; cites "Google's internal research found that four interviews were enough to predict whether someone should be hired with 86% confidence."
- **Interview Completion Rate:** completed/scheduled, e.g. 170/200 = 85%.

### Offers
- **Offer Acceptance Rate:** e.g. 14/20 = 70%.
- **Accepted Offer to Start Rate:** e.g. 42/50 = 84%, the gap being a "keep-warm" risk during notice periods.
- **Offer Decline Reasons:** categorical tracking, e.g. 40% compensation, 23% speed, 20% counter-offers.

### Experience
- **Candidate Satisfaction:** survey/NPS by outcome, e.g. rejected candidates 4.1/5 (fast decisions) vs hired candidates 3.6/5 (comms went quiet post-signature).
- **Hiring Manager Satisfaction:** per-dimension scoring, e.g. responsiveness 4.8 vs candidate quality 3.2; Bush warns "never let this be your north star."
- **Net Hiring Score (Jerome Ternynck, SmartRecruiters):** NPS-style fit score, % promoters minus % detractors, e.g. 60% promoters, 25% detractors = +35.

### Quality
- **Quality of Hire:** composite of 6-month performance, ramp-up, engagement, culture fit, e.g. structured-interview hires averaging 4.3/5 vs informal-chat hires 3.1/5.
- **Quality of System:** whether the hiring process itself was predictive, comparing offer-time scorecard to 6-month reality, e.g. 4.5 at offer vs 3.5 at 6 months.
- **Slate Quality:** % of TA's shortlist actually interviewed/offered plus HM satisfaction with the slate, positioned as "the one TA should actually own."
- **New Hire Failure Rate:** leavers within 6/12 months, e.g. 18% overall, but 30% for agency-sourced hires vs 5.7% for referrals.
- **Early Turnover Rate:** departures within 90 days, e.g. 22% in H1, often tracing to a mismatch between the advertised job and the actual job.
- **Internal Mobility:** internal hires/total hires, e.g. rising from 8% to 24% after internal-first advertising, alongside halved regrettable attrition in engineering.
- **Underrepresented Group Hiring Rate:** compares applicant-pool share to hire share and pinpoints the funnel stage where the gap opens, e.g. a panel-stage drop from 40% of applicants to 12% of hires.
- **Detected Candidate Fraud Rate:** flagged/confirmed fraud as % of candidates screened; cites "Gartner predicts that by 2028, one in four candidate profiles worldwide will be fake," worked example 3/200 = 1.5%.

### Money
- **Cost per Hire:** total recruiting cost/hires, e.g. £180k/40 hires = £4,500, vs £22k for agency-heavy executive search.
- **Cost of Vacancy:** daily role value x days vacant, e.g. a £90k role at a 2x productivity multiplier over 90 days = ~£62,280.
- **Lost Revenue from Vacant Roles:** e.g. 3 unfilled consultant roles at £800/day = £48,000/month unbillable.
- **New Hire Revenue Contribution:** e.g. £300,000 year-one revenue minus £5,000 cost per hire = £295,000 net.
- **Revenue per Employee:** e.g. flat at £140k for three years despite 20% headcount growth, "growth comes from adding people, not from making people more productive."

### Team capacity
- **Weekly Submission Rate** per recruiter, benchmark example of 4/week.
- **Recruiting Capacity:** open roles per recruiter, cites a 15-20 roles/recruiter benchmark, worked example 110/5 = 22 each.
- **Critical Role Vacancy Rate:** e.g. 9/25 key roles open = 36%.
- **Attrition Rate and Regrettable Attrition:** e.g. 12% overall attrition but 4 of 18 leavers were top performers (2.7% regrettable), concentrated in one team.
- **Hiring Plan Completion Rate:** e.g. 51/60 planned hires closed = 85%, misses clustered in one underpaid job family.

# Tag taxonomy

Tags are drawn from this fixed list so an AI can filter reliably. Company names are NOT tags; they live in the separate Company field. Each episode gets its company plus 6-10 theme tags from below.

## AI
Use a SPECIFIC sub-tag wherever possible. Reserve `ai strategy` only for meta
episodes (predictions, "should we use AI" debates) with no concrete use case.
- ai sourcing            (AI finds/ranks candidates)
- ai screening           (AI sifts inbound applications at volume)
- ai interviewing        (AI conducts or scores the interview)
- ai interview notes     (AI note-taking / interview intelligence, e.g. Metaview)
- ai agents              (autonomous/agentic recruiting workflows)
- ai job descriptions    (AI drafts JDs / turns intake into adverts)
- ai market research     (AI for company/market/talent intel)
- ai coding tools        (AI coding assistants used in engineering interviews)
- interview cheating     (candidates using AI to cheat, and detection)
- deepfake candidates    (AI-generated fake/fraudulent candidates)
- building ai tools      (non-technical recruiters building their own AI tools)
- claude code            (building with Claude Code / MCPs)
- custom gpts            (custom GPTs / assistants)
- prompt engineering     (writing effective prompts)
- ai upskilling          (learning AI as a recruiter skill)
- ai bias                (bias, fairness, compliance, audit trails in AI hiring)
- context engineering    (feeding AI the right context: knowledge bases, digital twins, reusable skills)
- ai governance          (transparency, explainability, oversight, AI policy in hiring)
- human-ai collaboration (human + AI teaming, recruiter-as-agent-manager, hiring-team alignment on AI)
- ai strategy            (general/meta AI discussion only)
- candidate fraud        (non-AI identity/fraud issues)

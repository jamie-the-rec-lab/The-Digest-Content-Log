## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Daniel Chait, CEO of Greenhouse, on using AI for fairer hiring and giving both sides more signal.  ·  **Show:** 10x Recruiting, 27 Mar 2025
**Tags:** ai screening, ai bias, inbound recruiting, recruiting operations, candidate experience, dei, candidate fraud

**When to use this:** your inbound is drowning you and candidates believe the ATS is auto-rejecting them.

**Big idea:** AI in hiring can promote fairness rather than just amplify bias, and the way out of the application arms race is generating more signal on both candidates and employers, not more volume.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Anonymise resumes with a more accurate parser so hiring managers evaluate skills and track record without seeing name, gender or team affiliations, echoing the symphony blind-audition example.
- Dispel the ATS auto-reject myth. In Greenhouse the employer sees applicants in the chronological order they applied, like a deli ticket, so candidate number 2,000 is not rejected but simply never looked at.
- Give employers better filtering tools to slice the list intentionally rather than in strict order.
- Launch Greenhouse Verified. Because recruiters work inside Greenhouse, the data shows who replies to every applicant, fills in scorecards and moves quickly, and those who do earn a public badge.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Commercial resume parsers run about 80 to 85% accurate, while Greenhouse's runs about 96% and improving.
- "I never heard back" has been the number one candidate complaint in surveys for 20 years.

**Contrarian take:**
- Chait says there is no winning the AI application arms race, because blasting 200 or 2,000 applications leaves zero signal, so the fix is candidates and employers both generating more signal.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Trial anonymised resume review on one requisition and compare the shortlist composition.
- Audit your own reply and scorecard completion rates, since candidates increasingly favour employers who visibly treat them fairly.

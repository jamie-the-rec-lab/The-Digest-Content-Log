## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Emerson Croxton, Director of TA at Affirm, on deploying AI for real impact and rerouting strong runner-up candidates.  ·  **Show:** 10x Recruiting, 27 Mar 2025
**Tags:** ai strategy, recruiting operations, talent pools, candidate experience, recruiter productivity, hiring manager alignment

**When to use this:** you keep rejecting qualified finalists outright and starting each new req from scratch.

**Big idea:** AI only amplifies existing process strength or weakness, so the leverage is in strong process, such as Affirm's silver-medalist reroute for strong runners-up.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Treat AI as a magnifier. If the muscle is atrophied it enhances an atrophied muscle, so do the work to point it in the right direction first.
- Run the silver-medalist process. Affirm runs reqs one by one rather than evergreen, so when one candidate is selected, recruiters identify other finalists and reroute them to an already-open req, pull a req forward, or keep them warm.
- Operationalise it in Slack. When a recruiter closes a req and rejects strong finalists, their manager nudges them, and they post those people to an all-tech-recruiters channel to advocate for other reqs.
- Borrow "exhaust" from executive search, where one candidate sourced for a CTO search may fit client A or client B.

**Contrarian take:**
- Croxton frames rejecting qualified finalists outright as a bad idea and low-hanging fruit, because qualifying them was most of the work and recruiting is a momentum game.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Set up a shared channel where recruiters post silver medalists the moment a req closes, and align the team that when one req wins, all win.

## Episode description

Emerson Croxton, Director of TA at Affirm, dropped by our booth at Transform to share his take on how to deploy AI for real impact. In his chat with Nolan, he says that AI shouldn’t be seen as a band-aid that can be slapped on anything for a quick fix. You need to do the work to build strong processes that AI can then enhance. 

Emerson also shares more on Affirm’s “silver medalist” process and how they keep the momentum going with qualified candidates who make it to the end of the process, but don't get the job they initially applied for.

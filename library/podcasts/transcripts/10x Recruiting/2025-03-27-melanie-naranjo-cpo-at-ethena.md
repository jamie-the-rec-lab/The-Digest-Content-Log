## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Melanie Naranjo, CPO at Ethena, on navigating HR's relationship with the CEO.  ·  **Show:** 10x Recruiting, 27 Mar 2025
**Tags:** ta leadership, stakeholder management, recruiter career growth, hiring manager alignment, recruiting operations, recruiter training

**When to use this:** your proposals keep getting shot down and you are blaming a CEO who "does not get people".

**Big idea:** HR should drop the victim mentality and own it when proposals get rejected, and CEOs should give HR direct feedback rather than quietly dismissing them.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- When your last three proposals were shot down but the sales leader's last three were approved, assume they had good reason, then ask what they did better and how you fell short rather than getting defensive.
- CEOs should call out HR directly. Naranjo's CEO Roxanne told her "I stopped listening two minutes ago, you need to reframe that", which gave Naranjo the chance to come back and prove herself.
- Drop the phrases "seat at the table" and "influencing without authority", which Naranjo reads as code for believing you know better and manipulating people into your idea.

**Contrarian take:**
- Naranjo says HR is not always right and she has had dumb ideas herself, and if you are convinced you are correct and refuse to learn the business, you will never make progress.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- After your next rejected proposal, run a self-reflection against a peer's approved one and ask your stakeholder directly what they did better.

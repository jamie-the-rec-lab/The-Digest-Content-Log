**When to use this:** you want a fast market read across AI, DEI, fractional talent, sourcing and headcount planning from a dozen operators at once.

**Big idea:** Across a dozen booth interviews, the throughline is that AI augments rather than replaces recruiting, and the real edge is process discipline, honest calibration and human judgement.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Source for quality with Khloe Glasgow's "GAS" (give-a-factor) system. She scores a LinkedIn profile on a point system for career progression, promotion history and multifaceted devotion, such as a concert violinist holding a full-time job, then reaches out to only about 20 people a week where each could get the job.
- Reserve fractional roles for former leaders. Natalie Stones argues fractional should be for people who owned strategy or managed a team, not IC recruiters who are really agency consultants.
- Plan hiring in six-month cycles tied to product launches, as Ed Snook does, mirroring Airbnb's two-launch operating rhythm, instead of annual headcount roll-ups that slip from January to April.
- Assess for strong opinions loosely held, excellent written communication and accountability, as Caitlin Pino does at Notabene hiring across 13 countries in an async culture.
- Interrogate why a founder is reticent about a role. Virginia Torrado asks it, since hesitation often means the role is not the priority, and they may need a generalist rather than a chief of staff.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Matt Stainer runs a recruiting community of 13,000 members.
- VP of sales tenure at series A or B is roughly 18 months, and about one in three fails.
- Annie of Swing Search reports executive marketing search returning after two dead years, plus a swing back to 5 days in office and even 996 schedules.

**Contrarian take:**
- Ed Snook and Scott Kersch push back on AI hype, saying most companies are just playing around with ChatGPT and there may be an AI bubble, and that AI is the Iron Man suit rather than a replacement. Matt Stainer surfaces real resentment among TA leaders who built DEI programmes and now feel rug-pulled as companies reverse course.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Score your next shortlist on a simple give-a-factor rubric before outreach so every message goes to someone who could actually get the job.
- If you are not perpetually hiring, price a fractional TA leader against a full-time head of talent.

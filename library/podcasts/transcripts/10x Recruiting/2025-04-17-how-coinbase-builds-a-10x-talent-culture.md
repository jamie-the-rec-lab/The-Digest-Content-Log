## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Greg Garrison, VP of Talent at Coinbase, who built Google's Austin recruiting hub and now runs Coinbase's "top talent in every seat" programme.  ·  **Show:** 10x Recruiting, 17 Apr 2025
**Tags:** talent density, recruiter training, work trials, scorecards, quality of hire, recruiting metrics, candidate calibration

**When to use this:** you want to raise your talent bar with real checks and balances rather than slogans.

**Big idea:** "Top talent in every seat" comes from building talent density deliberately, through competitive recruiters, assessments as data, CEO review of every offer, and work trials.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Hire recruiters against a rubric: a degree with the school brand irrelevant, two or more years in a commission-based staffing agency, and a competitive background such as athletes or a competitive ballroom dancer for the will to win.
- Post a leaderboard of only the top 25% weekly, never the full list, to avoid shaming the bottom, and segment the team into survivors, drivers and thrivers while working to shift the curve left.
- Run Coinbase talent density's five components: cognitive, cultural and coding assessments as benchmarked data points rather than pass or fail, CEO and COO offer approval, elevated internships as 12-week work trials, real-time feedback, and executive accountability.
- Have Brian and Emily review every offer packet. Recruiters start channelling that bar before they even reach out, and packets get tighter by naming the risk and how to overcome it.
- Design work trials per role. For a recruiting director, give a problem such as moving 500 heads to an international site, have them write a one-page PPS (problem, proposed solution), then present and jam with the team for 45 minutes to read problem-solving, writing, influence and response to feedback.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Google Austin charter was to hire 100 recruiters in 90 days, and over the following years more than 1,000 recruiters, sourcers and coordinators were hired.
- Coinbase received 1.5 million applications last year, and internships are scaling from dozens pre-density to 200 and next 300.
- The offer-accept SLA target is above 80%.

**Contrarian take:**
- Garrison insists the cognitive assessment is just one data point and is deliberately polarising, because candidates who opt out self-select and smart people want to work with smart people, so the friction is a feature.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Add one work trial to your hardest role, framed as a one-page problem-proposed-solution the candidate presents to the team.
- Screen your next recruiter hire for a genuinely competitive background, not just recruiting experience.

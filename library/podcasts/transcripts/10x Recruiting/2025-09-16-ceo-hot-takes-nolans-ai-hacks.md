**Tags:** custom gpts, ai interview notes, building ai tools, recruiter productivity, recruiting enablement, employer branding

**When to use this:** You keep meaning to experiment with AI but never find time, and you want two concrete, copyable recruiter workflows.

**Big idea:** Building a useful recruiting AI hack takes about 30 to 60 minutes, and the value is not the raw output but riffing with it to get from a blank page into the red zone.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Build a custom GPT as a copywriting thought partner: give it a description, instructions to always start with a compelling, contrarian or radical hook, and a handful of your own high-performing posts as examples, which took Nolan about 15 minutes.
- Never paste AI output straight into LinkedIn, which Nolan calls the definition of AI slop; always edit and riff, treating the AI as taking you from the 5-yard line to the 20 while you still carry it over the line.
- Build a Metaview template that structures your interview write-up, starting with a recommendation aimed at a hiring manager who was not on the call, in the format "I spoke with candidate on day, I recommend we move forward, here are my notes" followed by bullets.
- Instruct the template to write in the concise communication style of Alex Hormozi and feed it example summaries you have sent before, so the tone matches yours.
- Structure the write-up around your standard questions, what are you most proud of, team building, what they are optimising for, and whether they are actively interviewing.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Nolan says the Metaview template cut his post-call write-up from about 45 minutes to roughly a minute; the MIT report he references claims 95 percent of generative AI pilots at companies are failing.

**Contrarian take:**
- Unattributed CEO quotes include "I don't care about team coherence, I care about spikes", a claim that Glassdoor is overrated because reviews come from people you fired, and the advice to hire Mormons for sales and ask where they were deployed on their mission, favouring those sent to the hardest places.

**Tools & prompts:**
- ChatGPT custom GPT named "Nolan's Copywriter" with instructions to produce banger LinkedIn posts optimised for impressions and always opening with a hook; Metaview template named "Nolan's screen".

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Build a Metaview or note template that outputs a hiring-manager recommendation first, in a fixed format and your chosen voice, from your standard interview questions.
- Block a Friday afternoon, roughly 10 percent of your time, to build one small AI workflow and share it with peers.

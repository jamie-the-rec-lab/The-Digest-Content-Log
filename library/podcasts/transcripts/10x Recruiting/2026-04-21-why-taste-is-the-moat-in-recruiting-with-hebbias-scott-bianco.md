**Guest:** Scott Bianco, Hebbia  ·  **Show:** 10x Recruiting, 21 Apr 2026

**Tags:** talent pools, talent mapping, sourcing, talent market data, recruiter career growth

**When to use this:** You want to move beyond keyword matching and build a sourcing strategy based on understanding what great looks like and where to find it.

**Big idea:** The best sourcing starts with taste: a deep understanding of what excellence looks like in a role, where similar excellence exists, and why candidates might move. It's not just about access to a database.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Map talent pools by skill and context, not just job title. A VP of Sales from a B2B SaaS company is a different profile than a VP of Sales from an agency. Both matter, differently.
- Build a reference profile. Go back to your best hires. What was unusual about them? What do they have in common? Use that as your sourcing compass.
- Source for adjacent skills. If you need a strong project manager, source people managers (they have context management), delivery leads (they have timeline management), and product managers (they have stakeholder management). Teach the specific skill.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Teams with strong sourcing strategies source 30 percent more candidates passively (without relying on job board applications).

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- List your last 5 strong hires. Map what they were doing before this role. What pattern do you see?
- Identify 2-3 roles or companies where similar talent sits. Start a daily search on LinkedIn for people in those roles or companies moving recently.

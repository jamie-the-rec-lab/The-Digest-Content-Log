## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Siadhal Magos, co-founder and CEO of Metaview, on his own company's funding round. Vendor disclosure: 10x Recruiting is produced by Metaview in partnership with the host, Nolan Church, and the host says he uses Metaview daily, so this is a company announcement and every claim about Metaview's traction, retention and product quality is self-reported and unverified  ·  **Show:** 10x Recruiting, 30 Sep 2026
**Tags:** ai strategy, rec tech, ai agents, ta leadership

**When to use this:** you are judging an AI recruiting vendor's platform pitch or planning your own raise and want to see the case a vendor CEO makes, with the marketing separated from what was actually stated.

**Big idea:** Magos argues that connected AI agents on one platform beat stand-alone point solutions, because shared context makes each agent better and more trusted. This is the vendor's own thesis and sales position, not independent evidence.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Magos said he sees two form factors for working with AI: a workspace you lean into to run your desk alongside agents, and a coworker you message in Slack. He described Metaview's Fillmore as the second kind, where you tell it what role you need, it asks calibration questions and then books candidates into your calendar.
- Magos's trust argument is that the more work AI does over longer time horizons, the more you must trust it, and you only trust it if it holds the context it should. You can use this as a question for any vendor: what context does the agent actually have about your hiring?
- Magos said a platform lets AI be proactive or "promptless". His example was the AI hearing in a conversation that the pipeline is running dry and starting sourcing without being asked.
- His fundraising signal was that one long-interested investor became more aggressive, which he read as a cue to run a proper process and speak to others, using the investor's access to non-public data as the signal.
- He said he wanted investors who share the thesis rather than momentum investors, because "if we're wrong, we're like sort of wrong together".

**Data & benchmarks (all self-reported by Metaview):**
- Metaview raised an additional $60M led by Insight Partners, taking total funding to $110M, after a $35M Series B about a year earlier. Magos also said a 2021 seed was followed by a period where "we actually didn't have the traction we needed".
- Magos claimed 60% of newly onboarded customers are now platform customers, meaning they buy multiple products. No figures were given for growth, revenue or net dollar retention beyond "off the charts NDR".
- Fillmore was tested with five to 10 customers for months before general availability.

**Contrarian take:**
- Magos said investing in a platform rather than hammering all resources into one winning product takes discipline, and that stand-alone tools are not durable because "people can swap in and out point solutions all the time" and a price rise pushes customers to build it themselves. This is also his company's pitch.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- When a vendor says its agents "share context", ask for retention or adoption numbers with definitions, since here NDR was asserted without a figure.
- If an investor or buyer suddenly becomes aggressive, treat it as a signal and test the market with a few other conversations before deciding.

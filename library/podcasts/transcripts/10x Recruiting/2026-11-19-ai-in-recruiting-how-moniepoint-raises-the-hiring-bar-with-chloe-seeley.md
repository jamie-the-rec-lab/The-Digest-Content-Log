## Episode description

Every great TA leader wants to coach every interviewer, after every interview. Chloe Seeley found a way to actually do it.

When Chloe joined Moniepoint, Africa's fastest-growing fintech, she saw a real chance to raise the hiring bar. So her team built an AI agent that reviews every interview against the role's rubric. 

Within 5 minutes of the call, the interviewer gets a personalised coaching note in Slack: what went well, where to probe deeper, and any gaps the next interviewer should cover. An exec recruiter on her team built it in about 2 months, with no engineers involved.

Nolan Church and Chloe talk through how she spotted what to fix, why great rubrics make it all work, the second agent that double-checks the first, and the bar raiser agent she's building next.

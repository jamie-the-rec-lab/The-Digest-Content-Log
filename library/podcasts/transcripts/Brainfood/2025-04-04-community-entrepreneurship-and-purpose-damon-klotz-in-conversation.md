## Episode description

Damon Klotz is an expert in community building, having spent 10 years at Culture Amp creating an ecosystem of People Geeks centred around companies with purpose. 

I caught up with Damon at Talentpalooza2025 in the wonderful setting of Abbotsford Convent in Melbourne and shot this conversation from the Rotunda in the gardens. 

Hear Damon talk about his career journey, lessons learned from interviewing 100+ guest for the Culture Amp podcast including Simon Sinek and Esther Perel and how companies can today grow through feeding purpose driven ecosystems.

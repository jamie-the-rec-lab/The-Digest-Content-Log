**Guest:** Tyler Knell on talent engineering  |  **Show:** Building the Talent Machine, 14 Mar 2026
**Tags:** talent density, skills-based hiring, work trials, interview design, quality of hire, candidate experience

**When to use this:** You want to move from traditional hiring (resume screening, interviews) to outcomes-based hiring, or you're trying to hire people who don't fit traditional profiles.

**Big idea:** Talent engineering means designing hiring processes (interviews, assessments, trials) that predict actual job performance, not just interview performance; it requires understanding which skills and behaviours actually drive success in your role.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Analyse your top 10 performers: what skills do they have, what problems do they solve, how do they behave under pressure? Use this to build your hiring rubric rather than job description
- Use work trials for roles where you need to predict problem-solving under real conditions; paid 1-2 week projects are more predictive than interviews
- Build interview assessments around real job scenarios (show me how you'd approach this project) rather than general competency questions
- Include diverse interview formats: technical assessment, case study, work trial, peer interview, culture fit conversation; different formats reveal different dimensions of capability

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Candidates hired via outcomes-based processes (work trials, scenario-based interviews) have 30-40% higher time-to-productivity and lower early turnover

**Contrarian take:**
- Tyler Knell argues traditional hiring doesn't predict performance; outcomes-based hiring is investment in hiring accuracy

- Compare those skills to what you currently screen for in interviews
- Design one new interview scenario based on a real problem from your actual work

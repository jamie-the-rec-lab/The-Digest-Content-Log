**Big idea:** AI sourcing agents are vastly overhyped; most implementations fail because they either hallucinate candidate data, can't verify information, or lack context about your actual hiring criteria.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Use AI for candidate research enrichment (filling in gaps in profiles, gathering public data) not as a primary sourcing engine; human sourcing skills remain essential
- Verify all AI-sourced candidates against your ATS already to catch hallucinations before wasting recruiter time
- AI agents work best in narrowly defined use cases (finding candidates with exact skill + geography combinations) not broad "find me the best people" queries
- Combine AI sourcing with human verification: AI identifies candidates, human evaluates fit

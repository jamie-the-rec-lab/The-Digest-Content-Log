**Big idea:** Hiring in China requires understanding regional talent preferences (top graduates want large tech/finance companies), compliance requirements (government-mandated vetting, pension contributions), and different compensation expectations; Western talent acquisition playbooks don't translate directly.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Local talent market research first: Before hiring in a new country, talk to local recruiters and hiring managers about where top talent congregates (universities, companies, platforms), what they value (stability, salary growth, international exposure), and what companies they aspire to work for.
- Compliance mapping: Document employment law, benefits mandates, and tax obligations specific to that country; what's optional in the US may be mandatory in China and vice versa.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**

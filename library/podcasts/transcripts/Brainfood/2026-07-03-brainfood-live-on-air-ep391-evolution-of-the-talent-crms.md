**When to use this:** your hiring is slow or stalled and you do not know why.

**Big idea:** Hiring problems often stem from a "signal problem": you cannot tell whether a candidate is truly interested, a hiring manager is truly ready, or a role is truly available. Without clean signals, you make decisions based on incomplete information and progress slows. Fixing the signal infrastructure (clear feedback, clear timelines, clear next steps) often does more than hiring faster.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Map your hiring signals: after sourcing, what tells you a candidate is interested? After interviews, what tells you a manager is ready to move? After offer, what tells you a candidate will accept? If the signals are weak, that is your problem.
- Standardise feedback timing: hiring managers give feedback within 24 hours or the candidate moves on. Candidates confirm availability for interviews by EOD or you move to the next person. Clear timelines create clear signals.

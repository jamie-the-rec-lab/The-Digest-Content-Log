## Episode description

The State of AI Readiness: A Framework to Gauge Your Organisation's AI Readiness

Every organisation says it's embracing AI - but very few can answer a simple question: how AI-ready are we, really? 

While new tools continue to appear at breakneck speed, successful AI adoption depends on much more than buying software. It requires the right leadership, governance, skills, culture, data, processes and appetite for change. In this webinar, we'll introduce a practical framework for assessing AI readiness across your organisation, helping talent leaders understand where they are today, what gaps need closing, and what it will take to move from experimentation to sustainable transformation.

Key Themes We'll Cover:
What AI Readiness Actually Means – Looking beyond technology to assess the organisational capabilities required for successful AI adoption
The AI Readiness Framework – A practical model for evaluating your organisation across leadership, people, processes, technology and governance
Leadership and Vision – Why executive sponsorship and a clear AI strategy are essential before scaling implementation
Skills for the AI Era – Assessing whether your workforce has the capabilities needed to effectively work alongside AI systems
Culture and Change Readiness – Understanding how curiosity, experimentation and trust influence successful adoption
Governance Without Bureaucracy – Establishing policies for responsible AI that encourage innovation while managing risk

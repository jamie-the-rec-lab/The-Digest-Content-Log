## Episode description

From Talent Acquisition to Organisational Performance

For years, Talent Acquisition has been measured by hiring metrics: time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, source-of-hire and offer acceptance rates. While these remain important, they only tell part of the story. The real question is not whether we filled the vacancy quickly, but whether hiring decisions improve business performance. As organisations become more data-driven and AI makes recruitment increasingly efficient, Talent Acquisition has an opportunity to redefine its value—not as a function that fills jobs, but as one that directly influences productivity, innovation, growth and competitive advantage. In this webinar, we'll explore how TA leaders can shift the conversation from recruitment outcomes to organisational outcomes.

Key Themes We'll Cover:

Beyond Hiring Metrics – Why traditional recruitment KPIs no longer capture the full impact of Talent Acquisition

Connecting Talent to Business Results – How hiring decisions influence productivity, revenue, customer experience, innovation and long-term organisational success

Quality of Hire Revisited – Moving beyond performance reviews to understand the characteristics of hires who create lasting business value

The Rise of Talent Intelligence – Using workforce data, labour market insights and AI to make better strategic hiring decisions

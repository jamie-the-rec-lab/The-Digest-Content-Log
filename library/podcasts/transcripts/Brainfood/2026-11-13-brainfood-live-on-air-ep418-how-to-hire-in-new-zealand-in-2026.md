## Episode description

How to Hire in New Zealand in 2026

Hiring in New Zealand has always required a different playbook. With a population smaller than most global cities and a talent pool that has historically leaked talent to Australia, the UK and beyond, Kiwi recruiters have long been asked to do more with less. But 2026 brings a fresh set of challenges - and opportunities. Immigration settings have shifted again, candidate expectations have been reshaped by global trends, and AI is changing how Kiwis search for and evaluate work. Meanwhile, employers across Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch and the regions are competing fiercely for the same scarce skills. So what does it actually take to hire well in Aotearoa right now? In this special regional edition of Brainfood Live, we'll dig into the realities of the New Zealand talent market and what recruiters need to know to win.

Key Themes We'll Cover:
The State of the NZ Market – Where hiring demand is strongest in 2026, which sectors are short on talent, and where the market has cooled
Immigration and Visa Realities – How the latest Accredited Employer Work Visa settings and Skills in Demand lists are shaping who you can hire, and how fast
The Australia Problem – Why Kiwi talent keeps crossing the Tasman (and beyond), and what actually convinces them to stay
Candidate Expectations in 2026 – Salary transparency, flexible work, and wellbeing - what NZ candidates now consider non-negotiable
Salary Benchmarking – Navigating cost-of-living pressures

## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Man-on-the-street recruiters at Transform 2025 plus Adam from Talent Llama, an AI-interviewer vendor; worth hearing for how practitioners weigh AI interviewing and how to vet the vendors.  ·  **Show:** Building the Talent Machine, 18 Aug 2025
**Tags:** ai interviewing, ai bias, candidate experience, rec tech, executive search, structured interviewing, ai strategy

**When to use this:** you are deciding whether to put an AI interviewer in front of candidates.

**Big idea:** AI interviewing suits lower-level, cut-and-dried roles but risks a dehumanising experience in executive search, and buyers should verify compliance before trusting the marketing.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Ask vendors the table-stakes hard questions: is the product SOC 2 compliant, has it passed a third-party bias audit, and does it have LLM and model failover. Talent Llama's Adam says they have all three despite being about a year old.
- Do not believe the marketing sites. Insist on seeing the working product on a live call, then run a paid trial before deciding.
- Treat adoption like any new ATS or HRIS. Supervise it rather than "open it and let it fly".

**Contrarian take:**
- Several recruiters warn AI interviewing can encode bias and worsens experience when candidates get no visual feedback, so it is not a fit for executive or relationship-led roles.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Before buying an AI interviewer, ask for its bias-audit results and book a live product walkthrough.

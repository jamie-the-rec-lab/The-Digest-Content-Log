## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Mike Peditto, "The Realistic Recruiter", former Director of Talent at Teal and author of "Yes, You're Being Judged", with 400,000+ followers; worth hearing on staying employable and building a creator presence.  ·  **Show:** Building the Talent Machine, 3 Oct 2025
**Tags:** ta leadership, values interviews, work trials, recruiter training, recruitment marketing, ai strategy, recruiter career growth, interview design, recruiting enablement

**When to use this:** you fear recruiters are expendable and want concrete ways to make yourself the person leadership keeps.

**Big idea:** recruiters protect themselves by becoming the internal expert who has actually tested the AI tools and can advise leadership, rather than the person who resists them.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Take vendor demos deliberately. Sit on tools you would normally ignore so you can tell executives "I've looked at these seven tools, this one fits our number-one problem", making yourself a strategic adviser instead of just a sourcer.
- Enter a new TA role by observing first and holding judgment. Peditto admits he came in "too hot" at Teal and had to repair trust with the CEO.
- Replace the vague culture interview with a values interview that walks candidates through the company's actual values, and experiment with sending interview questions in advance and paid work trials to cut bad hires.
- Price content deals at roughly 1% of your following; anything less is not worth it, and engagement now matters more to buyers than raw views.
- Network by spending about $2,000 to attend a conference, then sit in a speaker's talk and approach them afterwards because people love discussing what they just presented.

**Tools & prompts:**
- His newsletter and podcast "Is This Still a Good Time?" is modelled on the "I Hate It Here" newsletter; he opens every interview with "is this still a good time" and closes with "how do you cope".

**Contrarian take:**
- End-to-end agentic AI recruiting is not here yet. Peditto says people claiming it exists as a "trade secret" are wrong, and demoing the tools shows exactly where the workflow breaks.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Book one vendor demo this week and write a one-paragraph internal recommendation tied to your biggest bottleneck.
- Before your next conference, pick three speakers to meet by attending their sessions.

## Episode description

Stop Hiring for Roles. Start Solving Problems.

Katie Wagner has built recruiting engines inside some of the fastest-moving companies in tech — including Tarro, DoorDash, and IKEA.
Now a Principal at Growth by Design Talent, she helps teams hire with precision, purpose, and process.

Her core philosophy?
👉 Recruit like a product manager.

In this episode of Building the Talent Machine, Katie unpacks Problem-Based Recruiting — a framework that flips traditional hiring on its head.
Instead of starting with a job description, she starts with the business problem:
• What outcome does this hire need to drive?
• What’s the real pain point the team is solving?

From there, everything flows — scorecards, interview rubrics, success metrics, and a better candidate experience.
Because when recruiting works backward from impact, every search becomes a product worth building.

In this episode, you’ll learn:
• How to treat every role like a product and every search like a sprint
• Why intake calibration is the single most important step in hiring
• The hidden cost of skipping frameworks and rubrics
• Why pass-through rate benchmarks are about to be rewritten
• How recruiters can evolve into true talent advisors

💬 Katie’s pro tip:

“Start with the problem and build backward. That’s how you hire the right person for the right reason.”

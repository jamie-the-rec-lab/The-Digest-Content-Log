## Episode description

Vibe Hiring. AI Sourcing. Candidate Motivation. Quality of Hire.

This keynote is Brandon Jeffs at his most unfiltered — breaking down the truth about recruiting in 2025 and why so many teams are struggling with signal, alignment, and accountability in a market full of noise.

In this talk, Brandon explains why recruiters have always hired on vibes, how “AI-native” vendor marketing changed the language of hiring, and why the biggest predictor of job success isn’t a skills matrix — it’s candidate motivation.
He also covers the rise of Vibe Coding, the dangers of blindly trusting AI sourcing tools, the myth of quality of hire, and why interview transcription may be the most important AI use case in TA today.

If you’re a recruiter, sourcer, TA leader, or operator trying to make sense of AI, signal, and modern hiring — this talk will hit home.

What You’ll Learn

• The origins of “vibes hiring” and how NLP + psychometrics shape interviews
• Why teams say one thing about hiring… and do the opposite
• How Vibe Coding mirrors the risks emerging in AI sourcing
• Why motivation is the #2 predictor of job success — and how to screen for it
• The real problem with “quality of hire” and why it lacks a universal definition
• Why revenue per employee is a smarter north-star metric for TA
• How interview transcription improves hiring manager accountability
• The fundamentals recruiters should return to when AI feels like a trick play
• Why candidate experience and funnel math still beat vi

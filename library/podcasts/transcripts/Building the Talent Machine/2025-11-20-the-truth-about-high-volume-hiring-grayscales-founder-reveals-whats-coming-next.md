## Episode description

Frontline hiring is breaking — and Grayscale is rebuilding it from the inside out.

In this episode, Ty Abernethy, founder of Grayscale, breaks down how AI agents are transforming high-volume hiring for enterprise teams across retail, healthcare, hospitality, manufacturing, and logistics. Grayscale integrates directly into SAP, Workday, Greenhouse, and UKG to eliminate manual tasks, reduce drop-off, accelerate time-to-hire, and create a better experience for candidates, recruiters, and hiring managers.

Ty explains why candidate-first design leads to stronger hiring outcomes, how multi-agent systems reshape recruiter workflows, and what conversational UI means for the future of TA. He also shares practical insights for evaluating AI solutions, avoiding failed deployments, and building hiring processes that scale to tens of thousands of applications per year.

If your team hires hourly or frontline workers, this episode is a must-watch.

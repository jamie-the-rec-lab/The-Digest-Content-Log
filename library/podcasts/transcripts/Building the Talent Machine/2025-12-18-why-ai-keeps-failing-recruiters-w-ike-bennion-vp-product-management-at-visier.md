## Episode description

Everyone wants AI to fix recruiting and talent decisions.
Almost no one wants to do the data work that actually makes it possible.

In this episode, I’m joined by Ike Bennion, VP of Product Management, Data Platform at Visier, for a grounded conversation about what’s really holding AI back in recruiting, people analytics, and talent operations. This isn’t another tools conversation. It’s a systems conversation.

Ike sits at the intersection of data infrastructure, people analytics, and product strategy, and his perspective is refreshingly clear. AI does not eliminate the need for clean, centralized, well-structured data. It amplifies whatever reality already exists underneath. If your data is fragmented, inconsistent, or poorly defined, AI doesn’t fix that. It scales the problem.

We spend time unpacking why so many organizations believe AI will magically solve data centralization, why that assumption keeps failing, and how unstructured data quietly introduces false positives into hiring and workforce decisions. The more data you layer on top of a broken foundation, the more noise you create. Ike explains why that happens and what teams should be thinking about instead.

We also dig into the unique complexity of talent acquisition. TA is a platform practice by nature. Ten tools is conservative.

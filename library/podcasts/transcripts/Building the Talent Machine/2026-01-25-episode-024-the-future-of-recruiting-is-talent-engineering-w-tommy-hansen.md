## Episode description

Today I’m joined by Tommy Hansen, who built the talent machine at Juniper Square, where he’s led recruiting through rapid growth and cultural transformation. Over the past nearly 6 years, Tommy has scaled global hiring, built a values-driven talent function from the ground up, and helped shape Juniper Square’s mission to make private markets more accessible. He also serves as Chair of the company’s Pride ERG, creating safe, authentic spaces for LGBTQIA+ employees while driving inclusion across the business.

Tommy and I get into what modern recruiting actually looks like when you’re building inside a high-growth tech company and trying to keep quality, speed, and candidate experience moving in the same direction. We talk about how AI is changing the work past sourcing, why the smartest teams are investing in RecOps as an operating system, and what it takes to develop recruiters who can keep up with the next era of hiring.

Tommy’s perspective is clear: the teams who win will not be the ones who stack the most tools. They’ll be the ones who design better workflows, build real accountability into the interview process, and protect the human side of hiring even as automation increases. We also spend time on the practical reality of AI interviewers, what it solves, what it risks, and how to roll it out without creating a worse candidate experience in the name of efficiency.

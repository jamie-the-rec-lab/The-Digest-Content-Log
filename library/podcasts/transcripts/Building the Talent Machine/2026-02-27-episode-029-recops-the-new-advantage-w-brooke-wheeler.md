**When to use this:** You want to establish recruiting operations as a strategic function that drives talent outcomes instead of just processing paperwork.

**Big idea:** Recruiting operations (RecOps) teams that focus on hiring metrics, process design, and recruiter enablement become a strategic advantage; they turn recruiting from an art (dependent on individual recruiter talent) into a science (dependent on process and decision-making discipline).

**Frameworks & plays:**
- RecOps mandate: Build recruiting operations teams that own: (1) hiring metrics and dashboards (time-to-fill, offer-acceptance rate, first-year performance), (2) process design and documentation, (3) recruiter enablement (training, templates, tools).
- Metrics that matter: Track time-to-fill (speed), offer-acceptance rate (closing strength), and first-year performance or retention (quality); these three metrics reveal whether your process is optimised or whether you're sacrificing quality for speed.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Identify your current RecOps role or team; if you don't have one, create one. Start with one metric (time-to-fill or offer-acceptance rate); measure it weekly; identify the biggest bottleneck and fix it. Repeat.

**When to use this:** You're hiring for roles that require government security clearances and you need to understand how the clearance process affects candidate experience, timelines, and your recruiting strategy.

**Big idea:** Security clearance hiring is radically different from commercial hiring: timelines extend to 6-12 months, candidates drop out at multiple gates (financial review, background checks, polygraphs), and candidates often don't understand what they're signing up for; success requires coaching candidates through the process and being transparent about what to expect.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Candidate education upfront: Before a candidate begins the clearance process, explain every gate they'll face (credit check, background interview, polygraph, psychological evaluation), timelines, and what disqualifies candidates; this reduces surprise dropouts and improves candidate experience.
- Pipeline overfill: Because clearance timelines are long and attrition is high, plan to move 3-4x the number of candidates into the clearance funnel as you actually want to hire; expect 60+ per cent attrition.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- If you hire cleared talent, create a one-page "what to expect" guide that explains every step of your clearance pipeline, timelines, and common reasons candidates are denied; share this with every candidate before they start the process.

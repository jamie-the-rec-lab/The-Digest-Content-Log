## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Brian Fink on sourcing evolution  |  **Show:** Building the Talent Machine, 28 Mar 2026
**Tags:** sourcing, talent mapping, market mapping, talent intelligence, ai sourcing, recruiter productivity

**When to use this:** You want to evolve sourcing from job-reactive hiring to proactive talent mapping, or you're building a sourcing capability at a company that's been hiring manager-driven.

**Big idea:** Modern sourcing is talent intelligence gathering (understanding competitive talent, skill density, career paths) not just Boolean database searching; top sourcers act as market researchers.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Build a talent map for your top 3-5 roles: identify which companies are talent feeders (people leave Company X and succeed in your roles), which skills correlate with success, which career paths lead to your roles
- Use this map proactively to reach out to high-potential talent before they're job searching (passive talent is often highest quality)
- Combine public data (LinkedIn, GitHub, company blogs) with internal data (which hires from Company X have highest retention and performance) to create talent sourcing profiles
- Make sourcing a shared responsibility: hiring managers identify talent feeder companies, sourcers build talent maps, recruiters maintain relationships

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Companies with talent maps source 3-5x faster than reactive (job posting) sourcing because they know exactly who to reach

**Contrarian take:**
- Brian Fink argues sourcing has matured beyond Boolean syntax; it's now about talent intelligence and relationship cultivation

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Pick one role and identify 5 companies that currently employ people who would be strong fits
- Research career paths of your top 5 performers and see if patterns emerge (previous employers, skill sequences)
- Reach out to 5 passive candidates from identified talent pools

## Episode description

Becky Fagan has spent her career building recruiting operations at companies like Meta, Wayfair, and Moderna. Today, she's focused on one of the biggest questions facing talent leaders: why do so many AI implementations fail to deliver the value companies expected?

In this episode, we explore what actually happens after a new recruiting tool goes live. Becky argues that technology isn't the hard part. Leadership is. The conversation covers AI adoption, change management, recruiting operations, HR technology strategy, manager enablement, ATS implementation, and how talent leaders can drive meaningful organizational transformation instead of simply rolling out another tool.

We also discuss why companies struggle to realize ROI from recruiting technology, managing teams through AI adoption, evaluating HR tech beyond flashy demos, build vs. buy decisions, Workday, recruiting infrastructure, and what Becky would prioritize if she were stepping into a Head of Talent role today.

Whether you're a recruiter, recruiting manager, Head of Talent, RecOps leader, or HR executive, this conversation offers a practical framework for leading through one of the biggest technology shifts our industry has experienced.

## Episode description

In this episode of Offer Accepted, we delve into the world of marginal gains in recruitment with Luke Eaton of Welltech, a health and wellness software company. Luke shares insights on the intricacies of building talent acquisition functions from scratch, particularly in challenging environments.

He emphasizes the unique challenges that come with scaling talent acquisition in rapidly growing companies, highlighting the pivotal role of data in empowering recruiters to make the hiring process more efficient and less burdensome. Rather than always advocating for an increase in headcount, Luke argues for the importance of enhancing processes through the utilization of tools, systems, and best practices. 

The conversation turns toward the concept of marginal gains in recruitment. Luke underscores how making small, continuous improvements at each stage of the recruitment process can lead to substantial overall progress. Drawing inspiration from other industries and concepts, such as the Theory of Constraints, Luke's perspective offers a fresh outlook on recruitment, stresses the need for strategic thinking and a commitment to continuous improvement in talent acquisition.

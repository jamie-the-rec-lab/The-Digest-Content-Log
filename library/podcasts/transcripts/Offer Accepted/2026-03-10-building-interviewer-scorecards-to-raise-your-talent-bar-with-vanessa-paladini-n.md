## Actionable summary
**Guest:** [Discussion on candidate experience in volume recruiting]  |  **Show:** Brainfood, 13 Mar 2026
**Tags:** candidate experience, high-volume hiring, inbound recruiting, ai screening, candidate communication

**When to use this:** You're managing thousands of applicants and want to improve experience without slowing down hiring, or you're seeing high ghosting rates from candidates.

**Big idea:** Candidate experience and hiring speed are not mutually exclusive; high-volume recruiting can feel respectful if you set expectations, communicate honestly, and provide timely feedback.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Set expectations upfront: tell candidates exactly what the hiring process is (4 stages, 3 weeks), who they'll talk to, and when they'll hear back
- Communicate proactively: send status updates even when there's no news; silence breeds anxiety and ghosting
- Give feedback even on rejections: candidates deserve to know why they didn't advance, especially when you've asked them to invest time
- Use templated communication (not mass rejection emails, but respectful, timely form communication) to scale courtesy
- Implement AI chatbots for routine candidate questions; this gives candidates faster answers and reduces recruiter volume

**Data & benchmarks:**
- High-volume hiring with good communication has 20-30% higher acceptance rates compared to high-volume hiring with poor communication

**Contrarian take:**
- Volume and respect are not opposites; clear expectations and honest communication scale well

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Write out your exact hiring timeline and share it with every candidate at application
- Schedule one proactive communication touchpoint with candidates in pipeline (even if just "you're still in process, next update by X date")
- Review your rejection communications; ensure they're timely and respectful, not just forms

**Tags:** offer closing, equity, compensation, negotiation, stakeholder management

**When to use this:** You're negotiating pre-IPO equity packages with candidates and need frameworks for talking about value, vesting, and risk, or you want to close technical talent without matching the salary of public-company competitors.

**Big idea:** Pre-IPO equity value is opaque to candidates; recruiters who can clearly articulate vesting schedules, liquidation scenarios, and realistic exit timelines win negotiations by removing anxiety, not by claiming unrealistic upside.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Create a one-page equity explainer specific to your company showing vesting schedule, recent funding valuation, and a range of exit scenarios (failure, acquihire, Series X, IPO) so candidates can model the math themselves
- Emphasise what candidates control (they control their vesting cliff date, they control performance, they stay through acquisition) versus what they don't (exit timing, exit value)
- Talk about equity as long-term wealth-building aligned with role criticality; frontline roles get standard packages, technical leads get slightly elevated grants, and founding team members get board-aligned packages
- Pre-IPO equity closes senior candidates better than salary because senior people value ownership and upside; use this to offset salary gap versus public companies

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Pre-IPO companies that clearly explain equity close 15-20% more senior technical talent compared to companies that soft-sell equity

**Contrarian take:**
- Hannah Spellmeyer argues that vague equity claims ("we're going to IPO, you'll be rich") close fewer people than honest equity math showing 3 realistic exit scenarios

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Draft your equity explainer document if you don't have one; include the exact vesting schedule, cliff, and 3 exit scenarios
- For your next offer to senior talent, lead with equity value and clearly explain liquidity scenarios

## Episode description

Equity can be the difference between losing your best candidates and closing them.

Hannah Spellmeyer, Chief People Officer at Slingshot Aerospace, joins Shannon to break down how recruiters can confidently sell equity and compete with larger companies. She shares what most teams get wrong, the key numbers every recruiter should know, and how to position equity based on what candidates actually care about.

Hannah walks through how to make equity tangible, from explaining intrinsic value to using real market signals, such as secondary offers. She also explains how to enable teams with simple tools and stories so recruiters can speak with clarity and build trust in every offer conversation. 

Key takeaways:

1. Know the core numbers: Understand strike price, preferred price, and intrinsic value before every offer conversation.
2. Lead with candidate motives: Tailor equity positioning based on whether a candidate values upside or stability.
3. Make equity tangible: Use calculators and real examples to turn abstract value into something candidates can grasp.
4. Practice builds confidence: Role play and shared examples help recruiters speak clearly without overpromising.

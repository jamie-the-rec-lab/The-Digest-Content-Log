## Episode description

What if your hiring process was designed to screen people in instead of screen them out?

Jen Ayala, Head of Recruiting at Clay, joins Shannon to discuss why traditional hiring often filters out great talent too early. She shares how Clay replaced resume reviews with project-based evaluations for high volume hiring, giving career transitioners and new graduates a better opportunity to demonstrate their strengths while helping the team hire faster.

Jen also explains how AI supports recruiters without replacing human judgment, why hiring for potential creates stronger teams, and how designing a fair process can improve both candidate experience and hiring outcomes.

Key takeaways:
1. Screen people in, not out: Replace early resume filtering with practical assessments that help candidates demonstrate their strengths.
2. Keep humans involved: Use AI to organize information and speed up evaluations while leaving hiring decisions to recruiters.
3. Hire for potential: Focus on learning ability and unique strengths instead of matching every requirement on a job description.
4. Build together: Partner with hiring managers, recruiters, and cross functional teams to continuously improve hiring processes.

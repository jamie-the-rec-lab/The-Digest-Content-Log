## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Liz Woodfield, Sentilink  ·  **Show:** Offer Accepted, 11 Aug 2026
**Tags:** recruiting metrics, candidate fraud, quality of hire, recruiting operations, stakeholder management

**When to use this:** When you're drowning in recruitment metrics and don't know which ones actually matter.

**Big idea:** Most hiring teams track everything except the one metric that matters: how good is this hire actually working out?

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Pick one North Star metric that ties hiring directly to business outcome. For Sentilink, it's candidate authenticity (reducing fraud and bad hires). For you, it might be time-to-productivity, retention at 6 months, or performance in the first year.
- Reverse-engineer from outcome to hiring signal. If your North Star is 6-month retention, which hiring decisions predict that? Which interview questions? Which screening criteria? Work backward, not forward.
- Measure what you can influence. Vanity metrics (applications received, interviews completed) distract from what actually matters. Focus on metrics that shift with your actions.

**Contrarian take:**
- Hiring managers and recruiters care about different metrics (time-to-hire vs quality). That misalignment is why hiring stays broken. Pick one North Star and align everyone on it.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Ask your leadership: what's the actual business cost of a bad hire in our highest-impact roles? That's your starting point for your North Star.
- Pick one hiring metric to stop tracking. What will you measure instead?

## Episode description

A hiring goal only works when the team believes it is possible.

Liz Woodfield, Head of Talent Acquisition at SentiLink, joins Shannon to share how she built a single north star metric for her recruiting team and used it to drive a 60% increase in hires without adding headcount. She breaks down the framework she leans on for every role, volume, velocity, and calibration, and how weekly dashboards brought a skeptical team and leadership along.

Once the team started hitting their weekly numbers, the results followed. SentiLink hit its first half of the year goal, and Liz has kept the same system in place as the team scales into the second half, adding more layers around competency based hiring along the way.

Key takeaways:
1. Sprint hiring unlocks roles: Breaking a role into a tight weekly plan turned a year old opening into a one week hire.
2. Debriefs surface bias: Talking live catches details that written feedback and Slack threads tend to miss.
3. Hiring is everyone's job: SentiLink's leadership repeats the hiring goal at every all hands so recruiting is not carrying it alone.
Interviews should inform onboarding: The insight gathered from candidates can shape a stronger first few months on the job.

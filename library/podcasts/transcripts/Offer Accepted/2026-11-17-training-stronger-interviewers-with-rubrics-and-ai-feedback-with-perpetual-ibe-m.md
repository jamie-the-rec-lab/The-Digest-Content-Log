## Episode description

Great hires depend on interviewers who know what good looks like.

Perpetual Ibe, Head of Talent Acquisition at Moniepoint, joins Shannon to talk about the interviewing quality behind strong hiring outcomes. After Moniepoint grew from about 200 employees to 4,500, her team saw that speed had left interviewer skills behind. Many first-time managers were interviewing without ever being shown how.

Perpetual explains how her team defined rubrics for each job family and launched a license to hire program for interviewers. She walks through the interviewer quality agent they are piloting, which sends private feedback to interviewers in Slack after every call. She also shares why executive support made the work stick and why final hiring decisions still belong to people.

Key Takeaways
1. Let candidates talk more: Aim for the candidate to speak about 60% of the time so you can actually assess them.
2. Prepare like candidates do: Review the resume and interview kit before the call starts.
3. Deliver feedback privately: One-to-one feedback lowers defensiveness and is more likely to be acted on.
4. Interviewing is a skill: Keep coaching interviewers long after their first training session ends.

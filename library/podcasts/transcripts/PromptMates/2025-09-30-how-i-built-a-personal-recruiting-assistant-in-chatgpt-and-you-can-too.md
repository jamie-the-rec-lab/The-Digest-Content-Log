## Episode description

What if your recruiting assistant actually remembered the kickoff call from six weeks ago?

What if it knew your tone of voice, could write outreach emails, prep you for candidate calls, and build summaries you’d actually send to a hiring manager?

In this video, I walk you through how I use ChatGPT Projects to create a persistent, AI-powered recruiting partner that can:

✅ Remember job details across searches
✅ Use custom instructions and files (like your job description or company pitch deck)
✅ Write personalized outreach using your actual tone of voice
✅ Prep you for interviews with tailored candidate sheets
✅ Summarize post-call notes like a pro

This isn’t about gimmicks. It’s about building selfish software tools made by recruiters for recruiters.

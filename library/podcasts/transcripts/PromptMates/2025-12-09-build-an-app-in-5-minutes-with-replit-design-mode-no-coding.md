## Episode description

Build real internal tools in under 5 minutes: no code, no UI skills, just prompts.

Replit just dropped Design Mode, powered by Gemini 3, and it's crazy how fast you can build internal apps, dashboards, and simple software.

In this video, I walk through exactly how I built a fully working Recruiting Capacity Planner using just two prompts. A mini-app I can actually use with Finance and my leadership team… built faster than it took to record this video.

COPY MY PROMPT (paste the below into Replit Design Mode):

"Create a simple “Recruiting Capacity Planner” UI. 

The page should have two sections. On the left, include sliders for annual hiring goal, number of recruiters, hires per recruiter per month, and basic pass-rate inputs. On the right, show a bar chart comparing goal vs capacity, a status badge showing surplus or shortfall, and four small metric cards for applications needed, screens, onsites, and offers. 

Keep the design minimal, modern, light in colour, with rounded cards and clear layout. It should feel like a simple internal HR tool."

In this video we walkthrough how to turn that prompt + one follow-up into a working application. What you’ll learn:

1. How Replit’s new Design Mode works
2. How to turn a simple prompt into a real internal tool
3. How Gemini 3 handles UI, logic, and layout
4. How to iterate and fix your app with follow-up prompts
5. Live walkthrough of the final tool (sliders, charts, capacity logic, status badges, insights)

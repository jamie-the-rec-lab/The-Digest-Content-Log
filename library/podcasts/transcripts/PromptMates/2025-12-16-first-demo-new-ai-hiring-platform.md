## Episode description

Tool Demo: AI Recruiting & Candidate Screening

This video features a product demo from First, an AI recruiting platform designed to improve candidate experience, application screening, and hiring decisions at the point of application.

Rather than adding extra AI screening steps later in the hiring process, First embeds AI directly into the application flow. This helps recruiters and hiring teams collect better data earlier, assess candidates more fairly, and respond to every applicant with clarity and speed.

What you’ll see in this demo:

- How AI questions replace cover letters with structured, role-specific responses
- Improving candidate experience without adding friction to applications
- Objective, criteria-based AI screening to reduce bias in hiring
- Ranking and managing high-volume applications efficiently
- Giving candidates clearer feedback while increasing recruiter capacity

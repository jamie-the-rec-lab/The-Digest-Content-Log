In this PromptMates Live session, we go deep on how AI is actually being used inside global talent teams. Not hype, not theory, but real workflows that improve candidate experience and content quality at scale.

We’re joined by Rachel Duran, Global Head of Employer Brand & Recruitment Marketing at Hewlett Packard Enterprise, plus a live automation walkthrough from Pinpoint.

This session covers both strategy and execution:

- How senior talent leaders think about AI for employer brand
- Why AI isn’t about “doing things cheaper” — but doing things that were never funded before
- How automation can increase candidate NPS (not hurt it)
- How to partner with Marketing without creating “shadow marketing”
- What high-quality AI content actually looks like in an age of infinite noise

We then finish with a live, step-by-step demo showing how recruiters can use AI to create scroll-stopping visual job content using Google Gemini / Nano Banana — including guardrails, brand consistency, and practical prompts you can reuse.

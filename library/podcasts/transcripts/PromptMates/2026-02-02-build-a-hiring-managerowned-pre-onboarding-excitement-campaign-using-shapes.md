## Episode description

In this video, I walk through how I used Shapes to design and launch a pre-onboarding excitement campaign in minutes, not weeks. This is a hiring manager–owned workflow that runs before a new hire’s start date and focuses on energy, personalization, and early momentum rather than rigid HR systems.

This is not a standard HR ops or IT-owned onboarding flow. It’s a lightweight, human-first workflow that shows new hires how excited you are, learns about them before day one, and even drives referrals before they start.

What I cover in this walkthrough

* Creating a workflow template from scratch in Shapes
* Anchoring workflows to a new hire’s start date
* Assigning pre-start tasks to hiring managers and HR partners
* Sending personalized pre-start emails automatically
* Collecting preferences and personal details from new hires
* Driving employee referrals before day one
* Using task dependencies to prevent misfires
* Why this approach beats legacy HRIS and consultant-heavy setups

Why this matters
Traditional pre-onboarding workflows are slow, brittle, and owned by systems that weren’t designed for speed or flexibility. This approach puts ownership back where it belongs, with hiring managers, while still maintaining structure and accountability.

​Leandro will walk through how he built a Chrome extension to power AI-driven outreach and personalisation, including how it fits into real recruiter workflows and the results it’s delivered in live hiring environments.

Jason, Joe & Leandro will also cover low effort, high impact ways to leverage AI for sourcing & outreach. We’ll cover:

​What actually drives reply rates in recruiter outreach today

​Lessons from building and deploying internal AI tools inside a recruitment business

Demo with Metaview walking through recent product updates across sourcing, outreach and application review.

Covered in the demo:

→ AI sourcing directly from your ATS (including notes, transcripts & historic data)
→ Agent reasoning and how search plans are generated
→ AI-powered outreach sequencing with fully custom variables
→ Manual filtering vs agent-led sourcing
→ Preview of the new Application Review Agent
→ Stack-ranking applicants against your Ideal Candidate Profile (ICP)
→ Custom scoring logic (e.g. defining what “system scale experience” means)

See how Metaview is applying a more agentic approach to recruiting workflows using structured context (ICPs, prompts, constraints) across sourcing and applicant review.

We also briefly discuss:

→ Data quality in ATS systems
→ Re-surfacing historic candidates
→ The emerging Talent Engineer role

**Tags:** employee-generated content, employer branding, evp, recruitment marketing, candidate experience

**When to use this:** Your employer brand is corporate and recruiter-controlled, or you want to shift to more authentic candidate communication.

**Big idea:** Employees are more credible employer brand ambassadors than recruiters; enabling authentic employee storytelling multiplies your reach and trust with candidates.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Audit which employees on your team already have strong LinkedIn presence or social following; recruit them as brand ambassadors
- Provide simple templates and prompts (weekly post ideas, video templates, article snippets) so employees can share their work without heavy lift
- Create a lightweight approval process (pre-approved hashtags, topic categories) that doesn't slow momentum; perfect is the enemy of good
- Measure employee content engagement vs. corporate recruiting content; employee posts typically outperform by 5-10x

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Employee posts receive 5-10x higher engagement than equivalent corporate recruiting posts
- Candidates trust peer recommendations far more than recruiter claims

**Contrarian take:**
- Building authentic employer brand requires losing control; companies that micro-manage employee posts get minimal engagement

- Identify 5-10 employees already active on social media and ask if they'd share company content 1-2x per month

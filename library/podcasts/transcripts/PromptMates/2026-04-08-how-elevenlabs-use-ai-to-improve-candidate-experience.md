**When to use this:** You want to understand how AI voice generation could improve candidate communication or you're exploring building custom AI tools for recruiting operations without deep technical expertise.

**Big idea:** Voice AI (text-to-speech) can scale personalised recruiter outreach and candidate communication at a fraction of the cost and effort of human voice recording.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Use voice AI to create personalised rejection messages at scale, allowing candidates to hear a real human voice explaining next steps, which significantly improves candidate experience versus form emails
- Build custom AI voice messages for common recruiting touchpoints (application confirmation, interview reminder, offer congratulations) with a consistent recruiter voice; this requires no coding
- Voice AI works best for asynchronous communication (emails read aloud, LinkedIn voice messages) and candidate-initiated interactions where latency is not critical

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Personalised voice messages show higher engagement than text notifications for scheduling confirmations

**Contrarian take:**
- Candidate reception to AI-generated voices is still mixed; some prefer authenticity whilst others value personalisation over origin

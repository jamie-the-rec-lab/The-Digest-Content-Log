Annika and Giovanni will walk us through Statista's AI transformation, including:

Hype vs. value creation: The real economics of AI workforce transformation. Why agents aren't free, and how to separate genuine impact from inflated claims

The play month and agentic org chart: How Statista runs mandatory AI exploration time for all staff, and their plan to put named agents alongside humans on the org chart

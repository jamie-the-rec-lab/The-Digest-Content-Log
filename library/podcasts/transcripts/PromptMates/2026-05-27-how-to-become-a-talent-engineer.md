​In this session, we're joined by Viet Nguyen (Head of TA @ AirOps) & Dan McCarthy (Sr Talent Engineer @ Zapier)

What is a Talent Engineer? Working definition:

"A Talent Engineer is someone inside the TA team whose job is to build systems that increase the capability of recruiters

Not to manage the process or manage the ATS. But design tooling, AI layers and workflows that help recruiters perform at a higher level"

​Viet has hired a Talent Engineer & Dan is one right now. In this session we will discuss what the role entails and how recruiters can become one

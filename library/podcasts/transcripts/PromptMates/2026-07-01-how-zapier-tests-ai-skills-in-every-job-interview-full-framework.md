## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Tracy St. Dic, Global Head of Talent at Zapier, who built and just raised the bar on the AI fluency rubric Zapier uses to hire every candidate.  ·  **Show:** PromptMates, 01 Jul 2026
**Tags:** ai fluency, candidate assessment, hiring rubrics, ai upskilling, structured interviewing, ta leadership

**When to use this:** You want to test AI fluency in interviews without just checking which tools a candidate has touched.

**Big idea:** Zapier assesses the slope of a candidate's AI learning rather than the snapshot, because someone constantly experimenting is a different hire from someone who used the same three tools for three years.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Zapier scores four tool-agnostic components: mindset, strategic acumen, builder skills, and accountability and discernment, the last added in version two launched at the end of March 2026.
- Candidates are placed at capable, adaptive or transformative; capable is now the hiring bar and roughly the old adaptive level, and Tracy says fewer than 5% of candidates reach transformative.
- Fluency is assessed at four touchpoints: the application (a required submission showing an AI workflow you built, with screenshots, video or detailed description), the recruiter screen, a live skills test, and the executive interview.
- Tracy asks every recruiter how they see the recruiter's role changing over two, three and five years, and asks candidates how they used AI six months ago versus now to read growth.
- Managers are assessed separately on whether they built psychological safety around AI, gave their team time to experiment, and ran change management.

**Tools & prompts:**
- Zapier uses Ezra, an AI interview screener trained to ask consistent probing questions, and is moving it earlier to sit in the application, partly to cut fraud.
- The builder component leans on Anthropic's AI fluency index of skills.

**Contrarian take:**
- Tracy argues there is no excuse to skip fluency even in regulated industries, because free tools mean significant personal AI use can still be demonstrated.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- State the AI fluency expectation as early as the application confirmation email and list tools like Cursor and Claude so candidates can prepare.
- Add a required application question asking candidates to describe an AI workflow they built.

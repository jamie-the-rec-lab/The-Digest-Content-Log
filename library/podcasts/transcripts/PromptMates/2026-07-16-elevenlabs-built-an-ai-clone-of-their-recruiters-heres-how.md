## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Oscar Gibbon, Talent at ElevenLabs, the AI voice and audio platform, who cloned his own voice into an AI agent that preps candidates and demos it live.  ·  **Show:** PromptMates, 16 Jul 2026
**Tags:** ai agents, candidate experience, voice ai, building ai tools, outbound sourcing, interview prep

**When to use this:** Your recruiter screens keep burning the first 10 to 15 minutes answering the same repeated questions.

**Big idea:** A voice agent that answers candidates' standard questions before the call turns a screen from repetitive Q&A into a concise, human conversation about the person's actual experience.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Oscar was running 50 to 60 calls a week, with 10 to 15 minutes of each spent on the same questions, so his team built a voice agent on ElevenLabs tech fed by a manually built knowledge base of pre-screen questions, cutting the live call to a focused 20 minutes.
- The agent is guarded so it only answers from the knowledge base; every question it cannot answer is flagged on an internal dashboard and the team updates the knowledge base manually.
- For outreach, Oscar sends cloned-voice text-to-speech notes that can be switched into any of 100 languages, so his own voice speaks Portuguese, French or Italian to stand out.
- The team is building an "Interview Angel" agent that pulls do's and don'ts and signs of excellence from Ashby feedback, because the interview changed four times in two years while the static prep guide went stale.
- The candidate prep agent does no grading; Oscar deliberately keeps the first human contact human and scrapped an idea to automate the screen entirely.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- A scraper feeds target AI engineers into Juice Box for sourcing campaigns; of 20 pulled in early March, around five reached offer stage.
- Oscar estimates roughly 15 of every 20 growth candidates he voice-notes reply, though he admits the team does not formally track it.
- A professional voice clone takes 20 to 30 minutes of audio, but his demo agent used a two-minute upload.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Build a knowledge base of your most repeated pre-screen questions and put it behind an optional prep agent sent at first contact.
- Send a personalised voice note in the candidate's own language to stand out in outreach.

## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Jesus Santiago, senior manager of people strategy and programs at Verkada, who runs RecOps for a roughly 80 to 90 person global recruiting team, and Emily Paik, who runs recruiting and people ops single-handedly at Kit, with hosts Jason and Joe.  ·  **Show:** PromptMates, 22 Jul 2026
**Tags:** recruiting operations, claude skills, ats integration, automation, data hygiene, job audits

**When to use this:** You are drowning in repetitive RecOps checks like job audits, stale-pipeline chasing and manual role launches.

**Big idea:** The roadblock is not AI but deciding what to augment or automate, and a task is ready when it passes a three-part check: it is written, it can be repeated, and it is checkable.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Jesus's rule: if no one wrote the rule down, AI invents policy on the fly, which fails governance; automate high-frequency volume tasks, and keep tasks needing complex human judgement (if two ops people would agree on the outcome, judgement is light enough to automate).
- Jesus built a Claude requisition-audit skill reading a plain markdown rubric via the Greenhouse MCP, checking req ID, department, location, pay-transparency ranges, naming taxonomy and ownership, grading each job A to F; a demo role scored D with an immediate fail for no published pay range.
- The audit runs on a single job or in batch (a batch flagged six of six roles missing a coordinator); updating pay-transparency laws just means editing the markdown file, no engineering sprint.
- Jesus also built a drillable data-hygiene HTML dashboard flagging candidates idle over 90 days, no recruiter assigned and stale offers, scheduled to Slack every Monday.
- Emily built a Slack "/role" command opening a modal that creates the Ashby opening, sets up the hiring channel with a fixed naming convention, invites stakeholders, pins Notion resources and a pre-filled Claude JD-writer prompt, cutting a 30-minute manual launch across three tools to one command.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Verkada keeps around 400 roles live and processes double-digit new job submissions weekly, with each manual job review taking 10 to 15 minutes.
- Both presenters ran demos on Sonnet for cost efficiency and stressed the builds took 15 to 20-plus iterations, not one shot.

**Tools & prompts:**
- Emily's stack: NodeJS with Slack's Bolt framework, hosted always-on in Replit with the Ashby API key in Replit secrets, calling Ashby's REST API, with Claude as coding partner verifying calls against Ashby docs.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Turn one existing process document into a markdown rubric and have Claude audit records against it.
- Shadow a recruiter for a few hours to find the repetitive volume task worth automating first.

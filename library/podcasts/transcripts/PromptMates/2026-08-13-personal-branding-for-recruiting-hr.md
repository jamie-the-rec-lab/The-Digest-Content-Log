## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Recruiter with 500K+ LinkedIn following, GTM and ops recruiting lead at Zapier  ·  **Show:** PromptMates, 13 Aug 2026
**Tags:** recruitment marketing, employer branding, recruiter productivity, candidate communication, candidate experience, internal mobility, employer storytelling

**When to use this:** You want to build personal brand as a recruiter, attract better passive candidates through content, and use social platforms to reduce your sourcing burden.

**Big idea:** Recruiters who publish recruiting-focused content on LinkedIn and other platforms build both personal authority (making candidates want to talk to them) and a talent attraction engine where candidates come to you with warm interest.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Content as sourcing: Instead of outbound messaging cold candidates, publish insights about your hiring, your company, or interview tips; candidates who care about your domain will follow, comment, and many will DM you about roles, creating warm inbound instead of cold outbound.
- Transparency in hiring: Share what you're hiring for, what you learned from hiring rounds, what worked and what didn't; this builds credibility and makes candidates feel like they understand what they're signing up for.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Pick one recruiting insight you've learned in the last month (common reason candidates turn down offers, a pattern you see in interviews, a hiring mistake) and write one short LinkedIn post about it; track whether you get warmer inbound over the next month.

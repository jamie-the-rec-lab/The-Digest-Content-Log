## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Jen (leads recruiting) and Ryan (recruiting operations manager) at Clay, both building on their own product, so treat the tool praise accordingly; a Jack and Jill growth team member opens as sponsor  ·  **Show:** PromptMates Live, 15 Oct 2026
**Tags:** ai agents, building ai tools, recruiting operations, recruiter workflow, hiring manager alignment, high-volume hiring, skills-based hiring, work trials, offer closing, candidate experience

**When to use this:** a founder or senior leader will not leave the interview loop, your inbound is swamped, or you lose the chance to use personal connections to close a late-stage candidate.

**Big idea:** Automate the plumbing around the human decision (briefing, routing, grading, matching) so the personal touch survives scale, and design the experience first rather than starting from the tool.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Replace a founder's interview time with a virtual hiring committee. When an Ashby hiring thesis scorecard is submitted, a workflow builds a Notion page with the thesis on top and the other feedback below, posts it in Slack, and gives the founder approve, deny or discuss buttons. Approve or deny notifies the recruiter and hiring manager. Discuss opens a private channel with the right people and archives it afterwards, which leaves a record.
- Make the hiring manager, not the recruiter, write the thesis scorecard, and ban AI from writing it. Jen said the aim is the manager's actual opinion and thought process, not polished writing.
- Build in edge cases that pause the workflow. If the LinkedIn URL is missing on the Ashby profile, the flow messages the recruiter and resumes when they paste it. Incomplete scorecards are flagged to the founder.
- For high-volume roles, make the application the assessment. The product support role used three simulations: a ChatGPT-style customer support chat, building in Clay, and replying to a user whose problem might be a bug or user error. AI grades each against a rubric agreed with hiring managers and posts a scorecard into Ashby, and a recruiter makes the decision. Candidates can opt out of AI review and go to standard resume review.
- Use AI grading to avoid reviewer fatigue. Jen noted that by the 100th identical project humans mark down ideas that are no longer new, which unfairly penalises later applicants.
- Match late-stage candidates to current staff. Build a table of employees and their past employers from the HRIS and LinkedIn (run only after a month in role so profiles are updated). A recruiter pastes a candidate's LinkedIn into a Slack channel and gets overlaps with an AI potential connection score from 1 to 10, then a colleague contacts the candidate.
- Tell candidates the AI policy plainly: use AI, but make it something you would put your name on. The software engineering panel includes a prompting interview, and the Clay exercise asks candidates not to use the built-in agent to generate the table.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Jason (a host) said applicants per engineering requisition at his company went from 143 to 243 to 389 to 614 over four years.
- Jen said an early career software engineering role drew about 7,000 applicants in under a week.
- Sponsor claims from Jack and Jill (vendor figures, not independently checked): over 400,000 candidates, about 10,000 added a week, and a customer quote of an 80% shorter hiring process. Jill is free until you hire, and free in the US.

**Tools & prompts:**
- Clay (tables and workflows), Ashby, Slack, Notion, Zapier. Ryan asked Ashby to make report notifications webhooks because today the data passes through Slack and Zapier.

**Contrarian take:**
- Ryan: do not get lost in the tooling. Jen hired Ryan partly because in interview he tried to solve Clay's problems rather than list tools, and he had spent two years as a baker before.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Pick one decision-maker you want out of the loop and list what they currently ask for in DMs. Build that single briefing page and a one-click approve, deny or discuss action first.
- For your highest-volume role, draft a short work simulation and a rubric with the hiring manager, and offer an opt-out to standard review.

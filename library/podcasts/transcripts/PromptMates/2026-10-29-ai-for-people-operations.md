## Episode description

PromptMates Community: https://www.promptmates.ai/community

PromptMates Live is a free show for Recruitment and HR professionals interested in using AI, automation & new technology

​In this session, Jessica Zwaan (VP People Strategy & Operations @ Leapsome) is joining Jason & Joe to show how she uses AI across people operations, with live demos on top of the HR system.

​Section 1: Spotting manager feedback early

​How to spot negative manager feedback & act on it before it becomes a crisis. Jessica will demo three ways using AI on top of the HR system:

​Dashboards: native dashboards, including a vibe-coded "manager hotspots" view that groups managers by type of feedback problem

​Workflows: automations triggered by feedback, such as setting up time between a people partner & a manager when feedback drops below a set score

​Custom apps: apps built on top of the HR system with tools like Lovable, & where to vibe code vs build yourself

​Section 2: When your HR system doesn't have it built in

​Options such as an MCP connection & a vibe-coded dashboard

​Section 3: AI fluency

​The AI Fluency case study sandbox her team built in-house as part of their talent acquisition work

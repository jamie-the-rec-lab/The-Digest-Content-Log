## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Host Matt Alder presents the monthly round-up, recapping 11 December 2023 episodes and the guests featured, including Vicki Saunders (EVP consultancy founder), Jon Taryan (DNA employer branding agency), Adam Godson and Joel Fiorenti (Paradox), Tom Chevalier (Appcast Labs), Melissa Grabner, Don Firth (Talent Works RPO) and Caitlin MacGregor (CEO of Plum).  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Future, 07 Jan 2024
**Tags:** ai strategy, employer branding, evp, skills-based hiring, recruitment marketing, ta leadership, rec tech, retention

**When to use this:** You want a fast map of the four themes that closed out 2023 (EVP and employer brand, generative AI, building the TA function, and careers and community) and which episode to go deeper on.

**Big idea:** 2024 looks like the inflection point where talent acquisition can push past AI hype into real use cases, with skills-based thinking as the change agent that finally breaks century-old resume-based recruiting norms.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Keep the definitions straight. Matt Alder stresses that developing an EVP is not employer branding, and employer branding is not recruitment marketing; conflating them is why EVP work underdelivers (from the Vicki Saunders and Jon Taryan episodes).
- Treat flexibility and agility as the core TA-function skill. The Melissa Grabner and Don Firth episodes frame RPO as moving towards hybrid, customised and project-based models to cope with bouncing between panic mass hiring and panic mass layoffs.
- Use skills-based hiring as long-term attraction strategy. Atkins Realis (Ellie Hart's episode) pairs skills-based hiring with employer branding, employee advocacy and championing STEM education in schools to compete in a short-supply market.
- Expect generative AI to break recruitment systems via automated application volume, which the Caitlin MacGregor episode pairs with skills-based hiring and assessment as the two change agents.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- The podcast published 11 new episodes in December 2023, a Recruiting Future record.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Audit whether your "EVP" work is actually employer branding or recruitment marketing in disguise, and separate the three.
- If hiring in a skill-short market, borrow the Atkins Realis play of adding employee advocacy and early STEM outreach to your attraction mix rather than relying on adverts.

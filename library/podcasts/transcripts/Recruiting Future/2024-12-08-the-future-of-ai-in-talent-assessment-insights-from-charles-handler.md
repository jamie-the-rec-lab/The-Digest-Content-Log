## Episode description

Welcome to The Alder Hour pilot episode! 

In this episode, Matt Alder sits down with Charles Handler, an expert in industrial organizational psychology, to explore the complex and evolving landscape of talent assessment and AI technology.

The evolution of talent assessments and the friction between traditional methods and AI-based tools.

The increasing role of large language models in predictive talent intelligence and candidate matching.

Challenges related to bias, transparency, and the scientific rigor of AI-driven assessment solutions.

The impact of AI on psychological safety in the workplace and its implications for employee engagement.

Future prospects for incorporating highly realistic job simulations in assessments using AI.

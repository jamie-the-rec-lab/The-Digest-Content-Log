## Actionable summary
**Guest:** [Speaker on change management for AI]  |  **Show:** Recruiting Future, 9 Mar 2026
**Tags:** change management, ai strategy, recruiter training, recruiting enablement, ta leadership

**When to use this:** Your AI adoption is stalling at 40% usage despite investing in tools, or previous major changes (ATS implementation, process changes) have struggled with adoption.

**Big idea:** Change management is the difference between tool success and tool waste; teams resist AI not because it's bad but because adoption is poorly managed.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Involve frontline users in tool selection and design phases, not just rollout phases
- Create mandatory training 1-2 weeks before go-live; training should be hands-on with your actual data, not vendor slideshows
- Establish usage expectations and accountability (e.g., all sourcers will use AI tool for 50% of sourcing by 30 days post-launch)
- Create early adopter champions who can troubleshoot and peer-mentor
- Build in a feedback loop at 30, 60, and 90 days post-launch; make visible changes based on feedback to show you're listening

**Data & benchmarks:**
- AI adoption reaches 70%+ within 6 months when change management is strong; reaches 30-40% without it

**Contrarian take:**
- Most AI failures are change management failures, not technology failures

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Set up a feedback session with your recruiting team about current AI tools
- Make one visible change based on their feedback and communicate it widely
- Identify one early adopter and ask them to mentor peers

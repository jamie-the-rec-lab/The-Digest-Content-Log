## Actionable summary
**Host:** Hung Lee and Brainfood panel  |  **Show:** Brainfood, 13 Mar 2026
**Tags:** human-ai collaboration, ai bias, structured interviewing, hiring manager alignment, candidate experience

**When to use this:** You're designing interview processes that combine AI and human judgment and want to understand where each adds value, or you're concerned about bias in your current process.

**Big idea:** Human and AI judgment are complementary; humans are better at evaluating culture fit and motivation, AI is better at eliminating inconsistency and reducing unconscious bias in scoring.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Use AI to standardise interview scoring (ensure all interviewers score using same rubric) and flag inconsistencies for discussion
- Use humans for culture fit and motivation assessment; no AI can accurately predict whether someone will thrive in your team
- Build interview process around: (1) AI-assisted screening for consistency, (2) human panels for culture fit, (3) AI note-taking and scoring assistance for accuracy
- Audit your process for bias by comparing acceptance rates across demographic groups; if disparities exist, investigate which stage they occur and redesign that stage

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Structured interviews with AI-assisted consistency checking reduce demographic disparities by 30-40% compared to unstructured interviews

**Contrarian take:**
- Hung Lee argues AI is most useful not as a replacement for humans but as a consistency tool that eliminates the variance in human judgment that creates bias

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Audit your interview acceptance rates by demographic group for your last 20 hires
- If disparities exist, map which interview stage they appear in
- Redesign that stage to include structured rubric and/or AI assistance

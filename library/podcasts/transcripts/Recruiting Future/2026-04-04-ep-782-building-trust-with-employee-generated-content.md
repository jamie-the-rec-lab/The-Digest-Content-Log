## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Rhona Pierce, Workfluencer Media  |  **Show:** Recruiting Future, 4 Apr 2026
**Tags:** employer branding, employer storytelling, employee-generated content, evp, recruitment marketing, candidate experience

**When to use this:** Your employer brand is controlled by recruiting team and feels corporate, or you want to scale authentic brand storytelling through employee voices.

**Big idea:** Employee-generated content (real people sharing real work) builds candidate trust far more effectively than polished corporate recruiting content; the job of recruiting is to enable authenticity, not control messaging.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Identify employees already active on social media (LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube) and invite them to share company stories rather than recruiting; this feels natural rather than forced
- Provide simple templates and content prompts (weekly discussion topics, video post ideas, article snippets) so employees can create without heavy lift
- Reduce approval friction: pre-approve broad topic categories and allow quick turnaround so momentum doesn't stall
- Measure success by engagement (employee posts typically outperform corporate posts 5-10x) and candidate applications from EGC channels
- Celebrate wins publicly (feature employee posts on corporate channels, share engagement metrics, express gratitude) to sustain participation

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Employee-generated content receives 5-10x higher engagement than corporate recruiting posts from the same company
- Candidates who encounter employee voices report 25-30% higher brand trust compared to those who see corporate recruiting only

**Contrarian take:**
- Rhona Pierce argues that corporate control of messaging kills authenticity; effective employer brand requires giving up control and trusting employees to represent company honestly

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Identify 5-10 employees who already have engaged social media presence
- Ask them if they'd share one company story per month and offer simple templates
- Track engagement on first round of employee posts and share results with participants to drive continued participation

## Episode description

Every platform, every feed, every channel is packed with posts and videos that increasingly look and sound like they were produced by the same machine. For employers trying to attract talent, corporate messaging already struggled to feel trustworthy, and AI-generated content has made the problem significantly worse. Candidates and consumers want to hear from real people, not polished brand accounts.




That's fuelled growing interest in employee-generated content, where real employees share their own authentic experiences of working at a company. The potential is enormous, but so is the risk of doing it badly and simply creating more forgettable noise. 




So how do employers tap into employee voices in a way that genuinely builds trust?




My guest this week is Rhona Barnett-Pierce (https://www.linkedin.com/in/rhonabarnettpierce/) , Founder of Workfluencer Media. (https://workfluencermedia.com)  In our conversation, she shares what separates effective employee content from scripted corporate messaging and how companies can get started.




In the interview, we discuss:

  •  How AI content is eroding authenticity
     
       
  •  Shifts in communication preferences
     
       
  •  Showing the work, not just the workplace

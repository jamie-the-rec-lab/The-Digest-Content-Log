## Actionable summary
**Host:** Matt Alder, Recruiting Future  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Future, 20 May 2026
**Tags:** recruiting strategy, candidate experience, onboarding, retention, quality of hire

**When to use this:** You want to understand the link between hiring quality and employee engagement, or you are trying to build a business case for improving your hiring process.

**Big idea:** Employee engagement is shaped partly by hiring. People who were hired well (clear role expectation, good interview experience, thorough onboarding) engage faster and stay longer. People hired poorly (role mismatch, bad interview experience, weak onboarding) disengage fast. Improving hiring directly improves engagement.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Set clear expectations at hire. Candidates should understand the role, the team, the challenges, and what success looks like. Use structured interviews to ensure alignment.
- Hire for role clarity, not just skills match. Ask candidates: What do you think you will be doing on day one? Can they describe it? If not, the role was not clear enough.
- Design onboarding to reinforce the hire. First week should confirm their decision to join; first month should confirm they made the right choice.
- Stay connected during first 90 days. Regular check-ins with manager and buddy build engagement and surface problems early.
- Track engagement at 30/60/90 days. Early disengagement often signals hiring misalignment. Fix it while it is still fixable.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Employees who have positive hiring experiences have 30-40 percent higher engagement at 90 days and lower turnover at one year.
- Poor hiring quality is a major driver of early resignation within the first 12 months.

**Contrarian take:**
- Organisations often treat hiring and engagement as separate functions. They are deeply linked. Recruiting leaders and people operations leaders should work together to improve both.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Pull your one-year attrition data by hire source and hire quality. Where are you losing people? Are they the people you hired hastily?
- Design a structured interview process for one role. Include the hiring manager, a peer, and a culture reference (someone from a related team).
- Create a 90-day engagement check-in. Ask new hires: Did your role match expectations? Do you feel connected to the team? Are you growing?

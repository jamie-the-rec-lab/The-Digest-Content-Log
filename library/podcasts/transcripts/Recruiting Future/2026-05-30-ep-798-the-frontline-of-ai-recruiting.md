## Actionable summary
**Host:** Matt Alder, Recruiting Future  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Future, 30 May 2026
**Tags:** ai sourcing, ai screening, recruiting operations, high-volume hiring, frontline hiring

**When to use this:** You manage high-volume recruiting (retail, hospitality, logistics) and are deploying AI for screening and scheduling, or you need to maintain quality while scaling.

**Big idea:** AI is reshaping frontline recruiting by automating high-volume screening and scheduling, freeing recruiters to focus on candidate experience and relationship building. The key is balancing scale with quality.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Use AI for high-volume screening but require structured resume parsing and consistency checks. Do not let AI screening be a black box.
- Automate scheduling and interview logistics completely. This removes friction from the candidate experience.
- Keep at least one human touchpoint early in the funnel to ensure candidates feel valued, not processed.
- Monitor AI screening for bias. High-volume hiring with biased screening becomes big problems at scale.
- Use AI data to understand candidate flow: where do candidates drop out? Why? Use this to improve the experience, not just move faster.

**Contrarian take:**
- High-volume recruiting under pressure to move fast often cuts corners on candidate experience. AI can speed hiring without sacrificing experience if used thoughtfully.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Audit your current high-volume hiring funnel. Where are the manual bottlenecks? Prioritise screening and scheduling automation.
- Test one AI screening tool on 100 candidates. Audit the results for accuracy and bias before scaling.
- Add a "candidate satisfaction" survey at key funnel points. Are you optimising for speed or experience? You need both.

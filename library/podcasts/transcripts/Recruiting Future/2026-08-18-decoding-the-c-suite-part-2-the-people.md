## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Layla Stonebreaker, leads People function at SmartRecruiters  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Future (Decoding the C-Suite Part 2), 18 Aug 2026
**Tags:** ai upskilling | internal mobility | recruiter training | human-ai collaboration | ai governance | psychological safety

**When to use this:** Your team resists AI tooling because they fear job replacement or don't understand how it affects their day-to-day work.

**Big idea:** Successful AI adoption in HR is about helping people experiment with safeguards and understand that AI frees them from low-value tasks to focus on judgment, complexity, and people. Bring them "along on the journey," not have things "happen to them."

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Use AI to eliminate administrative burden, not people. Layla: "How do you use AI to not only take away sort of that low value add...administrative responsibilities...but actually how do you use AI to be more sort of people-centric because now you're focusing on those sort of scarce skills, judgment, complexity, conversations with people?"
- Run AI masterclasses with leadership first. SmartRecruiters spent 50% of an offsite (one full day) on AI design training for the executive team. Leaders who understand AI can model curiosity and experimentation for their teams.
- Emphasise "staying curious" and learning mindset. Layla: "You have to stay curious...the playbook hasn't even been fully written and you don't even know what page you're going to turn to...if you don't stay on top of it, you all of a sudden you become even farther behind."
- Frame AI as augmentation, not replacement. Help people see how it expands their scope of impact, not contracts it.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Run one AI workshop with your leadership team (2 hours). Let them design a simple workflow using Claude or ChatGPT. Demystify the tool.
- Ask your team: "Which part of your job is lowest value and most repetitive?" Use AI to automate that. Celebrate the win.
- Create an experimentation sandbox where people can try AI tools without fear of "breaking things."

## Episode description

This is episode two of Decoding the C-Suite, a five part miniseries from Recruiting Future about how leadership really thinks about AI, recorded with the leadership team at SmartRecruiters (https://www.smartrecruiters.com)  seven months into their acquisition by SAP.



The headlines about AI and the workforce are relentlessly grim: surveillance, layoffs, roles disappearing. Lehua Stonebraker (https://www.linkedin.com/in/lehuastonebraker/) , who leads the people function at SmartRecruiters, starts from a different place: naming the fear rather than managing around it. In this episode she explains how to build psychological safety when employees are scared to admit they use AI, why she believes the org chart is gone, how engagement went up during an acquisition, and a vision of a connected employee lifecycle that finally uses everything an organisation learns about its people.

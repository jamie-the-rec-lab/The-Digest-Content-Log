**Tags:** ai screening, ai interviewing, interview design, ai agents, ai governance, human-ai collaboration, ta leadership, employer branding, employer storytelling

**When to use this:** you are rolling out AI screening or interviewing and worried about candidate trust, or working out how people will build judgment and stand out on employer brand once AI does the entry-level work and writes everyone's careers page copy.

**Big idea:** across six July and August episodes, AI in hiring built trust when paired with published transparency and a human still making the final call, while judgment and differentiation now have to be built deliberately, because AI removes the entry-level reps and generic copy that used to produce them by default.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Publish AI guidelines before using AI in screening or interviewing. Miracle's TA team "make transparency the foundation of all this. They publish AI guidelines," telling candidates how they would like them to use AI and how the company itself uses it.
- Keep a human owning the final call even when AI runs the first conversation. On the same Miracle rollout: "if you keep humans responsible for making the decisions, that's really how you're building that trust."
- Reframe assessment as an audition rather than an interview for a stronger signal, Zapier's approach, with the caveat (raised by the hosts) not to turn it into unpaid work for candidates.
- Assess AI fluency by ability to learn new tools rather than current knowledge. Zapier's reasoning: "what you know today is obsolete tomorrow."
- Audit your own employer brand copy against competitors the way James Ellis did: he scraped Fortune 100 career sites and found their positioning "just sounds identical," which the hosts say gives smaller companies room to stand out by naming what is actually different.
- Build judgment deliberately rather than assuming it comes from experience alone. Craig Freeman's approach is "identifying the critical judgments within each role and building assessments and development opportunities around that," including AI simulations to practise judgment.

**Contrarian take:**
- Rona Pierce argues AI in hiring can improve candidate experience when done with transparency and human oversight, calling Miracle's rollout "a pretty great case study on how AI is actually improving candidate experience contrary to what most people are saying that it's like making it worse."

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Draft and publish a short AI guidelines page for candidates before your next AI screening or interviewing rollout, stating what the AI does and what a human still decides.
- Pull five to ten competitors' career site copy and check how much of your own "why join us" messaging is interchangeable with theirs.

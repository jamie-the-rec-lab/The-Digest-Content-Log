**When to use this:** you want a quick read of what TA leaders are worried about now, or ideas for light-touch fraud checks and LLM visibility for your jobs.

**Big idea:** TA leaders are being pushed to tie their work to business goals while managing new risks (AI compliance, fraud) and preparing for candidates and employers to be represented by agents.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Define quality of hire for your organisation, not a standard. Stef Nikitas said her panel's takeaway was that it is not one size fits all, and at Ace Hardware it is a long-range measure of whether a hire makes an impact on performance and can move into leadership or another team.
- Screen for fraud with ordinary questions. Lorna Erickson recommended asking after the interview "what's a good phone number that I can reach you at", which a real candidate answers without noticing and a fraudulent one may struggle with, instead of gimmicks like asking people to touch their face.
- Build content for LLM answers. Ben Russell said SonicJobs built "career intelligence" pages (for example average salary for a lab technician) and within weeks was cited as the top source for some queries, ahead of large brands. This is a single company's early result.
- Start GEO from search data. His view is you need to know how people phrase career questions to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini before you know what to write.

- Mark Chaffey (hackajob, vendor figure): candidates introduced by Archer are five times more likely to get an interview than those applying directly through the career site, because recruiters trust Archer's recommendations. ## Episode description

TA leaders have to keep delivering for the business while they use AI to transform how hiring works. At RecFest USA in Nashville, AI was the main topic of conversation, although the people I spoke to talked about much more than the technology. So where should TA leaders be focusing their attention?



I recorded seven short interviews at the event, and they fall into three themes. Those themes are the value that talent acquisition brings to the business, the risks that employers now have to manage, and what comes next for hiring.



My guests are Jamie Leonard, Founder of RecFest; Stef Nikitas, Director of Talent Acquisition at Ace Hardware; Julia Levy, a former Fortune 500 talent acquisition executive; Jim Stroud, Head of Market Strategy and Industry Engagement at ProvenBase; Lorna Erickson, Co-Founder of Expert Interviewers; Mark Chaffey, Co-Founder and CEO of hackajob, the company behind Archer; and Ben Russell, Co-Founder and CCO of SonicJobs. In our conversations, they discuss who should own quality of hire, how employers are responding to legal risk and candidate fraud, and how AI is changing the way candidates and employers find each other.

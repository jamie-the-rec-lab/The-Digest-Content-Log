## Episode description

When employers talk about candidates using AI, the conversation is often about bulk applications, cheating or fraud. That conversation misses the sophistication of the technology being built for job seekers, some of which is changing how people find jobs and how they are matched to them. My own view is that the innovation in recruiting is being driven from the candidate side, and will continue to be, which is why TA leaders should know what is going on. So how do these tools work, and how should employers be thinking about them?



My guest this week is Aidan Cramer (https://www.linkedin.com/in/aidancramer/) , Co-Founder and CEO of AIApply (https://aiapply.co/) , a company building AI tools that help people find and apply for jobs. In our conversation, he explains how AI is being used to match people to roles, what the data shows about AI-assisted applications, where the line is between assistance and cheating, and what an agent to agent future means for employers and recruiters.



In the interview, we discuss:


     
       
  •  What are job seekers doing with AI and what is driving it?
     
     

     
       
  •  Bulk apply tools versus tools built around the quality of the match
     
     

     
       
  •  Why an AI agent needs to know when not to apply
     
     

     
       
  •  How job discovery is moving beyond keyword search to transferable skills
     
     

     
       
  •  What the data shows about interview rates for AI-assisted applications

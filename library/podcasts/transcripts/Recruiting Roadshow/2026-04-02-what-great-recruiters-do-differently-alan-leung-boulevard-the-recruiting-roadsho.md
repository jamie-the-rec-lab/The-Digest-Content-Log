**Big idea:** Talent acquisition becomes strategic when you stop talking about efficiency metrics (time to hire, cost per hire) and start speaking CFO language: revenue enablement, risk reduction, cost avoidance, and compound organisational value.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Build a "talent impact model" that shows how hiring decisions affect business outcomes: replacing a mediocre performer with a strong performer might be worth $200K in productivity gains in finance or sales roles
- Talk to your CFO about TA's role in headcount planning, retention, and internal mobility; connect talent strategy to revenue growth and risk management
- Every technology investment should be evaluated against the business outcome it enables, not just internal recruiter efficiency; a tool that speeds up hiring by 10% but improves quality-of-hire by 20% has very different business value
- When talking to hiring managers, use outcome language: "we'll staff this team with talent density so every person raises the bar" rather than "we'll fill three positions faster"

## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Nicole Sgarlato, Momentic  |  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 7 Apr 2026
**Tags:** early-stage hiring, startup hiring, recruiting operations, talent density, hiring manager alignment, recruiter productivity

**When to use this:** You're a founding recruiter at an early-stage company (seed to Series B) and need to know which foundational processes to build first, or you're scaling recruiting from founder-led hiring to professional recruiting operations.

**Big idea:** Founding recruiters must build lightweight processes (hiring rubrics, candidate debrief templates, offer checklist) that scale with the company without becoming bureaucratic, and the biggest mistake is over-hiring early (quantity over quality).

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Build three templates that scale: (1) a structured interview rubric that all interviewers use, (2) a standard debrief format so hiring managers sync quickly on candidates, (3) an offer approval checklist so founders stay aligned on comp and equity
- As founding recruiter, your job is to be an internal talent advisor to the CEO: say no to mediocre candidates even if they fill a hole, and say no to founders who want to rush hiring on a bad candidate
- Hire for talent density, not head count; every hire should raise the team's average capability, which means saying no to "90% of the way there" candidates
- Recruit for founder-team fit in early rounds because early-stage culture is fragile; misaligned hires pollute team dynamics faster than in later-stage companies

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Founding teams that hire for talent density (average interview score above 8/10) outgrow teams that hire to hit headcount targets by 30% in velocity

**Contrarian take:**
- Nicole Sgarlato argues that founding recruiters should function as culture guardians and hiring blockers, not just pipeline fillers

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Create your interview rubric (5-7 core competencies, 1-10 scoring) and share with all interviewers
- In your next all-hands, explicitly commit to hiring only candidates that "raise the bar"

## Episode description

Nicole Sgarlato, founding recruiter at Momentic, joins Brandon in San Francisco for a conversation that's equal parts career story and craft. Nicole spent ten years driving tow trucks at her family's business — while competing as a Paralympic athlete — before cold-applying to a recruiting coordinator role at Lyft in 2018 with no degree and no connections. From there she built her way through Lyft's IPO, scaled engineering teams at Retool, and landed at Momentic as employee six. The conversation covers what it takes to build a recruiting function from scratch, why candidate experience is non-negotiable even at the earliest stages, how AI is changing the top of funnel, and what it means to build an inclusive culture when you actually have a seat at the table to shape it.

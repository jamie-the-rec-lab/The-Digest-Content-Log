## Episode description

Tommy Weinert, founder of Mount Indie, joins Brandon in San Diego for a conversation about what it actually takes to recruit in one of the most high-stakes, niche markets in the industry — cleared defense and government contracting. Tommy breaks down why candidate fraud isn't just an HR headache in his world (it's a national security issue), why he believes process always beats technology, and what metrics he thinks recruiters should stop obsessing over. He also gets into how he's using AI creatively — not just as a resume parser, but to build internal tools and train better messaging models — and makes a strong case for why the future of recruiting looks a lot more like marketing than most people realize.

## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Kellie McCann, Cloop  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 14 Apr 2026

**Tags:** recruiting operations, candidate experience, recruiter workflow, onboarding, recruiting metrics

**When to use this:** You're managing the handoff between recruiting and onboarding and candidates are getting lost, ghosted, or surprised by the experience after offer acceptance.

**Big idea:** RecOps owns the candidate journey from source to day one. Clear handoffs, shared data, and aligned comms transform the candidate experience.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Audit the "gray zone". From offer acceptance to day one, who owns what? Is recruiting still involved? Does onboarding know the offer? Are comms consistent? Fix the gaps.
- Create a handoff checklist. When recruiting hands off to onboarding, what data must transfer? (personal preferences, communication style, onboarding questions, special accommodations)
- Measure handoff quality. Survey new hires: did your offer experience match your onboarding experience? If not, where did it break?

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Companies with clear recruiting-to-onboarding handoffs see 15-20 percent improvement in day-1 readiness and new hire satisfaction.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Map your current handoff process. Where does candidate data transfer from recruiting to onboarding? Where do gaps exist?
- Audit your last 5 new hires: did each one receive comms from recruiting and then onboarding? Were the messages consistent in tone and content?

## Episode description

Kelly McCann, founder of Cloop and former employer brand leader at Nike, Amazon, and Robinhood, joins the show to make the case that employer brand and recruiting operations are more intertwined than most organizations realize. Kelly brings a sharp perspective on why the candidate experience and the consumer brand are now one and the same, and why companies that treat them separately are leaving reputation on the table. The conversation gets into how AI is quietly reshaping the way candidates discover and evaluate employers through tools like ChatGPT and Google AI, often before they ever reach a career site. Kelly also shares how she thinks about the data feedback loop between rec ops and EB teams, the role of video and employee storytelling in building trust, and why leaving room for human connection in an automated process is becoming a competitive advantage.

## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Cailean Bailey, Radix  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 17 Apr 2026

**Tags:** recruiter career growth, sourcing, talent mapping, candidate communication, recruiting enablement

**When to use this:** You want to build a sourcing practice that's less dependent on job boards and more focused on relationship-building and long-term talent intelligence.

**Big idea:** Great recruiting is craft, not process. It comes from understanding people, building relationships over years, and knowing where talent lives before a role opens.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Invest in relationships before the role. The best sourcing feels effortless because you've already built trust with candidates over time.
- Use talent mapping to understand supply before you hunt. Who's in your target market? Where do they cluster? What causes them to move? Use that to build sourcing strategy.
- Source weekly, hire quarterly. Keep a continuous sourcing cadence so when roles open, you're not starting from scratch.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- List 20 people in your target market you'd love to hire (whether there's a role or not). Reach out with no ask. "I'm thinking about hiring in this space, curious if you're open to a conversation in 12 months."
- Review your sourcing: how many candidates came from your network vs. job boards? Commit to increasing network sourcing by 20 percent next quarter.

## Episode description

Cailean Bailey, talent acquisition partner at Radix, joins the show for a conversation rooted in the reality of building recruiting operations from scratch as a solo practitioner in early stage tech. Cailean brings a coach's mindset to everything from hiring manager relationships to candidate experience, making the case that the fundamentals of good recruiting have never really changed. The conversation covers why communication breakdowns are still the biggest point of failure in the hiring process, how to design ATS workflows that hiring managers will actually use, and where AI genuinely earns its place at the top of the funnel. Cailean also shares a candid take on candidate fraud, a refreshing perspective on metrics that matter, and why recruiter intuition is one of the few things AI will never be able to replicate.

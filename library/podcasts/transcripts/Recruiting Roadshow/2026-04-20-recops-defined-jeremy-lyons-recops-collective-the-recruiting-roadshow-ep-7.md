## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Jeremy Lyons, RecOps Collective  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 20 Apr 2026

**Tags:** recruiting operations, recruiting metrics, recruiter productivity, capacity planning, recruiting enablement

**When to use this:** You're building or scaling a recruiting operations function and need to understand what actually fits in RecOps vs. what's a recruiter's job.

**Big idea:** RecOps is the operational backbone that lets recruiters focus on relationships. It's not a department that handles recruiting, it's a department that enables recruiting to work.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Scope RecOps clearly: data hygiene, metric tracking, process documentation, tool management, training, and scheduling. Don't make them do sourcing or candidate screening.
- Measure what RecOps does. How much time did recruiters save by having clean ATS data? How many hours did coordinating interviews save? Make the impact visible.
- Invest in tooling slowly. RecOps should advocate for tools that 80 percent of recruiters would use daily, not nice-to-haves for 10 percent.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Write a one-page charter for your RecOps function (or pitch for one if you don't have it). What owns, what doesn't own.
- Track one metric: hours per week recruiting would spend on admin if RecOps didn't exist. Quantify the value.

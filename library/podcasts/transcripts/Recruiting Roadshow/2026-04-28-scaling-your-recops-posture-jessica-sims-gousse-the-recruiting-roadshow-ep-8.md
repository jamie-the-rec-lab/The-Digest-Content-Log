## Episode description

Jessica Sims-Gousse, the self-described "friendly neighborhood talent operations nerd," joins the show in LA with a perspective on rec ops that is equal parts technical and deeply human. With a background that spans pastry cheffing at the Ritz-Carlton, a surprise stint on Food Network, and recruiting operations roles at companies like Autodesk and Atlassian, Jessica brings a genuinely unique lens to the craft. The conversation covers why data integrity is always the first thing to break when teams scale too fast, how to approach change management with overwhelmed recruiting teams, and why the real strength of a RecOps professional has nothing to do with what systems they already know. Jessica also makes a compelling case for tracking diversity data beyond just sourcing and hires, and closes with a piece of advice that reframes the entire job: when you're building a process, design it for someone having the worst day of their career.

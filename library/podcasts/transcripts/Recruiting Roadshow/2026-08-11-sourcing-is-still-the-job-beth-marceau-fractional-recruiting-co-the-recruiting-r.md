## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Beth Marceau, Fractional Recruiting Co  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 11 Aug 2026
**Tags:** sourcing, fractional recruiting, outbound sourcing, recruiter productivity, recruiting operations

**When to use this:** When your team is overwhelmed with inbound and no one has time to source proactively.

**Big idea:** Sourcing is still the job. Inbound volume is a trap that kills proactive recruiting. The best hires come from sourcing, not screening.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Protect sourcing time. Inbound recruiting (screening applications) is necessary but not strategic. Sourcing (finding candidates no one else is looking at) is where competitive advantage lives.
- Fractional models work because they protect the boundary between sourcing and screening. One person/team does proactive sourcing; another handles inbound. Both are necessary, but they're different jobs.
- Invest in sourcing skills and tools. If your team doesn't know how to source (research, Boolean, outreach), no amount of inbound volume will matter.

**Contrarian take:**
- More inbound applications is usually a trap. It feels productive but it rewards companies with big brands. Sourcing is where smaller or less well-known companies win.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Audit this week: what percentage of your team's time went to sourcing vs screening? If screening is more than 60%, you've lost the plot.
- Block one day next week for your team to source. No inbound work. See what they find.

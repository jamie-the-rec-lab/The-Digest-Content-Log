## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Laurie David, The Fedcap Group  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 14 Aug 2026
**Tags:** hiring manager alignment, recruiter career growth, stakeholder management, recruiter training, ta leadership

**When to use this:** When hiring managers repeatedly reject your candidates or give vague feedback, or when recruiters struggle to ask the right business questions.

**Big idea:** Recruiters don't know what they don't know about the hiring manager's business problem. Close that gap and you close deals faster.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Ask the right business questions before you even think about sourcing. What's the actual business problem? Why now? What happens if you don't fill it? What's the financial impact? Most recruiters jump to "tell me about the role" without understanding the business context.
- Train your team to talk money. Hiring managers speak in business outcomes (revenue, cost, growth). Recruiters speak in features (requirements, competencies). Bridge that gap in your conversations. Tie every candidate attribute to a business outcome.
- Prepare candidates differently when you understand the business case. You'll know which of their experiences actually matter to this specific hire, and which are noise.

**Contrarian take:**
- Most recruiting conversations skip the business acumen layer and go straight to job specs. That's why candidates get rejected: the spec wasn't wrong, it was incomplete.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Before your next briefing with a hiring manager, prepare two questions: What's the business impact if this role stays empty? What would success look like financially? Listen to the answer before you start sourcing.
- Train at least one team member to shadow a business review meeting to understand what language and metrics matter in your company.

## Episode description

Laurie David, VP of Talent Acquisition at The Fedcap Group, joins Recruiting Roadshow to talk about why application volume is breaking recruiter capacity and what teams can do about it. Drawing on 20+ years of TA leadership at companies like Publicis, Randstad, and TEKsystems, Laurie covers the business acumen gap between recruiters and hiring managers, why "slow down to go fast" applies even when scaling hiring fast, the metrics that actually signal quality of hire, and where AI creates real value by cutting admin work so recruiters can focus on the conversation.

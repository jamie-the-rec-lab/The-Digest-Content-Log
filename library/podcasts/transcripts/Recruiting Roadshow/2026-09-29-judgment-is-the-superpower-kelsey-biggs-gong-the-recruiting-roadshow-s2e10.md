## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Kelsey Biggs, VP of Global Talent Acquisition at Gong, previously talent leader at Front and Square, with a strong outbound and sourcing focus. Gong is also the call-recording product she uses to coach her team, and the show is produced by Greenhouse.  ·  **Show:** Recruiting Roadshow, 29 Sep 2026
**Tags:** recruiting enablement, recruiter productivity, outbound sourcing, talent pools, quality of hire, candidate calibration, candidate fraud, human-ai collaboration, rec tech, ta leadership

**When to use this:** your inbound volume is not giving signal, your recruiters are overloaded by tools, or you want to coach recruiters on compensation conversations.

**Big idea:** recruiter judgment is the thing AI should not replace, so automate content, sourcing and scheduling and protect time for closing and hiring manager partnership.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Shift from inbound to outbound and referrals when screening cannot produce signal. Kelsey Biggs said about a year ago Gong stopped focusing on inbound because keyword matching and extra recruiter screens still gave no better signal, and up-front questions or work studies caused "massive drop offs".
- Coach on recorded calls in the manager's operating rhythm. Managers pick a few calls each week, listen to the recruiter only and leave timestamped comments such as asking why a question was not probed or noting that a call ended without any talk of compensation. She noted leaders listen while driving or walking.
- Raise total rewards early and often and use recordings for accountability, so hiring teams do not sink time into candidates whose drivers and pay expectations do not match.
- Let recruiters build agents that turn intake notes, job descriptions and notetaker output into a background scorecard of who looks like the best hire. She said it gives signals, not an answer, and starts a better conversation with the hiring manager about whether the job description or scope was wrong.
- When a recruiter's gut conflicts with the data, ask for more data. Her example was refusing to raise salaries for a role because it feels harder to hire, without backing evidence.
- Use the recruiter's view in the debrief instead of a separate culture interview, because recruiters see across all touchpoints and recordings who succeeds in a sub-team.
- Start sourcing in the ATS. She called it "wild" that teams immediately leave their candidate database for other platforms, and wants a system that reads transcripts of declined candidates and tags them automatically for future roles.

**Tools & prompts:**
- Gong call recordings for recruiter coaching, recruiter-built agents for scorecards, and a wish for automated rediscovery tagging inside the ATS.

**Contrarian take:**
- Kelsey Biggs predicted the next trend reverses the automation push, with more in-person meetings and more travel spend because candidates are not coming along on the faster, more automated journey.
- She also said sourcing and employer marketing are merging, with sourcers building prospect pools and content for 12 to 18 month relationships.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Have each TA manager listen to two recruiter calls this week and leave a timestamped comment on whether compensation was discussed.
- Before opening an external source on your next role, search your ATS for past silver medallists.

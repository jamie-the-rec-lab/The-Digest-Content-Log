**Big idea:** AI handling the first candidate screen is the inflection point where recruiting fundamentally changes; this creates opportunity (recruiters focused on closing and assessment) and risk (candidate experience degrades, bias amplifies) depending on how it's done.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- First screen AI best practices: If AI screens first, ensure candidates understand AI is involved, have a human review edge cases, and provide a human appeal process if they disagree with AI screening decision.
- Recruiter reskilling: As AI takes routine screening, redeploy recruiters to higher-value work (candidate relationship building, hiring manager partnership, offer closing) instead of eliminating roles.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- If you're deploying AI screening, communicate this clearly in your job postings and candidate communications; track candidate sentiment and experience to ensure automation isn't harming your employer brand.

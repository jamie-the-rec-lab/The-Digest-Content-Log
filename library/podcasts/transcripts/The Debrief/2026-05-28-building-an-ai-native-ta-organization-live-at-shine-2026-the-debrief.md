## Episode description

What happens when you rebuild hiring for the AI era? Recorded live at Shine, Ben Sesser, Tracy St. Dic, and Chris Abbas sit down with Scott Dicke, VP of Talent at MaintainX, to explore what an AI-native TA function looks like in practice.

In this special live episode, they discuss how recruiting teams are evolving, from coordinators becoming “Recruiting Operations Specialists” to how Zapier audits recruiter-built AI tools. Tune in to hear whether future recruiters will need traditional TA experience, why resumes may become obsolete, and how “token budgets” could reshape headcount planning.

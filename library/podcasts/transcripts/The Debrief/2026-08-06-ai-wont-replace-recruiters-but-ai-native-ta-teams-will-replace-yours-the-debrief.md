## Episode description

Every TA leader has AI on the roadmap. But deploying AI isn't the same as becoming AI-native.

More than 90% of organizations have introduced AI into their talent acquisition process, yet fewer than 5% report transformational outcomes. The tools are delivering measurable efficiency gains, from faster screening to shorter hiring cycles and improvements in quality of hire. The challenge is that most organizations are layering AI onto existing workflows instead of redesigning how talent acquisition operates.

For TA leaders, the priorities extend well beyond technology. They include deciding where AI creates the most value, preparing recruiters for changing roles, building governance before regulations catch up, earning executive confidence through measurable outcomes, and making the right long-term bets as AI becomes embedded across the hiring process.

In this episode of The Debrief, Chris, Tracy, and Ben discuss what it means to become AI-native and the leadership priorities that will separate teams using AI from teams transformed by it.

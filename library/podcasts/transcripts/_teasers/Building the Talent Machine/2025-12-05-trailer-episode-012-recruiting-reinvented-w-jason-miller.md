## Episode description

Artificial intelligence is reshaping recruiting, but the most interesting work isn’t happening on vendor decks. It’s happening inside talent teams that are building their own tools. Jason Miller is the Head of People Intelligence and AI at Natera, a genomics company operating in one of the most complex hiring environments in the world. Before this role he led recruiting at Roblox, Cloudera, and VMware, giving him a unique view into how different industries approach talent, data, and scale.

In this conversation, Jason explains why this is the first moment where talent teams can finally build instead of buy. We walk through the real paper cuts recruiters experience every day, the fragmentation of feedback and systems, and how internal AI tools can remove friction without adding headcount. Jason also shares why recruiting is still a craft, why community matters, and why this moment is a once in a generation opportunity for builders inside TA.

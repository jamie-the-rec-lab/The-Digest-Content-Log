## Episode description

People operations has spent years fighting for legitimacy. Natalie Ledbetter believes the next chapter won’t be won with frameworks or slogans, but by operating like a business.

Natalie is the Managing Partner of Ledbetter Global Advisory and a former Operating Partner and Chief People Officer at Boldstart Ventures. She works directly with Series A and B founders, helping them build people systems that actually support revenue, runway, and scale.

In this episode of Building the Talent Machine, Natalie and I cut straight to the real work. Why most people teams are still positioned as support functions. What changes when HR and TA understand customers, revenue mechanics, and deal cycles. And why influence only comes when people leaders think like operators.

We preview how Natalie rebuilt talent systems from the ground up at Stash, owning uncomfortable truths instead of spinning narratives. Interview loops, employer brand, compensation, behavioral norms, and even office design were rethought to intentionally align people, product, and revenue.

We also touch on the unglamorous reality of recruiting inside startups. The pressure. The accountability. The fact that TA is shaping who the company becomes long before the org chart catches up.

This conversation is for recruiters, people leaders, and operators who want a real seat at the table and know it has to be earned through understanding the business.

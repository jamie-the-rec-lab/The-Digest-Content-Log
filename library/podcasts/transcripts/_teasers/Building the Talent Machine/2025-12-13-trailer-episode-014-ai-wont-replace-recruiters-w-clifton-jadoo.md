## Episode description

Clifton Jadoo has spent the last decade recruiting inside some of the most well known startups in tech, including Hinge, Webflow, Wingspan, Foursquare, and Compass. He’s a recruiter who thinks like a People and Talent operator, grounded in the realities of how companies actually hire, scale, and make decisions.

In this episode, we talk about how AI is changing recruiting workflows without replacing the fundamentals. We dig into where AI creates real leverage, where it falls apart, and why judgment, context, and human connection still matter more than any tool.

We touch on why AI is both overhyped and underrated, how recruiters are using custom GPTs to move faster, why recruiters still own the final hiring call, and why trust and storytelling will outlast every technology cycle.

If you’re a recruiter trying to evolve without losing the craft, this conversation is for you.

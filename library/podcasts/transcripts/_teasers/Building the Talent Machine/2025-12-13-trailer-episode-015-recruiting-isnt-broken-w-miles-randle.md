## Episode description

Miles Randle knows what it takes to build the talent machine. 
He has been employee two. He’s built recruiting from nothing. He’s lived inside early-stage chaos and scaled teams the hard way, by doing the work instead of hiding behind tools, dashboards, or buzzwords.

In this trailer for Building the Talent Machine, Miles and I talk about what’s actually happening to recruiting as AI noise gets louder. Why AI has become a convenient excuse for weak sourcing, shallow interviews, and lazy thinking. And why the real edge recruiters still control has nothing to do with automation.

We get into pattern disruption, curiosity as a skill, and why recruiting is still a deeply human craft no matter how much tech you add on top.

What we touch on:

Why AI is an easy scapegoat for broken recruiting fundamentals

What pattern disruption really looks like in outreach and interviews

Why curiosity separates great recruiters from average ones

How early-stage recruiting and people ops bleed into each other

Why metrics and dashboards fail when you stop understanding people

Why recruiting is hard, emotional, and still worth doing well

This conversation is for recruiters, founders, and operators who feel the noise rising and want to remember what actually works.

## Episode description

Logan Marsh is a Talent Acquisition leader currently heading TA at Calendly, with nearly two decades of experience scaling recruiting teams at companies like Amazon, Okta, and DocuSign. He’s known for blending real operator experience with a sharp, data-driven perspective on AI, capacity planning, and what modern recruiting leadership actually looks like.

In this episode, Logan Marsh breaks down what truly separates transactional recruiting teams from strategic talent organizations. Host, Brandon Jeffs, and Logan go deep on how data, capacity planning, and AI are reshaping recruiter output without replacing the human layer that actually drives outcomes. Logan and Brandon challenge metrics like “quality of hire,” explain why time-to-fill is still a critical health metric, and share how elite TA leaders earn trust at the executive table. This conversation is a blueprint for recruiters who want to move from execution to influence.

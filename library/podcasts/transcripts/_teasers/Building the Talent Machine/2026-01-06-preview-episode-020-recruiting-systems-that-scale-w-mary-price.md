## Episode description

Mary Price has spent her career building and scaling talent functions inside household companies that were still becoming what they are today. Companies like Apple, LinkedIn, Slack, Abnormal AI, and OneTrust. 
She sat down to talk about what it actually means to be a builder in recruiting, how data and systems create leverage, and why human judgment remains irreplaceable even as AI reshapes the workflow. RecOps is here to stay, and Mary’s outlook and leadership in this conversation outline why this is the hottest topic in Talent Acquisition going into 2026.
Mary shares concrete examples from building amazing organizations on how Recruiting earns trust with executives, how talent acquisition connects to onboarding and development, and how strong systems prevent hiring chaos when the market shifts. She even shares a few leadership tips from none other than Steve Jobs during her time working with him.

In this episode we cover:
A clear explanation of how recruiting systems, not heroics, determine hiring outcomes at scale
Practical guidance on blending talent acquisition with talent management to influence post-hire success
A grounded perspective on where AI genuinely helps recruiters and where it cannot replace human judgment
Real examples of using data to reset hiring manager expectations and avoid endless “purple squirrel” searches
Insight into how strong recruiters build long-term talent density, not just short-term pipelines

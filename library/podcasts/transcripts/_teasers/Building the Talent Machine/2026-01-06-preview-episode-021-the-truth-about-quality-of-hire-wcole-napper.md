In this episode, Brandon sits down with Cole Napper to unpack why quality of hire keeps resurfacing as a problem recruiters are asked to solve, but rarely given the tools or authority to measure correctly. They explore how people analytics, labor market context, and executive accountability change the conversation from recruiting theater to real business outcomes.

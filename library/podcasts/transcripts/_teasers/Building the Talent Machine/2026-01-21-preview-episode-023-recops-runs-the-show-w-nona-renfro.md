## Episode description

Nona Renfro is a global talent leader who’s shaped hiring strategy at some of the world’s most influential companies, including Amazon, PayPal, Google Fiber, Meta, and Micron Technology. 

She’s built TA ops teams from the ground up and now advises AI startups like Bling on bridging recruiter reality with machine learning. Nona’s career sits at the intersection of enterprise transformation and AI innovation, and she’s one of the clearest voices on what the future of recruiting will actually look like.

Nona and I sat down to break down a reality a lot of TA leaders are living right now, recruiting with AI. We talked about why some folks keep viewing talent acquisition as low-hanging fruit for automation, while recruiters are still stuck battling the same fundamentals: broken workflows, scheduling friction, and disconnected systems. The core challenges modern RecOps teams are solving.

We also zoom in on what actually makes AI useful in recruiting: not replacing humans, but strengthening the parts of the funnel where signal is hardest to find and waste is highest. Nona shares her take on where AI can truly add leverage (especially at top of funnel), why the end-to-end agentic recruiting dream is mostly hype right now, and why recruiting teams have to redesign the candidate journey before they throw more technology at it.

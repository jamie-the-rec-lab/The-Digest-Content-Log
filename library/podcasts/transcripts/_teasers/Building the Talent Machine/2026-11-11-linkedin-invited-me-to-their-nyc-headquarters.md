## Episode description

Brandon Jeffs gets an exclusive behind-the-scenes tour of LinkedIn's New York City headquarters inside the iconic Empire State Building.

After spending more than a decade using LinkedIn as a recruiter and building his professional network, Brandon gets an invitation to visit the people behind the platform that helped shape his career.

From LinkedIn's incredible office spaces and collaborative work environments to an unexpected encounter with CEO Dan Shapero, this vlog offers a look inside one of the world's most recognizable technology companies.

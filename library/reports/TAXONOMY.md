# Tag taxonomy — reports

Tags are drawn from this fixed list so an AI can filter reliably. The report
publisher (Ashby, Gem, LinkedIn, etc.) is NOT a tag; it lives in the separate
Company field. Each report gets its publisher plus 6-10 tags from below: aim
for a mix of the metrics it covers, the themes, and the segment it measures.

## Benchmark metrics (what the report actually measures)
- time to fill
- time to hire
- offer acceptance rate
- source of hire
- cost per hire
- recruiter capacity
- recruiter productivity
- applications per hire
- interview load
- funnel conversion
- pipeline conversion
- sourcing channels
- referral hiring
- candidate ghosting
- quality of hire
- req load
- hiring volume
- attrition & retention
- internal mobility
- compensation benchmarks
- offer decline reasons
- dei

## AI (use a SPECIFIC sub-tag; reserve `ai in hiring` for meta/adoption-level reports with no single use case). Mirrors the core taxonomy so AI is queryable across podcasts, newsletters and reports alike.
- ai sourcing
- ai screening
- ai interviewing
- ai interview notes
- ai agents
- ai bias
- ai governance          (transparency, explainability, oversight, AI policy)
- human-ai collaboration (human + AI teaming, recruiter-AI roles, hiring-team alignment)
- ai adoption            (adoption/maturity of AI across the org)
- ai in hiring           (meta/general AI-in-hiring only)

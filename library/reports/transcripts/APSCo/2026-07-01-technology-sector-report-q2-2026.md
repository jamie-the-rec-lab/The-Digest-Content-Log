## Key figures
- UK tech vacancies up 4.3% year-on-year in Q2 2026 (126,861 advertised)
- AI vacancies up 41.8% year-on-year; AI Engineer roles up 74.4%
- Permanent roles were 88% of tech vacancies and rose 4.9% year-on-year
- AI adoption in recruitment firms: 34% have initiatives to trial AI tools, 32% actively integrating, 26% experimented at small scale, only 6% report tangible benefits
- Automation of CV formatting, compliance and admin saving recruiters up to 17 hours per week

## Full report
APSCo's quarterly Technology Sector Report, produced with CV-Library, tracks UK tech hiring demand. Q2 2026 showed resilience: 126,861 tech vacancies advertised, up 4.3% year-on-year, with AI demand surging (AI vacancies +41.8%, AI Engineer roles +74.4%). Permanent roles dominated at 88% of vacancies and grew 4.9%.

APSCo's parallel data on recruitment firms shows AI adoption spreading but returns lagging: 34% have initiatives to trial specific tools, 32% are actively integrating and 26% have experimented, yet only 6% report tangible benefits so far. Automation of transactional tasks (CV formatting, compliance checks, admin) is saving recruiters up to 17 hours a week, shifting the role toward higher-value work. APSCo also publishes a broader Recruitment Index tracking sector sentiment and monthly permanent/contract placement trends.

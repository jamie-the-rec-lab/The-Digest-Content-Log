## Full report

February 2024 Update: After spotting a rise in application volume from January 2021 to January 2024, Ashby examined ~14M applications. From the January 2021 average to the January 2024 average:
- 3x growth in job applications per business role (207%).
- 2.6x growth in job applications per technical role (161%).
Peak volume across all functions occurred the last week of August 2023.

The remainder is the original May 2023 analysis of 13 million applications (January 2021 - April 2023), predominantly US-based tech companies with 1-1,500 employees. Company size did not show significant differences, but sub-job-categories did.

## Benchmarking applications per job
Focus on inbound Applications per Job per Week — answering "How many inbound applications should I expect when I open a job?" Application per Job counts direct applications through the company website and job boards (excludes referrals and sourced candidates).

## Key takeaways
- Application rates per week increased ~3x from January 2021 - April 2023 across business and tech roles.
- The strongest peak occurs in the first week of a job posting, followed by a steady state of heightened rates.
- The average of the first four weeks can proxy for later expected volume.
- Benchmarks inform strategies for managing inbound (thresholds/blocking, auto-reject, advanced filtering).

## Application rates per job / per week increased ~3x
Business roles experienced an earlier uptick in 2022 than tech roles. Believed drivers: reduction in total roles available and ~150,000 people in US tech impacted by layoffs in 2022.

## How to manage increased volume
Recommendations: start with a strong job posting; set up a timeline/priority ranking; establish thresholds (first week is 2-2.5x higher than following weeks) with automated blocking and auto-reject; rely on advanced filters; add application question checkpoints; leverage social media; track results against your own baseline.

## First week is consistently 2.5-3x higher than following weeks
Inbound rates show a strong first-week peak of 2.5-3x, then a sharp decline to a steady state. Pattern holds across job categories by year. Considered drivers: visibility, limited application window, novelty, early-bird advantage.

## Little variation by company size; stark difference by role type
Inbound rates by job category changed dramatically over the years. Support, Success, and Recruiting roles stand out with significant increases in application volume.

## Wrap up
Metrics Ashby planned to watch in 2023: time to offer; time to hire and candidate experience; interviews per hire; passthrough rates by role category; qualified candidate trends; DEI passthrough rates by stage; recruiter productivity.

- Average survey response rate ~17.8% (steady 16-19% July 2022-Sept 2023).
- 94% of responses come within nine days of receiving the survey.
- Response rate by length: 1-5 questions 20.1%; 6-10 questions 20.1%; 11+ questions 18.2%.
- Response rate by anonymity: Do Not Allow Anonymous 20.6% (highest); Allow Anonymous 17.7%; Enforce Anonymous 15.3% (lowest).

## Full report

Data comes from 67.4K survey requests sent and 11.9K survey responses received between January 2022 and October 2023 through Ashby Candidate Experience Surveys.

## Average Response Rates
Average response rate for the last year was roughly 17.8%, steady between 16% to 19% from July 2022 to September 2023. 94% of responses come within nine days of receiving the survey.

### 4 Stats on Candidate Survey Response Rates
- Insight 1 (length): 1-5 questions 20.1%; 6-10 questions 20.1%; 11+ questions 18.2%. Up to 10 questions leads to highest response rate.
- Insight 2 (anonymity): Allow Anonymous 17.7%; Do Not Allow Anonymous 20.6% (highest); Enforce Anonymous 15.3% (lowest).
- Insight 3 (stage): response rates lowest for candidates cut at initial screen, highest for hired candidates.
- Insight 4: benchmark response rates by function/department rather than overall average.

### 3 Lessons from Notion's High Response Rates
- Lesson 1: Intentionally customize survey questions tied to recruiting expectations.
- Lesson 2: Add free-text questions for richer insight.
- Lesson 3: Automate survey send at a 3-day delay after archive/hire.

## Candidate NPS Benchmarks
NPS refresher: 0-6 detractors, 7-8 passives, 9-10 promoters. Average Candidate NPS found to be 37. (Retently B2B NPS averages 39-67; Qualtrics consumer NPS -7.1 to 28.)

### 2 Initial Candidate NPS Findings
- Finding 1 (by stage): active candidates past 1+ interview stage average 28 NPS; candidates who receive the offer average 62 (excellent); hired candidates average 85.
- Finding 2 (time in process): candidates 15-59 days show minimal variance; 60+ days shows noticeable decline — 54.9% drop for candidates rejected at onsite after 60+ days, 16.5% drop for hired candidates in process beyond 60 days.

### 5 Tips from Talent Teams with Strong Candidate NPS
Top three Ashby customers by NPS: M-KOPA, Gorgias, Notion.
- Tip 1: Empower a recruiter to own candidate experience (Gorgias dedicates 20% of recruiter time).
- Tip 2: Send surveys at various hiring stages / cohorts.
- Tip 3: Dig into factors driving NPS change (filter by Department, Outcome, Recruiter).
- Tip 4: Foster a culture of constructive accountability (monitor NPS by recruiter).
- Tip 5: Determine the right time to send (Notion sends all surveys after the onsite stage).

## Conclusions
Most candidates respond within nine days. Survey design depends on strategy, with some preference toward up to 10 questions and non-anonymous responses. Candidate NPS is generally an under-analyzed metric.

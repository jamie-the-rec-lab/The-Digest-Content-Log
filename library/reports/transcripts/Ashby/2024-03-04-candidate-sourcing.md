## Key figures
- Average sourcing email open rate: 86% (consistent Jan 2022-Jan 2024, business and technical).
- Average Campaign Reply Rate: 19.6%; 30-40% higher in 2023 than 2022.
- Reply rate by function: business 18.7%, technical 19.4%.
- Reply rates plateau around 23% beyond three emails in a sequence.
- Average interest rate across campaigns: 37.7%.
- Most recruiters set four days between emails; reply peak for campaigns sent 12 PM-6 PM local.
- Social sourcing: reviewed 5 profiles to add 4 in 2022; 6 to add 4 in 2023.
- AI personalization lift: ~46% higher reply rate (35.3% with AI vs 24.1% without).

## Full report

Data comes from over 500K email sequences sent through Ashby between January 2022 and January 2024. Sourcing provides recruiting teams greater control over their pipeline. Goals: benchmark open, reply, and interest rates; uncover campaign trends; understand how AI streamlines efficiency.

## Sourcing Outreach Benchmarks
Definitions: Sourcing campaign = set of one or more emails; Email open = at least one opened; Email reply = at least one responded; Interest rate = a response classified as showing interest.

### Average Sourcing Open Rates
Consistent open rate averaging 86% from January 2022 through January 2024, consistent across business and technical.

### Average Reply Rates
Focus on Campaign Reply Rate (candidates who replied to any email in the sequence). Average 19.6% response rate Jan 2022-Jan 2024. Response rates jumped at the start of 2023 and remained 30-40% higher in 2023 than 2022. By function: business 18.7%, technical 19.4%.

### Reply & Interest Rate by Campaign Setup
Most sequences send three emails; reply rate steps up with at least one additional email but plateaus around 23% beyond three. Almost half of all replies respond to the first email. Average 37.7% interest rate across campaigns, peaking for those who respond after the first email. Most recruiters set four days between emails.

### Outreach Rates by Job Category
Legal and Recruiting candidates more likely to reply; Finance and Engineering less likely. Little variance by industry or company size.

### Outreach Rates by Day or Time
Saturday lowest for open rate. Fairly consistent reply rate across time buckets, slight peak for campaigns sent 12 PM-6 PM local to campaign creator. Peak sourcing activity roughly 10 AM to 4 PM.

## Trends in Sourcing Campaigns
Outreach campaign volume swung down in the second half of 2022, bottoming in the first half of 2023, with an uptick in the second half of 2023 (sharper for technical roles). Possible drivers: uptick in job openings, team expansion (Ramp doubled its sourcing team in 2023), higher-level/senior hiring.

### Sourcing by job category vs inbound
Engineering has high sourcing activity. Customer Support has the lowest sourcing investment but highest inbound in 2023. Sales is driven far more by outbound sourcing than inbound.

### Sourcing on social platforms
Sourcers review an extra profile for every four candidates added: 5 profiles to add 4 in 2022; 6 to add 4 in 2023. Blacksmith reviewed ~100 profiles for a typical role but closer to 5,000 for a Sales Director role.

## AI in Sourcing
Ashby released AI tokens in the first half of 2023 for personalization. Campaigns using AI personalization saw a ~46% lift in reply rate (35.3% vs 24.1%). At Liveflow, AI personalization enabled each team member to reach 150 people per week with higher response rates.

## Conclusions
Email open and reply rates generally remain stable over time. Sourcing remains essential alongside growing inbound. Personalizing emails considerably increases reply rates while saving time.

## Full report

This report analyzes 230K applications that reached the offer stage from January 2021 through March 2024 to benchmark Offer Acceptance Rate (OAR) by function, industry, and more.

### How We Looked at OAR
OAR is calculated based on candidates entering the offer stage and evaluating their final outcome, controlling for when teams create offers and for organization-based rejection reasons.

### Report Highlights
- 2023 exhibited the highest average OAR at 81%.
- Time in Offer Stage dipped 16% in Q3 2023, from ~3 days to ~2.5 days.
- A peak in rejections coming from the organization (vs the candidate) from Q4 2022 to Q3 2023.

## Offer Acceptance Rate Benchmarks
Across the three-year period, average OAR is 78%. By calendar year: 77% (2021), 76% (2022), 81% (2023). The 2023 OAR of 81% is the highest in years analyzed to date, particularly in the last two quarters of 2023 as total roles dwindled.

### OAR by Function
- Technical roles: 73% average OAR.
- Business roles: 84% average OAR (11 percentage points higher).
The gap was most prominent Q2 2021 to Q2 2022. Engineering candidates are less likely to open outbound sourcing, yet Engineering sees high sourcing volume.

### OAR by Industry
Only small differences by industry: Business Services 82%, Health Technology 80%, SaaS and Cloud 79%, Financial Technology 77%, Online Marketplaces 76%, Consumer Apps 73%, Media & Entertainment 72%.

## Time In Offer Stage
Time in Offer was generally stable at 3 days until Q2 2023, then a 16% drop to 2.5 days (an average).

### By Accepted vs Rejected
- Accepting candidates: Time in Offer ~2.8 days until Q2 2023, then fell to two days.
- Declining candidates (archived): ~6 days.
- OAR increases as Time in Offer decreases.

### Business vs Technical Roles
- Business roles: consistent ~3 days.
- Technical roles: consistently ~4 days, at least a day more.

### Relationship with OAR
An inverse relationship in recent quarters: as days in offer stage decrease, overall OAR increases.

## Offer Declines by Reason
Offer rejections primarily come from candidates. A peak in roles rescinded from the organization from Q4 2022 through Q3 2023, aligning with industry-wide layoffs. By function:
- Offers rescinded by the organization represent 17% of declined offers for technical roles.
- Offers rescinded by the organization represent 27% of declined offers for business roles.
Companies are much less likely to rescind an offer for a technical role than a business role.

## Offers Accepted by Source
The fewest hires come from internal, agencies, and external job boards; the most come from inbound applications (growing fastest since Q1 2023); sourcing and referrals sit in the middle. Inbound as a source of hires trends massively upward over the three-year period.

## Conclusions
OAR fluctuated alongside the macro hiring environment. Industries with the most technical hiring correlate with lowest OAR. A clear trend of more job offers coming from inbound applications.

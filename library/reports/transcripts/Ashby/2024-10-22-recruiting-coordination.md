## Full report

This report maps the world of recruiting coordination using proprietary ATS data based on interview scheduling for over 2.8M candidates who get at least one interview, across over 5.1M interview events.

### Report Highlights
- Talent teams at organizations with 500-10,000+ employees are getting leaner with coordinators while the work remains roughly the same.
- Scheduling through Ashby can be done, on average, 40% sooner than through legacy ATS systems.
- Average scorecard submission rates were 84-89%, with the majority submitted within two hours.
- The largest opportunity for faster coordination is the time between when events are scheduled and when the first event begins.

## Part 1: Defining Recruiting Coordination

An initial review of 50 recruiting coordinator job descriptions surfaced the most-mentioned skills/criteria. Organizations take one of a few shapes: (A) Traditional RC Org Structure; (B) Distributed RC Org Structure (RC capacity limited, work distributed across recruiters); (C) No RCs in Org Structure (recruiters, hiring managers, or technology handle coordination).

### Volume of Recruiting Coordinators per Organization Dropped Starting in 2022
Coordinators Used per Organization fell from a high of ~4 RCs per Org in Q1 2022 to a low of 2 by end of 2023. Schedules Created per Org and Coordinators Used per Org both trended down, while Schedules per Coordinator rose over the same period (more work per coordinator).

Broken out by company size:
- <100 employees: metrics mostly flat with a slight downward trend; averaged ~1.4 coordinators (Q1 2021-Q2 2024).
- 100-500 employees: ~2.7 coordinators (Q1 2021-Q2 2022) dropping to ~1.7 (Q3 2022-Q2 2024); Schedules per Coordinator ticked up slightly in 2024.
- 501-10,000+ employees: sharpest drop in Coordinators per Org in 2022; Schedules per Org picked up in 2023; Schedules per Coordinator rapidly rose from Q2 2023, signaling much more scheduling work per coordinator.

### Recruiters to Recruiting Coordinator ratio
Recruiting Coordinators worked with ~4 Recruiters on average (Q1 2020-Q2 2024). The number went from ~5 (start of 2021) to ~4 (end of 2022), trending up slightly in 2023.

### RC Weekly Scheduling Range
Bottom 25th percentile: coordinators managing 14 schedules per week; 90th percentile: 47 schedules per week.

## Part 2: Stages of the Coordination Process

Coordination work sits in three buckets: (1) Single Event Scheduling; (2) Onsite Coordination; (3) Assessment and Debriefs.

### 1. Single Event Scheduling
- Recruiters are associated with the majority of schedules created (average 70%).
- Recruiting Coordinators ~26.1%; Hiring Managers ~3.7%.
- Single-event interviews: recruiters manage 76% of schedules, RCs 21%. Multi-event: recruiters 21%, RCs 67%.
- 50% of candidates provide availability within 24 hours.
- ~65% of candidates go from availability request to first interview completed within a week; majority within two weeks; last 8-10% linger beyond two weeks.
- Interviews start a median 6 days after availability is requested (median five days inclusive of weekends).

### 2. Onsite Coordination
- 54% of single-event interviews are scheduled within 1 day (excluding direct booking) vs 34% of panel interviews.
- Panel interviews often occur a day later than single-event interviews.

### 3. Assessments and Debriefs
Metrics: Scorecard Submission Rate (proportion submitted before the next stage change) and Hours to Submission.
- Submission rates 84-89%, majority within a median of two hours (flat Q1 2021-Q3 2024).
- Ashby users: ~90% completion, median 1.9 hours. Legacy ATS: ~84% completion, median 2.3 hours — legacy is 21% slower.

## Part 3: Where New Technology Drives Efficiency

### Direct Booking Links
- With Ashby's Direct Booking Links, 72% of single-event interviews get scheduled within one day of requesting availability vs 54% without — 33% sooner.

### Scheduling Speed vs Legacy
- 4 days from interview event created to scheduled in Ashby vs 5.6 days in legacy — scheduling is on average 40% faster (~1.5 days faster).

### The Human Element / Interview Notice
- Interviews conducted with one day of notice see a 68.2% passing scorecard rate vs 63.2% with seven days of notice (passing = score of 3 or 4 on Ashby's 1-4 scale).

## Conclusions
Technology streamlines coordination (Direct Booking Links help 72% of single-event interviews get scheduled within one day). Scheduling with Ashby is ~1.5 days faster than legacy systems. Panel interviews occur a day later than single-event interviews and require the most human involvement.

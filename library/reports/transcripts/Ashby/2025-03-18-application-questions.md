- The number of questions per application has nearly doubled in the past four years.
- Even with more questions, the average rate of questions answered is relatively stable.
- Roughly half (46.8%) of application forms in 2024 included a long-form question.

## Full report

The dramatic increase in application volume has correlated with applicants being asked to answer more questions per job. This report analyzes over 4.8M applications from January 2021 to December 2024.

The number of questions per application has nearly doubled in the past four years. Even with significantly more questions, the average rate of questions answered is relatively stable. (This data only considers submitted applications, not abandoned ones.) Jobs with more questions tend to have more optional questions, which could be a factor.

Looking at all job applications in 2024, roughly half (46.8%) include long-form questions.

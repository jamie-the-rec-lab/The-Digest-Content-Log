# How common are ghost jobs?

- **Source:** Ashby
- **Date:** 2025-04-08
- **URL:** https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/ghost-jobs
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** 22,000+ jobs, January 2021 to December 2024
- **Extraction:** full

## Key figures
- 82% of open jobs on Ashby in 2024 were filled (led to a real hire); 18% not filled.
- Of unfilled: 5.5% paused, 1% had an offer but no hire, 3.5% no reason given.
- At enterprise orgs, 89% of open jobs were filled in 2024.
- Among companies with 50+ employees, 97.5% of jobs move at least some candidates to an active interview stage.
- Cited external stat: a May 2024 Resume Builder survey found 4 in 10 companies posted ghost job listings last year.

## Full report

Ghost job boards are job posts from real companies for openings that don't exist (distinct from fake job boards, which are recruiting scams). Reasons include future roles opened early, roles closed/on hold for budget, impressing investors, gauging the talent market, team direction changes, or posting multiple jobs but hiring for one. A May 2024 Resume Builder survey found 4 in 10 companies posted ghost job listings last year.

This report analyzes over 22,000 jobs from January 2021 to December 2024 using real job data.

From Ashby's customer base in 2024:
- 82% of open jobs were filled (led to a real hire).
- 18% were not filled: 5.5% paused, 1% had an offer made but no hire, 3.5% no reason given.

About 1 in 5 jobs close without a fill. Larger organizations tend to be more stable; smaller companies see more variation. At enterprise orgs, 89% of open jobs were filled in 2024.

A job closing without a hire doesn't mean it's a ghost job. Among companies with 50+ employees, 97.5% of jobs move at least some candidates to an active interview stage, indicating almost all jobs represent a real investment of time. Most companies have a high fill rate.

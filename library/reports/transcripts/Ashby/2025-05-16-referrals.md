# Are referred candidates more likely to get hired?

- **Source:** Ashby
- **Date:** 2025-05-16
- **URL:** https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/referrals
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** 38M+ applications across 93K jobs, January 2021 to December 2024
- **Extraction:** full

## Key figures
- Application source mix (2021-2024 avg): inbound 93.8%, sourced 4.8%, referral 1%, agency 0.4%, internal 0.05%.
- Referral applications fell from 2% in Q1 2021 to less than 1% by Q1 2024.
- Internal: 42% application-to-interview; 32% interview-to-offer.
- Referred: 40% application-to-interview; 16% interview-to-offer.
- Agency: 42% application-to-interview; 8% interview-to-offer.
- Inbound offer rate fell ~70% (7 in 1,000 to 2 in 1,000), the largest decrease of any source.

## Full report

This report analyzes over 38M applications across 93K jobs from January 2021 to December 2024, across three stages: applications, interviews, and offers.

## Applications: Where are applications coming from?
Between the start of 2021 and end of 2024, average source mix: 93.8% inbound, 4.8% sourced, 1% referral, 0.4% agency, 0.05% internal. Referral applications decreased over time, from 2% in Q1 2021 to less than 1% by Q1 2024, while inbound increased.

## Interviews: Which sources are selected to interview?
Internal, referred, and agency candidates were more likely to get interviews than inbound applicants — and be hired after an interview.
- Internal: 42% go application to interview; of those interviewed, 32% go to offer (some hired without a formal interview).
- Referred: 40% go application to interview; of those interviewed, 16% go to offer.
- Agency: 42% go application to interview; of those interviewed, 8% go to offer.

## Offers: What is the source of hire for closed roles?
Offer rates decreased among all application sources except internal applicants (the least common source, <1% of applications). Inbound applications experienced a ~70% reduction in offer rates (7 in 1,000 to 2 in 1,000), the largest decrease of any source, correlating with inbound volume tripling. Inbound applicants remain the dominant source of all hires made, consistent with more scrutiny and an employer's market.

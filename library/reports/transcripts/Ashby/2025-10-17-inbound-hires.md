# How many hires really come from inbound?

- **Source:** Ashby
- **Date:** 2025-10-17
- **URL:** https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/inbound
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** Nearly 250K hires between January 2021 and June 2025, across inbound, referral, sourced, agency, and internal sources

## Full report

Inbound has consistently dominated as a source of hires, accounting for between 43% and 52% of all hires over the past four years. This report analyzes nearly 250K hires between Jan 2021 and June 2025.

### 1. Smaller companies vary widely in source of hire.
Inbound averages around 50% across all company sizes, but the average hides a wide spread. Small organizations show a "dumbbell" pattern (some heavily inbound, others heavily alternative). Enterprise organizations converge closer to the middle; about 10% of 500-10,000 employee companies hire 70%+ of roles from inbound.

### 2. Inbound applicants make up the majority of hires.
In Q2 2025, inbound hires hit their highest point in four years at 52% of all hires, a +9 percentage point increase vs Q4 2022. Sourced (~16%) and referral (~18%) hires held steady; agency hires declined slightly; internal hires are rising but still a small fraction.

### 3. When inbound is low, sourcing dominates.
For jobs with few inbound applications, sourcing is the top driver of hires. As inbound applications increase, inbound hires increase while sourcing decreases in share. Referrals stay consistent at ~17% regardless of inbound volume.

### Adjacent findings
- Executive roles are less likely filled through inbound (~35%).
- Tech jobs skew toward sourcing (24%) compared to business roles (18%).
- Business jobs skew more inbound-heavy than tech.

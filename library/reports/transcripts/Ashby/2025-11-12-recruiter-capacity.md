# What is a recruiter's capacity?

- **Source:** Ashby
- **Date:** 2025-11-12
- **URL:** https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/capacity
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** 26M+ interactions and 100K jobs from thousands of talent professionals, November 2024 to August 2025
- **Extraction:** full

- At peak capacity (90th percentile, week level): RCs interact with 27 jobs; Recruiters & Sourcers interact with 14 jobs.

## Full report

Recruiter capacity was defined by examining weekly data from thousands of talent professionals: over 26M interactions and 100K jobs (November 2024 to August 2025). Interactions include app review, emails mappable to a job, interviews with accepted/tentative status, and interviews scheduled. Capacity = active jobs a talent professional worked on that week. Findings focus on the 90th percentile at the recruiter/week level.

### 1. As companies grow, talent teams handle more jobs in parallel.
The most capacity efficiencies are seen with Recruiting Coordinators, who can handle 3x more jobs at an enterprise org than at an org with fewer than 100 employees.

### 2. Recruiting Coordinators handle the most jobs in any given week.
At peak capacity (90th percentile): RCs interact with 27 jobs; Recruiters & Sourcers interact with 14 jobs. At Mapbox, interview volume jumped 150% within a year after a $280M Series E, prompting expansion to four coordinators and adoption of Ashby Auto Scheduling.

### 3. Talent professionals are handling more jobs than nine months ago.
Across the last nine months, the busiest RCs went from managing ~22 jobs per week to closer to 27. A prior report showed Ashby automations schedule interviews 40% faster than legacy systems.

### 4. Capacity is relatively steady through the year, outside the holiday season.
The 90th percentile of jobs handled per week holds steady across the year with an expected holiday dip, though individual recruiter experiences are more variable. About a third of talent professionals reached the 90th percentile at least once during the year, and over half of all organizations had someone hit that mark.

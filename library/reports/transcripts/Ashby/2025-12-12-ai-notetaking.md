- **Source:** Ashby
- **Date:** 2025-12-12
- **URL:** https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/ai-notetaking
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** Interviews from early adopters of Ashby AI Notetaker (released September 2025) vs interviews conducted without it
- **Extraction:** full

- Within 5 hours of an interview, scorecard submission rates are ~9% higher when AI Notetaker is used.
- Within 24 hours, ~6% more scorecards submitted with AI Notetaker (70% vs 66%).
- Interviews using AI Notetaker resulted in scorecards with ~44% more words.

## Full report

In September 2025, Ashby released its AI Notetaker to record, transcribe, and summarize interviews. To gauge early impact, interviews from early adopters were compared to interviews without it. Results show improvements in both speed and depth of scorecards.

## 1. AI Notetaker Speeds Up Scorecard Submission
- Within 5 hours of an interview: scorecard submission rates are ~9% higher with AI Notetaker.
- Within 24 hours: ~6% more scorecards submitted (70% vs 66%).
These early windows matter because scorecards that slip past the first day often roll into the next morning, adding 12-24 hours of lag unrelated to true evaluation time. Beyond scorecards, recordings make debriefs more tangible and can let assessment happen off the recording instead of adding extra interview rounds.

## 2. Scorecards with AI Notetaker Include More Detail
Interviews using AI Notetaker resulted in scorecards with ~44% more words. While word count alone isn't quality, the increase reflects transcribed specifics and direct quotes, provided without sacrificing focus on the candidate.

Early signals: when interviewers stay focused on candidates and rely on AI for the mechanics, teams move faster, feedback improves, and decisions become more grounded in shared context.

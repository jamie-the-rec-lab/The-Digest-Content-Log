# Did hiring pick up in 2025?

- **Source:** Ashby
- **Date:** 2026-01-30
- **URL:** https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/2025-hiring
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** Fixed cohort of hundreds of companies across eight consecutive quarters spanning 2024 and 2025
- **Extraction:** full

## Key figures
- All four quarters of 2025 featured at least 11% more hiring than the baseline (Q1 2024).
- Smaller companies (<100 employees) reduced quarterly hiring by as much as 25% vs baseline.
- Companies with 250-1,000 employees: hiring nearly unchanged year over year.
- EMEA hiring increased just 2% from 2024 to 2025.
- US unemployment rose to a four-year high in late 2025 even as job gains continued.

## Full report

This report examines a fixed cohort of hundreds of companies across eight consecutive quarters spanning 2024 and 2025, tracking relative changes in hiring behavior. Findings: hiring did increase in 2025, though unevenly. Larger companies drove most of the growth while smaller startups remained conservative; North America (AMER) consistently outpaced EMEA.

## 1. Hiring Was Up in 2025
- Each quarter of 2025 featured more hiring than the same quarter the year prior.
- All four quarters of 2025 featured at least 11% more hiring than baseline (Q1 2024).
Even as hiring rose on average, many companies scaled back recruiting goals or became more judicious.

## 2. Larger Companies Fueled the Rise in Hiring
Using Q1 2024 as baseline across eight quarters:
- Smaller companies (<100 employees): hiring declined in all four quarters of 2025, reducing quarterly hiring by as much as 25% vs baseline.
- More mature startups (101-200 employees) and mid-sized companies (201-1001): hiring increased in 2025 but fluctuated quarter to quarter; for 250-1,000 employees, hiring was nearly unchanged YoY.
- Companies over 1,001 employees: expanded hiring steadily from start of 2024 through end of 2025 and were consistently more aggressive.

## 3. EMEA Lagged Behind AMER in Hiring
- Hiring among EMEA-based companies increased by just 2% from 2024 to 2025.
- AMER gains were more consistent and larger in magnitude.
- For smaller companies the AMER-EMEA gap was small; as company size increases, the gap widened steadily, with larger organizations driving most of the divergence.

Hiring in 2025 increased relative to 2024 but selectively and unevenly. Larger companies drove most of the gains while smaller startups remained cautious. Hiring remains fundamentally different from the post-2020 boom years, with teams interviewing significantly more candidates per hire.

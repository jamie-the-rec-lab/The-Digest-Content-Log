## Full report

This report analyzes over 109M applications and 247K jobs from January 2021 through March 2026.

### Report Highlights
- High-Volume Baseline: Applications per Hire tripled from 2021 to 2024 and remained above 300 throughout 2025.
- Recruiter Resilience: Hires per Recruiter have recovered from 2023 lows, with the average recruiter reaching ~7 hires per quarter.
- Stronger Conversion Rates: Candidates reaching the interview stage convert to offers at rates surpassing 2021 levels.
- The Interview Intensity Gap: Technical hiring averages nearly twice the interview hours and one additional interview event vs business roles.
- Stabilized Timelines: Time to First Fill settled at 8 weeks (business) and 10 weeks (technical).

## Part 1: The Recruiter Experience

### Application volumes remain elevated
- Applications per Hire tripled from 2021 to 2024 and remain elevated.
- Throughout 2025, every hire required more than 300 applications on average.
- The average recruiter today processes 291 applications per hire, compared to roughly 100 in early 2021.

### Hires per Recruiter has rebounded
- After bottoming out at 4.5 hires per quarter in early 2023, the average recruiter reached 7.3 hires per quarter by Q1 2026.
- By 2023 both Applications per Hire and Hires per Recruiter increased together.

### This rebound varied by Role Type
- Business recruiting led the rebound, increasing from a low of 3.3 hires per quarter in late 2022 to 4.3 by early 2024.
- By Q4 2024 the gap reached a multi-year low: business recruiters averaging 4.1 hires per quarter vs 3.4 for technical.
- By Q1 2026, business hiring reached a 5-year high of 5 hires per recruiter; technical stabilized at 3.8.

## Part 2: Interview Intensity

### Teams are interviewing 52% more applicants
- Business roles average 11.7 interviews per hire, a 36% increase from 2021 (~8).
- Technical roles average 17.6 interviews per hire, a 52% increase from 2021 (~11).

### Data roles have the most applications Interviewed per Hire (Q1 2025-Q1 2026)
- Data roles average 19.5 per hire, Product Management 18, Engineering 17.9.
- Customer Support roles average just 9.5, the lowest.
- HR roles sit at 14.3.

### Technical roles average nearly twice the interview hours than business roles
- By Q1 2026, the average technical hire averaged 23.3 hours of interview time vs 12.2 hours for business — more than 11 additional hours per hire.
- Both numbers have stabilized since mid-2023.

### Customer Support roles have the fewest Interview Hours per Hire
- Data roles top the chart at 24.9 hours per hire, Engineering 24.7, Product Management 23.5.
- Customer Support sits at 8.9 hours, a more than 3x difference.

### Technical interview time is far less predictable
- Approximately 23% of technical hires average 5+ hours of interview time, compared to 6% of business hires.

## Part 3: Funnel Conversion and The Candidate Experience

### Candidates are roughly 50% less likely to receive an interview than five years ago
- In 2021, approximately 7-8% of applications resulted in an interview. Today between 3.6% and 4.7% depending on role type. Rising volume and fraudulent applications drive this.

### Once in the process, candidates are more likely to receive an offer
- After a low of 3.9% (technical) and 5% (business) in early 2023, offer conversion rates climbed.
- By Q1 2026, business roles reached 10.4% and technical roles 7.3%, surpassing 2021 levels.

### The candidate's time investment has remained stable
- Business candidates average ~2.4 hours of interviews before an offer; technical ~3.5 hours. Both flat since 2021.

## Part 4: Hiring Velocity and Timelines

Preferred lens is Time to First Fill (days from job opened to first hire).
- Median Time to First Fill: 56 days business, 76 days technical (roughly 8-10 weeks).
- Interview events per hire: technical 5, business 4 (one extra round adds ~a week).
- Median Time to Hire: business 30 days (75% within 46 days), technical 40 days (75% within 57 days).
- Time to Hire (application to accepted offer): technical ~48 days, business ~38 days.
- 75% of business hires happen within 50 days; technical within 60 days.

## Part 5: Sourcing and Candidate Origin

### The majority of hires are inbound applications
- Inbound has accounted for more than 50% of all hires since Q1 2023.
- By Q1 2026, inbound accounts for 52% of all hires, up from 38% in early 2021.
- Sourced and referral hires have both declined as a share.
- The remaining 14% of hires come from sources such as agencies and internal transfers.

## Conclusions
Applications per Hire tripled from 2021 to 2024 and remained above 300 throughout 2025. Hires per Recruiter recovered to ~7 per quarter. Candidates reaching the interview stage convert to offers at rates surpassing 2021. Technical hiring remains resource-intensive (nearly twice interview hours, one more interview event). Time to First Fill settled at 8 weeks (business) and 10 weeks (technical). The interview experience for hired candidates is largely unchanged; the increased burden landed on recruiting teams.

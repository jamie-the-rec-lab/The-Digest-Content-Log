## Full report

### Introduction
The recruitment industry has been shrinking, with firms showing declining revenue for three years according to the GRID 2025 Industry Trends Report. A slow economy and the "Great Stay" mean low job activity at the same time that record numbers of candidates, assisted by AI, are applying for every open role. It is harder than ever for recruiters to identify the right candidates before AI-assisted job boards beat them to it, while individual candidates struggle to get a response to the hundreds of applications they send. The result: the talent experience is getting worse, even as recruiters work harder. Bullhorn surveyed nearly 2,800 people who worked with recruitment firms in the past three years.

### Only one-third of talent are working with recruiters
35% of candidates surveyed most frequently turn to recruitment firms to find jobs, down from 45% last year — versus 66% who use LinkedIn, Indeed, or other job boards. Professional candidates are the most likely to use recruitment firms (40%), commercial candidates 33%, and healthcare 30%. Only 26% of Gen Z candidates choose to work with recruiters. The recommended response: invest in AI that works non-stop in the background to amplify and accelerate recruiters. About half of the talent pool have already interacted with AI as part of their job search, and 77% had a positive experience.

### AI is changing the standard for speed and responsiveness
Three-quarters of candidates have stopped working with a recruiter because the process was too long or communication was lacking, and 16% never heard back that their application was received.

**AI eliminates the application "black hole":** Candidates overwhelmingly say getting a response to every application submitted will be AI's biggest benefit, with 32% calling this the greatest game-changer and another 13% citing knowing where they stand — together 45% who feel AI addresses the black-hole feeling. 27% say AI moves them through the process quickly and 10% say AI helps them figure out if they are a good fit. Concerns center on not reaching a real person or being screened out by inaccurate algorithms.

**Candidates appreciate the quick response only AI enables:** The number one reason candidates appreciated a chatbot screening was an immediate response; second was moving quickly to the next stage. More than three-quarters cited a positive experience because of AI-driven speed and responsiveness. 54% of candidates gave up on a recruiter because the process was too slow or communication too sparse.

**AI voice agents are at least as good as people in early screenings:** 73% of candidates had a positive interview experience with an AI voice agent, the top reason being that the interaction felt natural and "human." 88% rated the AI interview as equal to or better than a live interview.

**AI increases candidate loyalty 30–45%:** Use of AI at all results in a 30% jump in loyalty; a positive AI experience boosts loyalty by 45%.

### Slow and inconsistent processes are turning candidates away
When asked what is most important in the talent experience, candidates ranked the right job opportunities, number of job opportunities, and fast placement highest. Satisfaction with speed and responsiveness of recruiters fell nearly 20% year over year. Complicated forms and technology contribute to dissatisfaction (forms too long, too many clicks, too few notifications). Dissatisfaction with communication during interviews falls from 40% to 26% for candidates who interacted with an AI voice agent.

**Loyalty gains at every stage:** 85% of candidates whose onboarding was automated would keep working with their recruiter; fewer than 50% of firms are automating many recruitment stages, so easy wins remain (in some cases a 60% loyalty increase). Frequent communication (more than once a week) can raise loyalty as much as 80%.

**Upskilling and hours:** About half of candidates have participated in reskilling/upskilling programs; 91% who did would continue with their firm vs 63% who didn't. 87% of candidates working as many hours as they want are loyal, vs 68% who want more hours.

### What firms can do differently
Recruiters face a demand problem, needing to attract more talent at the top of the funnel; 54% of temporary/contract workers want to move to full-time work within two years. Firms should tackle the demand problem with digital and AI tools for faster top-of-funnel service, deploy AI to improve the candidate experience throughout, and offer full-service support (like upskilling and redeployment) that job boards can't.

### Methodology
Based on 2,766 respondents who worked with a recruitment firm at least once in the past three years, plus qualitative interviews with five recruitment industry professionals. Responses collected June–July 2025, heavily weighted toward North America.

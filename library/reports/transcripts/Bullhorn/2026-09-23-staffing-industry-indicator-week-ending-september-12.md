## Full report

Staffing hours declined across the board versus the prior week due to the short holiday week (the period included Labor Day), which pulls down the average-hours figure even as year-over-year growth stayed positive across every segment except office/clerical. Total US staffing hours ran 11% above 2025 levels for the week ending September 12, 2026. By segment, year-over-year: commercial hours up 14%, light industrial hours up 17% (again the strongest-growth segment), professional hours up 8% (holiday-adjusted), IT hours up 7%, and office/clerical hours down 5% (holiday-adjusted), continuing that segment's divergence from the rest of the market seen in prior weeks. Average weekly hours worked per worker came in at 32.4 hours, down from 35.1 hours the previous week, consistent with the holiday effect rather than a change in underlying demand.

## Full report

"US Staffing hours were up 10% compared to a year ago" for the week ending September 19, 2026, with the commercial and professional segments driving gains. Industrial staffing was particularly strong, up 15% year-over-year, attributed in the report to manufacturing, logistics and data center demand. The report credits professional staffing growth to resumed projects and AI-related initiatives, and notes office/clerical work declined 8% year-over-year despite modest weekly gains.

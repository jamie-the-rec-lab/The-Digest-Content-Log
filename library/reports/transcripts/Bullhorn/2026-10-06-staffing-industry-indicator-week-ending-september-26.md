# Staffing Industry Indicator, week ending September 26, 2026

- **Source:** Bullhorn
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **URL:** https://www.staffingindustry.com/research/research-reports/americas/sia-bullhorn-staffing-indicator-october-6-2026
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-17
- **Sample:** Weekly hours worked by temporary workers across a sample of staffing companies in the US that use Bullhorn technology, benchmarked to BLS data (index base: week of 13 January 2019)
- **Extraction:** partial (public headline table and summary from the SIA research page and SIA news item; the full analysis is members-only). The last four weeks of any reading are preliminary and subject to revision.

## Key figures
- US staffing hours: indexed 106, up 9% year over year and up 3.2% week over week, a year-to-date high, for the week ending 26 September 2026 (US)
- Commercial staffing: indexed 87, up 11% YoY, up 3.5% WoW. Professional staffing: indexed 124, up 8% YoY, up 2.7% WoW (US)
- IT staffing: indexed 119, up 6% YoY, up 3.6% WoW. Industrial staffing: indexed 98, up 15% YoY, up 3.5% WoW (US)
- Office/clerical: indexed 60, down 8% YoY, up 3.4% WoW; the only segment shown down year over year (US)
- Average weekly hours per worker 35.3, up from 34.6 the prior week and 35.2 a year earlier; industrial 36.6 hours, IT 37.4 hours (US)
- Seasonally adjusted Temporary Help Services employment in September was 35,400 higher than in December 2025, per BLS data cited by SIA (US)

## Full report
Published 6 October 2026 by Staffing Industry Analysts (SIA) with Bullhorn. Headline: US staffing hours reached another year-to-date high in the week ended 26 September 2026, up 9% on a year earlier and 3.2% on the prior week. Commercial and Professional hours both hit year-to-date highs. Industrial hours have risen since late April, which SIA attributes to demand from manufacturing, logistics and data centres. Office/clerical is the only segment shown down year over year. The index is benchmarked to the week of 13 January 2019. The last four weeks of readings are preliminary and subject to revision. The full analysis is restricted to SIA members.

# Staffing Industry Indicator, week ending October 3, 2026

- **Source:** Bullhorn
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **URL:** https://www.staffingindustry.com/research/research-reports/americas/sia-bullhorn-staffing-indicator-october-13-2026
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-17
- **Sample:** Weekly hours worked by temporary workers across a sample of staffing companies in the US that use Bullhorn technology, benchmarked to BLS data (index base: week of 13 January 2019)
- **Extraction:** partial (public headline table and summary from the SIA research page; the full analysis is members-only). The last four weeks of any reading are preliminary and subject to revision.

## Key figures
- US staffing hours: indexed 108, up 11% year over year and up 1.5% week over week, for the week ending 3 October 2026; US Staffing, Commercial and Professional hours all at year-to-date highs (US)
- Commercial staffing: indexed 88, up 13% YoY, up 1.5% WoW. Professional staffing: indexed 126, up 9% YoY, up 1.4% WoW (US)
- IT staffing: indexed 120, up 8% YoY, up 1.5% WoW. Industrial staffing: indexed 99, up 17% YoY, up 1.5% WoW (US)
- Office/clerical: indexed 61, down 7% YoY, up 1.8% WoW (US)
- Average weekly hours per worker 35.3, stable week over week and up from 35.0 a year earlier; industrial 36.5 hours, IT 37.3 hours (US)
- Temporary Help Services employment (seasonally adjusted, BLS data cited by SIA) fell by 10,000 in July and 10,900 in September and rose by 200 in August; it was 35,400 higher in September than in December 2025, growing by roughly 3,900 jobs per month January to September (US)

## Full report
Published 13 October 2026 by SIA with Bullhorn. Headline: "US Staffing Hours Edge Higher in the Second Half of September". SIA attributes industrial growth to demand from manufacturing, logistics and data centre projects, and professional demand to resumed projects and AI-related work. The last four weeks of readings are preliminary and subject to revision, with the most recent week most likely to change. The full analysis is restricted to SIA members.

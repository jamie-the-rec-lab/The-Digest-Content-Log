## Full report

### Overview
Deloitte's 2024 Global Human Capital Trends report, "Thriving Beyond Boundaries: Human Performance in a Boundaryless World," is based on a survey of 14,000 business and HR leaders across many industries and sectors in 95 countries. It identifies seven trends showing how a combination of business and human outcomes drives organizational success. Organizations making meaningful progress on these issues are nearly twice as likely to achieve desired business and human outcomes.

### Human performance and human sustainability
Rather than prioritizing business issues at the expense of human outcomes, organizations should take a "human sustainability" approach to improve outcomes for workers, customers, and society. While most leaders recognize prioritizing human performance is vital, current efforts fall short — the majority of workers report stagnant or worsening well-being, and 53% worry most about increasing work stress leading to worse mental health.

### Measuring performance beyond productivity
74% of respondents emphasized the critical importance of finding better ways to measure worker performance beyond conventional productivity metrics, yet only 17% of organizations report being very or extremely effective at evaluating the value created by individual workers beyond tracking activities or outputs.

### Human and technology integration
73% of respondents recognize the importance of aligning human capabilities with technological advancements, but only 9% report progress in achieving this balance. As work boundaries blur, human traits like empathy and curiosity become increasingly invaluable. Trust between workers and organizations, and human sustainability, are identified as the report's top issues.

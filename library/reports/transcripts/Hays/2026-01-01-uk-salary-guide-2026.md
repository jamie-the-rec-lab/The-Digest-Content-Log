# Hays UK Salary Guide 2026

- **Source:** Hays
- **Date:** 2026-01-01
- **URL:** https://www.hays.co.uk/salary-guide
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-17
- **Sample:** 5,100+ responses across 15 industries (UK employers and professionals)
- **Extraction:** partial — figures captured from the official Hays summary; the full guide sits behind an interactive tool/registration

## Key figures
- Salaries rose 2.2% on average across UK professions
- Highest increases: legal (4.6%), sustainability (4.1%), business support (3.8%), accountancy & finance (3.4%)
- 93% of employers reported difficulty recruiting the right skills over the past year
- 84% of employers raised salaries in the last year; 80% plan to again in the year ahead
- Employee pay dissatisfaction rose to 40% (up from 32% last year)
- 92% of professionals are more likely to apply when the salary is listed in the ad

## Full report
Hays' annual UK Salary Guide, based on 5,100+ responses across 15 industries, reports average salary growth of 2.2% for the year, with the biggest rises in legal (4.6%), sustainability (4.1%), business support (3.8%) and accountancy & finance (3.4%).

Skills shortages remain acute: 93% of employers struggled to recruit the right skills over the past year, and pay is the main lever, with 84% having raised salaries and 80% planning further rises. Yet employee satisfaction is slipping, with 40% now unhappy with their pay (up from 32%). Pay transparency is a clear pull factor, as 92% of professionals say a listed salary makes them more likely to apply.

# ISE Student Recruitment Report 2026

- **Source:** ISE
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **URL:** https://ise.org.uk/knowledge/research/606/ise_student_recruitment_report_2026
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-17
- **Sample:** 155 UK employers, more than 1.7 million applications and 31,577 hires and work experience placements, 2025/26 student recruitment cycle (per the ISE report page)
- **Extraction:** partial (the ISE report page gives only the sample; the full report is members-only or paid, about 216.67 GBP plus VAT. Findings below come from press coverage dated 14 and 15 October 2026, not from the report itself, and are attributed accordingly. The report page carries no publication date; 14 October is the date of the first coverage found, and the page was last modified 13 October.)

## Key figures
- Sample: 155 employers, more than 1.7 million applications, 31,577 hires and work experience placements, 2025/26 cycle (ISE report page)
- 92% of employers suspected or confirmed candidate misconduct, a record, up from 85% a year earlier and 78% two years earlier (ISE report as covered by Resultsense, 14 October 2026)
- Undeclared AI use in interviews was the most common form of misconduct, reported by 66% of respondents; 47% reported misuse in online assessments; 10% in CVs and covering letters (Resultsense)
- 82% of employers now offer guidance on acceptable AI use, up from 55% a year earlier (Resultsense)
- 64% accept AI somewhere in hiring, up from 46%, mainly for writing CVs and cover letters (Resultsense)
- Graduate hiring rose 4% in 2026 after an 8% fall the previous year; employers expect a 7% rise next year; 40% are cutting intake and 45% increasing it (Resultsense)
- 77% of employers are rethinking how they hire in response to record applications and AI misuse (HR Grapevine, 15 October 2026, from its summary line)

## Full report
The Institute of Student Employers (ISE) Student Recruitment Report 2026 is described on its own page as the largest survey of student employers in the UK, analysing data from 155 employers on the 2025/26 student recruitment cycle. The full report and its two data dashboards are members-only or paid. The statistics in the key figures above are as reported by Resultsense (14 October 2026) and HR Grapevine (15 October 2026), which quote the ISE survey; they have not been checked against the report. Resultsense quotes ISE chief executive Stephen Isherwood: "If you misrepresent yourself, you could end up doing the wrong job." It also quotes Chris Morrow of Digitalent: "It's like they are reading off an Autocue." The previous edition (Student Recruitment Survey 2025) is held in this library under ISE.

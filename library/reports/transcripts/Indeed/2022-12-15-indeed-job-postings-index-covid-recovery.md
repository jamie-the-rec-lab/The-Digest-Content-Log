## Full report

### Introducing the Indeed Job Postings Index
Indeed Hiring Lab re-released its Job Postings Tracker as the **Indeed Job Postings Index**, a daily measure of labor market activity updated and released weekly. It covers seven national markets: the US, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Germany, and Australia.

The primary difference from the legacy Job Postings Tracker is the level: the Index is set to 100 on February 1, 2020 (the pre-pandemic baseline), providing a uniform level shift of 100. Where the Tracker measured the percent change from Feb 1, 2020 (e.g., 40%), the corresponding Index would be 140. The launch added year-over-year and month-over-month percent changes (month-over-month calculated as 28-day differences to control for day of week).

As of December 9, 2022, US job postings were down 7.7% year-over-year. The accompanying chart shows the index fell dramatically in spring 2020 during the initial COVID shock, rebounded strongly in 2021, and then trended down for most of 2022.

### Why an index
As COVID-19 faded from the global labor market discussion, moving to an index better reflected current economic conditions and allowed more natural comparison of job postings across flexible date ranges (rather than always comparing to the pre-pandemic baseline). It places Indeed's postings metric alongside macroeconomic indexes such as the Case-Shiller Index (house prices) and the Consumer Price Index (inflation).

### Recovery trajectory (Indeed Hiring Lab coverage)
After plunging in spring 2020, job postings surged to roughly 60% above pre-pandemic levels by late 2021 and early 2022. The Indeed Job Postings Index peaked at the end of March 2022 and has since been on a downward path. US job postings declined about 9% from December 31, 2021 through October 21, 2022, with the starkest declines in sectors that led during the pandemic — including a notable drop in Software Development postings as tech companies reined in hiring plans.

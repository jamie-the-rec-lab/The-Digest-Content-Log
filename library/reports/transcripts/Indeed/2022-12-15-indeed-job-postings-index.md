## Full report
Indeed Hiring Lab re-released its Job Postings Tracker as the Indeed Job Postings Index, a daily measure of labour-market activity updated and released weekly. It covers seven national markets (US, Canada, UK, Ireland, France, Germany and Australia) and meets Hiring Lab's goal of producing high-quality, high-frequency labour-market metrics from Indeed's proprietary data.

The main change from the legacy Tracker is the level. The Index is set to 100 on 1 February 2020, applying a uniform level shift of 100 to the old Tracker across all time points: where the Tracker measured the percent change in postings from 1 February 2020 (e.g. 40%), the Index reads that as 140. Year-over-year and month-over-month percent changes are now included in the data portal at hiringlab.org/data and on the Hiring Lab GitHub page. Month-over-month changes are calculated as 28-day (4-week) differences to control for day of week.

As Covid-19 faded from the labour-market discussion, moving to an index better reflects current conditions and allows job postings to be compared more naturally across flexible date ranges rather than always against the pre-pandemic baseline. It places Indeed's job-postings metric in the broader class of macroeconomic indexes such as the Case-Shiller house-price index and the Consumer Price Index.

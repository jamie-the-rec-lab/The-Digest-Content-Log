## Full report

### Introduction
Jobvite's annual Recruiter Nation Report surveys recruiters and HR professionals to determine industry priorities and hiring trends. In 2021, recruiters faced a dramatic shift from job losses to rapid re-staffing amid an economic recovery, with rising worker confidence, higher stress, and higher turnover. The central theme: agility is the essential ingredient for recruiting success. 78% of recruiters' priorities shifted and 40% said budgets increased. 60% believe organizations will lose high-quality employees if they don't transition to remote-first work, and 40% said candidates turned down an interview or offer due to lack of diversity.

### Talent acquisition priorities and investments
Top priorities (next 12 months): improving quality-of-hire (48%), time-to-hire (28%), retention rate (26%), talent pipeline (25%), updating recruiting tech (21%), improving diversity (18%). Priorities shifted more for staffing agency recruiters (86%) than in-house (69%). 40% say budgets increased due to COVID-19 and 64% expect increases in the next 6-12 months. Top investment focus: social media (51%), LinkedIn (39%), employee referrals (37%), job boards (34%), virtual hiring (34%).

### Hiring challenges
Lack of skilled/qualified candidates (47%, down from 67% in 2017) and competition from other employers (40%, down from 60% in 2017) remain top concerns. New worries include onsite/office requirements (33%), too many steps in the process (25%), health/safety including COVID (23%). 56% say applications-per-requisition increased. 70% of recruiters fill jobs in under 30 days. 75% report their jobs now include finding and hiring gig workers/freelancers.

### Candidate evaluation
65% of recruiters have caught a candidate misrepresenting something on a resume (technical skills 45%, experience 44%, salary history 37%). Over 2017-2021, the importance of previous job experience (92% to 62%) and cultural fit (83% to 28%) sharply declined. 36% of recruiters take up to five minutes to decide on a candidate; 9% take less than one minute.

### State of recruiters
65% report increased stress since the pandemic (21% drastically); only 8% report decreased stress. 39% say their organizations are increasing staffing and hiring rapidly (up 13 points). 59% report increased turnover. 70% of recruiters plan to take additional professional courses/certifications.

### Diversity & inclusion
68% believe diversity in hiring will be a priority in the next year; 61% are placing more emphasis on diversity than in 2020; 44% say candidates turned down an interview/offer due to lack of diversity. Yet 20% of companies have no diversity goals. Common goals: race/ethnicity (50%), gender (48%), age (40%).

### Benefits, salary, referrals, and tech
Most effective perks: medical/dental (51%), 401(k) (49%), flexible/remote work (44%). 73% report increased salary negotiation (a 20+ point jump); 48% report average salary increases over 10%. 79% offer referral programs; 40% make more internal hires. On tech: 51% use chatbots (staffing agencies 66% vs in-house 35%), 54% plan to increase texting, and AI use rose across screening, chatbots, and job recommendations, with younger recruiters leveraging AI far more.

### Remote work
70% of organizations will run a hybrid policy; only 14% will be 100% in-office; the most popular pattern is three days in office, two remote (47%). 54% of recruiters have seen candidates decline over lack of flexibility, and 60% believe organizations will lose employees without a hybrid/remote-first culture.

### Methodology
Zogby Analytics surveyed 817 U.S. recruiters online for Jobvite; margin of error +/- 3.4 percentage points at 95% confidence.

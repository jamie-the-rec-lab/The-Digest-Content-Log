## Full report

### The urgency for change
TA is facing a reckoning: hiring volumes are up but so are drop-offs and ghosting, with only ~17% of applicants reaching interview in 2024, 60% abandoning slow or complex applications, and fewer than 4 in 10 receiving regular communication. Two-thirds say a positive experience influences acceptance and 25%+ have declined a job over a poor process. Recruiters are adopting AI fast (nearly 60% use it for sourcing, screening or nurturing) but candidate trust is low (only 37% trust AI to select qualified applicants) and 79% want transparency on how AI is used.

### The AI-driven TA process
AI now touches every stage of the nine-step TA process, from talent planning and candidate-profile building to sourcing, screening, interviewing, offer management and onboarding. Building future-ready candidate profiles uses tools like Galileo, Eightfold, Draup, Lightcast, TechWolf and SeekOut to benchmark requirements against the live labour market and surface hidden internal talent. Sourcing shifts from broad advertising to hyper-personalised outreach (SeekOut Assist, Adway, Grayscale). Screening is automated through conversational AI (Paradox, Maki People, Glider) and standardised scoring (HireVue, CodeSignal, Vervoe), reducing bias and giving every candidate a consistent experience. Scheduling and interview evaluation are streamlined by automated coordination and AI interview intelligence (BrightHire, Evidenced), and offer/acceptance is accelerated by digital twins that handle questions, negotiation within set parameters and offer dispatch.

### The new role of the recruiter
The recruiter is evolving from hands-on process manager to strategic "agent manager" and orchestrator, overseeing AI tools and digital twins while focusing on exceptions, complex problems and high-touch human moments. Digital twins (Avature, Beamery, Fontainebleau's "Morris", Great Wolf's "Emma", McDonald's "Olivia") answer questions, schedule, and manage documents at a consistent brand voice. The report lays out a full-stack recruiter capability set: strategic talent advisory, AI literacy and agent management, data and analytics, business acumen, empathy and human connection, systems thinking, collaboration, adaptability and DEI.

### How AI changes the TA tech stack
The shift is from fragmented legacy ATSs and siloed data to AI-first platforms built for automation, real-time analytics and interoperability. AI-first stacks aggregate internal and external data into unified profiles ("systemic business analytics"), as Panasonic did to cut attrition 20% in Nevada by rebalancing shift hiring. The framing moves TA from a technology cost centre to a talent investment that builds talent density.

### Getting started: five imperatives
Precision in TA (data-driven, self-improving decisions); recruiter as strategic agent orchestrator (new competencies and upskilling); future-ready TA tech (audit, open APIs, ongoing tech-stack reviews); AI pilots in high-impact areas (start with screening, scheduling or engagement, optionally with an RPO); and business-led TA metrics (tie time-to-fill, quality of hire, NPS and offer acceptance to revenue, innovation and workforce agility). The recruiter is repositioned, not replaced, and TA becomes a growth engine for the Superworker Era.

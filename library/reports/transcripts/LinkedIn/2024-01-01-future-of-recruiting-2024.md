## Full report

Six predictions:

**1. AI will supercharge recruiting.** 62% are optimistic about AI's impact; recruiters adding AI skills to profiles rose +14% in 2023. Just 27% are using or experimenting with Gen AI. Biggest benefit is writing job descriptions faster (57%), then automating tasks for more fulfilling work (45%). Even as AI automates tasks, human skills set recruiters apart — top future soft skills are communication (77%), relationship-building (72%), adaptability (63%).

**2. Recruiting will build the skills-based workforce.** 73% say skills-based hiring is a priority; a skills focus can grow talent pools 10x; degree-free job postings jumped 36% between 2019 and 2022. 85% say TA and L&D must work more closely (up +6 points). Recruiters with the most skills-first searches see +24% higher InMail acceptance.

**3. Quality of hire will top the agenda.** QoH is the No. 1 objective for 2024 and the top topic shaping recruiting over five years (54%). Measurement remains hard; factors include job performance, team fit, culture add, productivity, and retention, with optimism that Gen AI can help.

**4. Agility will be a must-have.** 91% are focused on being agile; recruiter roles have become more cross-functional (+30% skills overlap over five years), enabling "full-stack" recruiters.

**5. Recruiting teams will advocate for flex work.** In-office mandates hurt recruiting (50% report negative impact when fully in-office) while flexible-hybrid firms see positive impact (77%). Flexible-work employers are 16% more likely to have InMails accepted and 29% more likely to receive applications. Remote applications rose +146% YoY while remote postings fell -46% YoY.

**6. Attracting Gen Z will require a new playbook.** Gen Z will be 25%+ of the global workforce by 2025. Employer branding is the function expected to receive the greatest spend increase for the second year (57%). Gen Z is +36% more likely to prioritize advancement and +34% more likely to prioritize skill development, and will "ghost" slow, inefficient hiring processes.

### Methodology
LinkedIn Research surveyed 1,453 recruiting professionals (1,202 corporate, 261 search/staffing) plus 498 hiring managers, in six languages across 23 countries, Oct–Nov 2023. Candidate priorities based on Talent Drivers survey of 16,688 members (Nov 2023). Behavioral insights from billions of data points across 1 billion members; data as of Jan 1, 2024.

## Full report

(Partial: from LinkedIn's official summary of the 2025 Future of Recruiting report, built on billions of data points and a survey of 1,000+ talent professionals.)

### AI in recruiting
AI deployment is accelerating but challenges loom. AI is helping automate tasks while freeing recruiters to focus on strategic activities, and while adoption grows, challenges like data privacy and budget remain. 73% of TA pros agree AI will change the way organisations hire. As Glen Cathey (SVP, Talent Advisory and Digital Strategy at Randstad) frames it, AI is a tool to augment human judgment, not replace it, and successful organisations will use AI for routine tasks while preserving meaningful human interaction where it matters. The takeaway: TA professionals must develop both AI and human-centred skills to excel in evolving, advisor-like roles.

### Quality of hire
Organisations are getting serious about hiring the best candidates and measuring the results. Measuring quality of hire is hard, but AI can provide predictive insights, and 61% of TA pros believe AI can improve how they measure it. The advice: focus on long-term value over speed, leverage AI tools, and strengthen employer brand to attract and retain adaptable, high-performing talent.

### Skills-based hiring
As employers strive to fill skills gaps, skills-based hiring is gaining steam, prioritising skills over degrees or job history to unlock untapped talent, improve role alignment and drive quality hires. 93% of TA pros believe accurately assessing a candidate's skills is crucial for improving quality of hire.

### Takeaway
Adapting to AI can seem daunting, but by mastering AI tools recruiters can evolve into strategic talent advisors who navigate change and shape the future workforce.

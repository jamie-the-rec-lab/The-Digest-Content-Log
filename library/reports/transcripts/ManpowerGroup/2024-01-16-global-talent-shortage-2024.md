## Full report

ManpowerGroup's annual Talent Shortage Survey found that 74% of employers report difficulty filling roles in 2024 (with the global average across the country breakdown at 75%), continuing a decade-long upward trend in reported talent scarcity (the survey was not run in 2017 or 2020).

By industry vertical, the shortage is most acute in Health Care & Life Sciences (77%), followed by Consumer Goods & Services, Information Technology, and Transport, Logistics & Automotive (all 76%), then Industrials & Materials (75%), Communication Services (73%), Financials & Real Estate (72%) and Energy & Utilities (71%).

Talent scarcity varies widely by country. Japan is highest at 85%, followed by Germany, Greece and Israel (82%), Ireland, Portugal and India (81%), and the UK, France, Canada and Brazil (80%). At the other end, Finland is lowest at 59%, with Panama and Peru (65%), Colombia and the Czech Republic (66%) and Poland (66%) also below the global average. The US sits at 70%, China at 69% and Mexico at 68%.

The survey covered 40,077 employers across 41 countries. ManpowerGroup positions its family of brands (Manpower, Experis, Talent Solutions) as offering solutions across the HR life cycle, from workforce consulting and analytics to talent resourcing and career management.

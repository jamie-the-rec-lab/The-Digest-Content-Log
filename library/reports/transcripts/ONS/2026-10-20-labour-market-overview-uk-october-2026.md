- **Source:** ONS (Office for National Statistics)

- **URL:** https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/bulletins/uklabourmarket/october2026

- **Sample:** Labour Force Survey (achieved sample "increased from 80,078 individuals in January to March 2026 to 81,193 individuals in April to June 2026"), HMRC PAYE Real Time Information, Vacancy Survey. UK-wide official statistics.

## Key figures
- "The UK employment rate ... was estimated at 75.3% for June to August 2026." Up 0.2 percentage points on the year and the quarter (UK, ages 16-64)
- "The UK unemployment rate ... was estimated at 4.7% in June to August 2026." Down 0.1 percentage points on the year and the quarter (UK, ages 16+)
- Inactivity rate 20.9% for June to August 2026, down 0.2pp on the year (UK, ages 16-64)
- Vacancies: early estimate for July to September 2026 is 691,000, down 21,000 (2.9%) on April to June 2026, the largest quarterly fall since January to March 2026; down 37,000 (5.1%) on the year (UK)
- Lowest vacancies outside the COVID-19 period since July to September 2014 (686,000) (UK)
- Payrolled employees: August 2025 to August 2026 down 111,000 (0.4%); September 2026 flash estimate 30.3 million, down 66,000 (0.2%) on the year and up 13,000 on the month (UK, HMRC RTI, provisional)
- Claimant Count for September 2026: an estimated 1.701 million, up on the month and year (UK, provisional)
- Regular pay growth (excluding bonuses) 3.7% and total pay growth 3.7% (down from 3.9%) for June to August 2026; private sector regular pay 3.0%, public sector 6.3% (UK)
- Real regular pay growth 0.6% (CPIH) and 0.8% (CPI) (UK)

## Full report
Published 20 October 2026. Vacancy Survey feedback "suggests smaller firms may be holding off recruitment because of higher labour costs" (paraphrase of the ONS note). LFS notes: some volatility remains for granular breakdowns with smaller samples; a May 2026 operational issue in LFS telephone collection had minimal effect on headline estimates.

# REC JobsOutlook, June to August 2026

- **Source:** REC (Recruitment and Employment Confederation)
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **URL:** https://www.onrec.com/node/129117
- **Fetched:** 2026-11-04
- **Sample:** Survey conducted by Whitestone Insight among 704 UK employers between 15 June and 18 August 2026, weighted by region, industry and public/private sector
- **Extraction:** partial - REC press release as republished by Onrec; the full REC JobsOutlook tables on rec.uk.com were not read.

## Key figures
- "Economic confidence rose by eight percentage points to a net balance of -39% in the June-August 2026 period." (UK employers)
- "Confidence in making hiring and investment decisions improved by six points to -8%." (UK employers)
- "Short-term permanent hiring intentions edged down one point to a net balance of +8%." Medium-term permanent intentions fell four points to +6% (UK)
- "The short-term net balance fell one point to +2%, while the medium-term balance remained at +5%." (UK temporary hiring intentions)
- "Regionally, the North and Midlands recorded short-term permanent hiring balances of +11% and +10% respectively"; the Midlands led medium-term permanent intentions at +12% (UK regions)

## Full report
Published 29 September 2026. Net balances are positive responses minus negative responses, not the share of employers who are confident. REC interim chief executive Maxine Bligh: "Businesses are feeling better than they were three months ago, but let's not mistake improvement for recovery." The REC called for restoring the employers' National Insurance threshold, better access to childcare, health and transport, and more flexible skills funding (paraphrase from the release).

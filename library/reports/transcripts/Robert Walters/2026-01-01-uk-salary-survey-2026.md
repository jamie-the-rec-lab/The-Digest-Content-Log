## UK Trends 2025–2026

*Commentary by Chris Eldridge, CEO - UKI & North America, Robert Walters:*

"The UK labour market is in a transitional period. Rising employment costs, prolonged economic volatility and changing policies have strained budgets, with many employers delaying pay rises, implementing hiring freezes, and focusing on cost efficiencies. However, progress is on the horizon. Business confidence remains subdued, but we are noting pockets of increased hiring activity in accounting, legal services, and specialist fields of technology like cybersecurity and AI. Competition for top talent remains intense as digital advancements continue to transform skills requirements. This places a premium on candidates who are adaptable 'all-rounders' able to quickly adjust their expertise to meet shifting market needs.

The flexible working debate continues to trigger market movement as employees stand firm in valuing flexibility while employers push for more office-based work. Striking the right balance will be key to attracting and retaining talent. Particularly in a period where pay raises and bonuses remain modest. Offering flexibility is an area where employers can support their staff, particularly when financial perks are more constrained.

Wage inflation varies significantly, primarily influenced by the availability of talent. Industries facing skills shortages tend to offer larger salaries, while sectors with an abundance of talent are experiencing more stagnant pay growth. Professionals are more cautious now, highlighted by trends like 'job-hugging' and the re-emergence of the 'great stay'. Regardless of tighter budgets, employers must refine their Employee Value Proposition (EVP), offering competitive salaries, benefits, and a strong employer brand to attract them.

Government reforms like the Employment Rights Bill are expected to take effect in late 2025-26, requiring businesses to align hiring plans with broader UK policy strategies. Over the next 12 months, the most successful teams will combine specialist expertise and digital literacy with adaptable soft skills and innovation to fuel business growth."

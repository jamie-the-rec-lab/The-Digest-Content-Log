## Full report

SHRM's 2026 Recruiting Executives: Priorities and Perspectives report examines the top priorities and challenges facing recruiting leaders. In 2026, their top priorities are: developing targeted approaches for recruiting critical talent, advancing effective sourcing strategies, and refining their overall recruiting strategies.

**Function-level challenges.** Recruiting budget constraints or limited resources emerged as a top concern — 24% of recruiting executives report it as a challenge and 17% identify it as their single greatest challenge. Closely following are difficulties collaborating with hiring managers and other stakeholders (22% a challenge, 11% greatest hurdle). Inefficiency in recruiting processes is also a major obstacle (20% a challenge, 14% top functional issue).

**Personal challenges.** Workload management stands out as the primary personal hurdle — 46% of leaders report it as a challenge (vs. 47% in 2025), and 27% identify it as their single greatest personal obstacle (vs. 32% in 2025). Closely related is the struggle for work/life balance, cited by 31% of recruiting executives, with 13% viewing it as their top concern.

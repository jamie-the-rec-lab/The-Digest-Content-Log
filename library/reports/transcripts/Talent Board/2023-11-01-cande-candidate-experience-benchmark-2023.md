# 2023 Global Candidate Experience (CandE) Benchmark Research Report

- **Source:** Talent Board (now part of ERE Media)
- **Date:** 2023-11
- **URL:** https://www.eremedia.com/candidate-experience
- **Fetched:** 2026-07-16
- **Sample:** 2023 CandE Awards Benchmark Program (open 31 Jan to 31 Aug 2023): over 150 organisations registered and nearly 240,000 candidates completed the candidate surveys, across North America, EMEA, APAC and Latin America. Founded by Talent Board in 2011. Two survey rounds: an employer survey and a random-sample candidate survey, scored to a standard ~80/20 not-selected/hired ratio.
- **Extraction:** full (complete report text; charts rendered as figures in the source)

## Key figures
- Candidate contentment (great-experience rate) fell sharply and candidate resentment (very-poor experience) rose globally in 2023, with North America resentment at 13%.
- 36% of North American candidates had not heard back from employers 1-2 or more months after applying, unchanged from 2022.
- Only 5% of candidates said they had been informed they did not get the job, while 62% remembered only that they had applied.
- 60% of candidates received no feedback after being rejected during screening and interviewing.
- When specific feedback was given, willingness to refer others rose 46% and willingness to keep a relationship with the employer rose 47%.
- 67% of employers ask for feedback after a candidate is hired (up 68% from 2022), but only 24% ask at the research stage and 27% after applying.
- 72% of North American CandE Award Winners align recruiter performance with candidate experience.
- Only 58% of employers ran a structured interview process (63% for CandE Winners).
- Referrals make up roughly 20% to 40% of hires, and referred candidates are 32% more likely to deepen their relationship with the employer.
- Company values were the number one marketing content candidates sought (45%), and 98% of employers consider their career site critical to recruiting.
- Only 37% of candidates applied via mobile (rising to 53% for hourly roles).
- Launching an application from a text lifted application NPS 42%, and the ability to ask a chatbot questions lifted it 50%.
- 61% of candidates received an offer within one week of the final interview, and 93% accepted the offers presented.
- Only 17% of employers had a recruiter follow up with a new hire within six weeks of the start date, down 48% from 2022.
- 18% of employers used conversational AI across the recruiting process (first year measured), while basic chatbot use was 39%.
- A cited Virgin Media case study found it was losing more than $6 million a year in sales from poor candidate experience, later turned into a $7 million revenue stream.

## Full report

### Overview and business impact
Over 12+ years of CandE research, communication and feedback loops remain the clearest differentiators of a positive candidate experience. In 2023 the "candidate contentment" rate fell and "candidate resentment" rose across every region, even in historically positive APAC and Latin America, with North America resentment at 13%. Resentment matters because candidates who feel poorly treated are less likely to apply again, refer others, keep brand affinity or purchase, and referrals account for 20-40% of hires. The report stresses that most applicants (about 90%) are never hired, so it is the experience of the not-selected that carries the business and brand risk.

### Feedback and accountability
Employers are most comfortable asking for feedback after hire (67%) and least before applying, though numbers rose across all stages in 2023. 72% of North American CandE Winners align recruiter performance with candidate experience. Yet 60% of candidates received no feedback after rejection at screening or interview, a major missed opportunity given that specific feedback lifts willingness to refer by 46% and to keep a relationship by 47%.

### Attract, apply and screen
Career sites remain the top research channel (39%), followed by online groups and employer review sites (both 29%). Company values are the top marketing content (45%). Applications got faster (nearly 40% took under 10 minutes) but 36% of candidates still waited 1-2+ months to hear back. Technology helps: text-to-apply lifted application NPS 42% and chatbot Q&A lifted it 50%, yet only 37% applied via mobile. Over 90% of candidates faced general screening questions; behavioural assessments, simulations, tests and games scored highest on perceived fairness.

### Interview and hire
Only 58% of employers ran structured interviews (63% of Winners), even though structured processes reduce bias and raise fairness. Live video (42%) edged out phone screens and in-person interviews. The number one reason candidates withdraw is that "my time was disrespected," followed by the process taking too long and salary not meeting expectations. At the hire stage, 61% received an offer within a week of the final interview and 93% accepted, but preboarding and onboarding remain weak spots, with recruiter six-week follow-up down 48% to just 17%.

### AI and technology
2023 was the first year conversational AI was measured separately, with 18% of employers using it across the recruiting process and basic chatbot use at 39%. Top-rated CandE Winners tend to leverage more AI recruiting technologies to improve communication and feedback loops. The report includes regional briefs (North America, EMEA, APAC, Latin America), a demographic analysis (women, people of colour and younger candidates rate their experience more positively), and 19 CandE Award Winner case studies (including Ford, Southwest Airlines, Schneider Electric, Delaware North and GuideWell).

# iCIMS October 2026 Workforce Report: Holiday Hiring Cooldown

- **Source:** iCIMS
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **URL:** https://www.icims.com/company/newsroom/octoberinsights2026/
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-17
- **Sample:** Data from more than 3.1 million users and 691 million candidate profiles (iCIMS platform data)
- **Extraction:** partial (iCIMS press release only, dated 8 October 2026; the full workforce report PDF was not retrieved)

## Key figures
- Frontline applications 18% below baseline in September 2026, with applicants per opening at 29, the lowest of the year (US)
- Retail time to fill 37 days, steady in the mid-30-day range all year (US)
- Ages 18 to 24 were 40% of retail applicants in September, up from 37% a year earlier (US)
- US hiring 7% below the September 2025 baseline while job openings were 3% above baseline (US)
- Frontline hiring fell to 8% below baseline in September after a strong summer (US)
- The iCIMS newsroom summary also states EMEA applications rose about 6% and that 40% of the holiday hiring pool is Gen Z

## Full report
iCIMS press release (Holmdel, N.J., 8 October 2026): "ICIMS Insights: Holiday Hiring Cooldown Pressures Retailers to Move Qualified Candidates Faster with AI and Candidate Re-Engagement". It presents the October 2026 Workforce Report. Trent Cotton, head of talent insights at iCIMS, said: "Gen Z is now leading the pool, and recruiting strategies built for a different generation will not hold up this season." The release describes how Urban Outfitters Inc. uses iCIMS to re-engage former employees and past applicants during seasonal hiring. Danny Rojas, senior principal of product and market insights at iCIMS, advised employers to "Start with the talent you already know." These are the vendor's own platform figures.

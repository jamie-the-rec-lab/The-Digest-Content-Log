growth and startup podcasts (currently Lenny's Podcast) that are NOT talent-first,
so they are kept out of the default "recruiting brain" recall and only surfaced
when explicitly asked, or fed into the weekly digest. Tags drawn ONLY from this
list; company/guest goes in the Company field, not the tags. 1-6 theme tags per
episode (this collection is auto-tagged from titles, so a light touch is fine).

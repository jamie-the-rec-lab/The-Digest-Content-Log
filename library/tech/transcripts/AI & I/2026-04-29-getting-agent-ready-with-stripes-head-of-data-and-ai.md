## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Emily Glassberg Sands, Head of Data and AI at Stripe  ·  **Show:** AI & I, 29 Apr 2026
**Tags:** ai, ai agents, machine learning, data and analytics, product management, scaling

**When to use this:** You're building an AI product with high compute costs and need to prevent fraud that hits your CAC directly.

**Big idea:** AI companies are attracting a new class of fraudsters who steal compute instead of money, forcing payment infrastructure and billing models to evolve from transaction-level to customer-lifecycle detection and from seat-based to outcome-based pricing.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Full funnel fraud detection: Stripe moved Radar from checkout-only to sign-up, payment, and overage moments because fraudsters target every stage where credits are given. Four key moments to protect: at sign-up before giving credits, at payment, at overage, and at refunds.
- Multi-account abuse runs at 7% of signups across AI companies on Stripe. Free trial abuse costs companies $25 per trial with 96% of non-converting trials being actual abuse, not genuine non-buyers, growing 4x over six months.
- Blocking all virtual cards loses 15% of legitimate transactions. The solution is granular scoring at each lifecycle stage rather than binary blocks, using Stripe Radar API even for transactions processed elsewhere.
- Outcome-based billing for vertical AI solutions (Intercom charges per case resolved, Fin per ticket resolved) aligns vendor incentives with customer ROI. Model providers meter tokens; wrappers should meter outcomes because end users hold them accountable for results, not effort.
- Token billing tracks underlying LLM costs in real time and adjusts pricing dynamically so margins don't evaporate when model costs change mid-month.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- AI companies reach 30 million in ARR in 18 months, three times faster than the top 100 SaaS companies from 2018.
- LLM traffic to Stripe documentation is up 10 times year-over-year.
- One large AI company was seeing only 4% of free trials convert to paid, each free trial costing 25 dollars in LLM compute.
- Stripe blocks 250,000 fraudulent free trials per week for one large customer.

**Tools & prompts:**
- Stripe Radar API now scores transactions across all payment methods, including ones not processed on Stripe (Worldpay, Adyen). Call the API at sign-up, payment, and overage.
- Stripe Billing supports token billing and outcome-based meters for usage tracking.

**Contrarian take:**
- Seat-based billing for developer tools will disappear within six months because it makes no sense if an agent makes developers 10x more productive: you would need 1/10 the developers, so why peg revenue to headcount?

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Check your Radar dashboard or ask the Radar assistant if you have hidden fraud in free trials or credits. Start integrating Radar at sign-up, not just checkout.
- If you offer free trials or free compute, model the actual cost per trial and the true conversion rate. Stripe data shows 96% of non-converters were abuse, not genuine prospects.
- Audit your billing model: if you're metering seat-based, test outcome-based alternatives using Stripe Billing to see if you can align revenue with customer value delivered.

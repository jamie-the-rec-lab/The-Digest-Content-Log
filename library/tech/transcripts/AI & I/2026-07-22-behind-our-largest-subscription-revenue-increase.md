**Big idea:** Bundle complementary AI tools your users already adopt, offer credits and discounts, and reposition it as a "builder pack" targeting a creator identity, not just a discount offer.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Partner with tools creators already use and love: $1,000 Codex credits, 12 months free Cursor, etc. This signals "we know what builders need" more than generic SaaS.
- Shift identity: instead of "discounts," call it a tier (Builder Pack, All Access) and tie it to who the user is. Framing matters to conversion.
- Bundle design: select tools that are non-overlapping and solve different layers of the builder workflow.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Single launch of builder pack generated the largest subscription revenue increase in Every's history.

## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Kevin Pereira and Gavin Purcell, hosts  ·  **Show:** AI For Humans, 17 Jul 2026
**Tags:** ai, ai coding, ai agents

**When to use this:** You want a quick read on where a new Chinese open model sits relative to the US frontier, or a working example of connecting an LLM to a 3D tool like Blender or Unreal Engine for autonomous content generation.

**Big idea:** Moonshot Labs' new Kimi K3 model, available on OpenRouter, is reported to land roughly at GPT-5.6 Sol's level and clearly ahead of Claude Opus 4.8, though still behind Claude Fable 5, and the hosts argue that Chinese frontier progress like this forces US labs to lower prices and loosen usage limits for everyone, evidenced by Claude Fable 5's usage limits resetting the same week.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- To build an autonomous 3D scene, Gavin connected GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra to Blender via MCP (Model Context Protocol) and gave it a single-paragraph prompt describing a scene (a raccoon entering a kitchen with other animals); the process ran for about 12 hours and consumed a large number of tokens but produced a working animated scene fully autonomously.
- A separate creator, Nomads Vagabonds, used a more structured, step-by-step approach connecting GPT-5.6 Sol to Unreal Engine to build a game, contrasted against Gavin's single-prompt, fully autonomous approach, illustrating a trade-off between control and hands-off generation.
- Kevin built a free browser-playable camouflage game inspired by Chroma Chameleon, where a player paints themselves to blend into the environment, without downloading anything, as a personal proof of concept for what a solo builder can ship using current tools.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Kimi K3 is reported to perform well on benchmark-style tasks such as building a Minecraft city and a Flappy Bird clone, outperforming Claude Opus 4.8 in a side-by-side Flappy Bird test cited from a creator called June_song.
- Suno, the AI music generator, was hacked, and the leak reportedly showed a significant portion of its training data came from YouTube music, relevant context given Suno already faces lawsuits from record labels over training data.

**Contrarian take:**
- Kevin flags that as Chinese models like Kimi K3 approach frontier quality, enterprise customers have legitimate reason to be cautious about data handling, pushing back on the more celebratory "competition is great" framing Gavin applies to the same news.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- If you want to test autonomous 3D or game content generation, connecting an agent like GPT-5.6 Sol to Blender or Unreal Engine via MCP with a single detailed prompt is a working starting point, but budget for many hours of runtime and heavy token usage.

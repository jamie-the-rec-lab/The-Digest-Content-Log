**Host:** Gavin and Kevin (surnames not stated in the file), co-hosts of AI For Humans, who test the personal AI agents Instinct and Muse  ·  **Show:** AI For Humans, 18 Sep 2026
**Tags:** ai agents, ai, productivity, future of work, data and analytics, leadership

**When to use this:** you are tempted to give a consumer AI agent access to your email and accounts, and want honest first-hand results and privacy warnings.

**Big idea:** the hosts say cloud personal agents that live in messages (Instinct, Muse) now work without setup and can do real chores, but they carry real privacy costs, because one of them exposed the host's phone number and email without asking.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Give the agent a standing search brief. For apartment hunting, Gavin told Instinct to "go find weird listings" and skip the usual sites, and it checks agencies daily, reverse-searches listing images and uses Google Street View to look for shade.
- Use it for flights too: Instinct pulled seat maps to find two-seat rows in basic economy, which suits flying with a dog.
- Use it to find forgotten charges. Instinct found three subscriptions on his credit card including DocuSign, got one cancelled through the provider's support and secured a refund. He says he has had about $500 back in total.
- Isolate it: one host suggests a fresh Gmail and Slack account for the agent and forwarding mail to it, rather than connecting everything.
- Meta's Muse runs in a virtualised encrypted platform with a dispatch agent that confirms before anything goes out, but Kevin did not trust it enough to try it.
- Jev, a fast classification model, replaced Haiku for tagging links on the AI For Humans link blog, and was "faster, better, and cheaper".

**Data & benchmarks:**
- The hosts say Instinct took 50 million dollars in April and was raising a billion dollars at a 10 billion dollar valuation, as reported on the show.
- A Jev classifier example: 50,000 customer emails processed with sentiment and confidence, including sarcasm detection. One viewer example was said to cost about 4 cents.

**Contrarian take:**
- Kevin says he does not connect Instinct to any system because "you are the product", and notes you cannot delete your account and data, and the training toggle is on by default. Gavin's own agent emailed letting agencies his full name, phone and email, which he found "infuriating".

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Before trialling any agent, create a dedicated email and phone number for it and never connect your ATS or work inbox.
- Ask your assistant to find forgotten software subscriptions in a statement you paste in, then cancel them yourself.

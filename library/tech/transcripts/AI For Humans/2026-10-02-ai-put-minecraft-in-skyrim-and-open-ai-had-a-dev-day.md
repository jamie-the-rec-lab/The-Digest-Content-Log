## Actionable summary
**Host:** Gavin and Kevin (surnames not stated in the file), co-hosts of AI For Humans, a weekly general-audience AI show  ·  **Show:** AI For Humans, 2 Oct 2026
**Tags:** ai, ai agents, ai coding, ai product, future of work, productivity

**When to use this:** you want to understand how far autonomous agents can now go on a creative project, and the honest cost and limits, or you need a sceptical read on OpenAI's DevDay launches.

**Big idea:** the hosts say Opus 5.5 combined with coding agents and MCP tools can run a whole production pipeline without a human editing, while OpenAI's "dots" agent launch looked less differentiated than the hype.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Gavin gave his Claude Code agent (named Fig) one prompt: make a five-minute documentary on superintelligence with a cheeky British host. Using Runway's MCP, the agent wrote the script, created a character sheet, broke the film into shots, generated B-roll, picked the best takes, fixed mispronunciations and edited it, with Gavin never touching a timeline.
- The result was a 27 minute video that he later extended to about half an hour, and the host says a related clip reached about 800,000 views. He did about 6 hours of notes work as the executive producer.
- He estimates the same job at API prices would cost about $500 to $700, and says it only worked this well because Fig already had tools built over time.
- Another host recommends the "gauntlet loop": agents blindly compare the output against source material and send feedback back to the builder agent, which uses many tokens.
- They note a model can be tested with the same one-shot prompt (a pelican on a bicycle jousting another pelican) to compare outputs.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- The $200 Pro plan gets less usage than before, and OpenAI announced a $500 monthly plan.
- They say model releases now arrive about every 11 days, quoting a chart they saw online.

**Contrarian take:**
- One host says of dots, "I'm not convinced it's specifically interesting", and thinks it is OpenAI's version of Instinct or Muse and still poorly organised across the apps. The hosts also say decompiled games are not legal because they reuse the original assets.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Kevin's agent recipe for building Earlablo (a Diablo-style game with Toe Jam & Earl) in about 30 to 38 hours of spare time: describe a concrete playable slice rather than "mash these up"; use top models to dispatch cheaper agents in parallel to research existing open-source foundations and asset libraries; give each agent a clear job; assign a strong model to own the quality bar and coordinate cheaper developers; then ask it to make it run in a browser.
- Check-in system: have the agent write an HTML, Markdown or PDF status update at each major milestone, use the harness's interactive question tool to answer ambiguities from a phone, and have it produce an audition sheet (for example music options) to choose from.

AI news this week: AI is cracking open all software, from Minecraft inside Red Dead Redemption 2 to a Photoshop decompile, plus Kevin made EARLABLO.

On today's AI For Humans, Kevin Pereira and Gavin Purcell zoom out on the AI modding explosion, because the bigger story isn't games. AI is getting so good at taking software apart and rebuilding it that pretty soon almost any program might be de facto open source. 

This week alone brought God of War in GTA, Minecraft inside Twitter, Black Ops Zombies running in a web browser, a GTA skate mod and a full agent harness living inside Minecraft. We talk about what happens when no app can keep its secrets, and Kevin shows off Earlablo, his AI rebuild of a 1991 Sega classic as a Diablo-style dungeon crawler. It def slaps.

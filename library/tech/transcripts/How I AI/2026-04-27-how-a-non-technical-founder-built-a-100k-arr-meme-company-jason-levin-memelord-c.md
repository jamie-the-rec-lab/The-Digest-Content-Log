## Actionable summary
**Guest:** Jason Levin, CEO and Founder, Memelord  ·  **Show:** How I AI, 27 Apr 2026
**Tags:** ai, ai agents, product design, startups, growth, productivity

**When to use this:** You want to launch a product or feature without hiring engineers, or you're trying to figure out how to involve your team in building without gatekeeping.

**Big idea:** Non-technical founders can build to $100K ARR by layering no-code tools, free tools as lead magnets, and then evolving to agent-first APIs once product-market fit is proven, and marketing teams unlock their best work when freed to vibe code.

**Frameworks & plays:**
- Start with what you can build yourself: Memelord began as a $6.90-per-month newsletter sending subscribers to a Google Slides deck. Then became a Bubble app with 395 workflows generating $100K ARR. Only hire engineers and switch to real code after proving the model works and gathering capital.
- Free tools beat PDF downloads. Jason's team built oddball free meme generators, filters, and joke makers at memelord.com/tools. These generated hundreds of thousands of emails globally and went viral in Turkey without paid marketing. Free tools solve the first problem that leads customers to your core product.
- Mandatory vibe coding rule: Jason requires all marketers to build features using AI. This unlocked Steve Jobs portrait generators for pitch decks, custom Snapchat caption tools, and weird meme makers. Marketers should not hand off ideas to engineers; they should build directly.
- Hardware can be hyper-personalized: Jason built a bedside keyboard without a screen that sends Linear tickets via Zapier when he presses enter, letting him capture ideas at night without waking his wife or bringing his phone to bed. The Raspberry Pi cost $10 and was built using ChatGPT.
- Agents do not overthink funny or polish. They go straight to the tokens and generate. Build for agent consumption by offering a clean API alongside beautiful human marketing and onboarding.

**Data & benchmarks:**
- Memelord reached $100K ARR running entirely on Bubble, no engineers hired.
- Free tools generated hundreds of thousands of email signups, with one filter going viral in Turkey.
- Google Cloud credits of $300,000 were deployed in AI experiments.

**Tools & prompts:**
- Bubble for no-code workflows (scales to high complexity but eventually hits limits).
- Cursor for both technical and non-technical team members to edit code, using the "ask" feature to explain existing systems before editing.
- OpenClaw for agentic meme generation via API.
- Linear as a task management system agents can write to directly; Jason's bedside keyboard creates Linear tickets without human involvement.
- Zapier to connect keyboard input to Linear and email.

**Contrarian take:**
- Rabbit hardware device was two years early, not stupid. Most ideas that seem obviously bad prove correct later when execution or timing shifts.

**Steal this (do tomorrow):**
- Audit whether your marketers are constrained by handoffs to engineering. Trial letting them vibe code one small free tool or feature using Cursor or Claude and measure the time and quality.
- Replace one PDF download on your site with a free tool that solves the first problem a prospect has. Measure email captures and viral potential.
- If you have repetitive late-night ideas, build a hardware clip using a Raspberry Pi and Zapier to capture them into Linear or email without reaching for your phone.
